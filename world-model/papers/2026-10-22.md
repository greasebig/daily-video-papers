## 1. Agentic World Action Models / Agentic World行动模型

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.30136v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30136v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30136v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent world action models provide visual foresight and future prediction for robot manipulation, but lack explicit mechanisms for compositional skill reuse. Agentic robotics combines code-based planning and reasoning with reusable skills, but often lacks predictive grounding in physical dynamics. We introduce AW-0, an agentic world action model framework that integrates code-based planning with joint video and action prediction. We jointly model code, future video, and robot actions as three coupled generative streams within a unified mixture-of-transformers architecture. Within this architecture a code expert generates parameterized skill routines and control logic while video and action experts generate future visual states and low-level motor commands. Code serves two complementary roles: it structures multi-step prediction and control within the world model and represents skills as reusable programs for subsequent agentic execution. We further introduce an automated curation pipeline that labels robot and human video trajectories with aligned programs and consolidates recurring routines into reusable skill libraries. Evaluations on RoboTwin 2.0 and physical bimanual manipulation tasks show improved task performance over VLA and WAM baselines, alongside gains in language following, semantic reasoning and robustness, demonstrating that agentic code enables connection between planning, prediction and execution within WAMs.

最近的世界行动模型为机器人操纵提供了视觉预见和未来预测，但缺乏组合技能重用的明确机制。 Agentic机器人将基于代码的规划和推理与可重复使用的技能相结合，但通常缺乏物理动力学的预测基础。 我们引入了AW-0 ，这是一个代理世界行动模型框架，将基于代码的规划与联合视频和行动预测相结合。 我们在统一的变形金刚混合架构中将代码、未来视频和机器人动作共同建模为三个耦合的生成流。 在此架构中，代码专家生成参数化技能例程和控制逻辑，而视频和动作专家生成未来的视觉状态和低级电机命令。 代码有两个互补的角色：它在世界模型中构建多步预测和控制，并将技能表示为后续代理执行的可重用程序。 我们进一步引入了一个自动化的策展流程，用对齐的程序标记机器人和人类视频轨迹，并将重复的例程整合到可重复使用的技能库中。 对RoboTwin 2.0和物理双手动操作任务的评估显示，任务性能优于VLA和WAM基线，同时在语言跟踪、语义推理和鲁棒性方面有所提高，这表明代理代码可以在WAM内实现规划、预测和执行之间的连接。

## 2. PhysiCalWorld: In-Context Physics Calibration for World Models / PhysiCalWorld ：世界模型的上下文物理校准

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.30125v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30125v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30125v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Existing world models typically perceive a scene through current visual frame or a narrow history window. However, these observations may not reveal the physics properties that determine transition dynamics, such as an object's mass distribution, joint damping, or surface friction. Without calibrating these physics factors for world models, predictions tend to regress toward an empirical prior and induce severe drift in novel test scenes. To this end, we propose PhysiCalWorld, which calibrates action-conditioned world models to the instance-specific dynamics via in-context prompting, without fine-tuning or analytical physics modeling. Our method encodes an exploratory interaction clip collected within the test scene into a compact physics descriptor token, which grounds subsequent predictions in the scene's actual physical behavior. PhysiCalWorld is trained end-to-end with the latent prediction loss alone, without physics labels. At inference time, the descriptor token is extracted once and can be reused across rollouts with negligible compute overhead. We evaluate PhysiCalWorld across four environments covering diverse physics variations and show that it substantially improves rollout prediction accuracy and visual planning success rate. The learned descriptors also form a physically structured latent space and generalize beyond the physics ranges seen during training.

现有的世界模型通常通过当前的视觉框架或狭窄的历史窗口感知场景。 然而，这些观测可能无法揭示决定过渡动力学的物理特性，例如物体的质量分布、关节阻尼或表面摩擦。 如果没有为世界模型校准这些物理因素，预测往往会回归到经验先验，并在新颖的测试场景中引起严重的漂移。 为此，我们提出了PhysiCalWorld ，它通过上下文提示将动作条件世界模型校准为实例特定的动态，而无需微调或分析物理建模。 我们的方法将测试场景中收集的探索性交互剪辑编码为紧凑的物理描述符令牌，从而为场景的实际物理行为中的后续预测奠定基础。 PhysiCalWorld仅使用潜在预测损失进行端到端训练，没有物理标签。 在推理时，描述符令牌被提取一次，并且可以在计算开销可忽略不计的情况下跨部署重复使用。 我们评估了涵盖不同物理变化的四个环境的PhysiCalWorld ，并表明它大大提高了推出预测准确性和视觉规划成功率。 学习的描述符还形成了一个物理结构的潜伏空间，并超出了训练期间看到的物理范围。

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Dynamic scheduling requires dispatching online arrivals while anticipating future jobs, as poor decisions can compound downstream costs under nonlinear tardiness objectives. We show that a pretrained large language model (LLM) can serve as the exogenous component of a structured world model, forecasting uncertain future job arrivals. Specifically, we exploit an afterstate decomposition, which separates known deterministic shop transitions from uncertain exogenous job arrivals, enabling the LLM to forecast only the latter through a compact belief representation. Building on this, we develop Afterstate World-Model Planning (AWMP), which combines an evolved, belief-conditioned dispatching strategy with short scenario-based rollouts. A surprise-triggered refresh mechanism queries the LLM only when newly observed arrivals deviate sufficiently from previous forecast, and online residual self-calibration further conditions the LLM on its recent forecast errors to handle nonstationary arrivals. Across diverse arrival distributions, AWMP reduces weighted tardiness by 6-21% on dynamic job-shop and 22-46% on flexible job-shop benchmarks, relative to the best of eight classical priority-dispatching rules.

## 5. How Representational Priors Shape Generalization in World Action Models / 代表性先验如何塑造世界行动模型中的泛化

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.29168v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29168v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29168v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World action models (WAMs) are shown to substantially diverge under generalization performance based on their generative backbones. We investigate this gap through the representational geometry that WAMs inherit from their backbones and how policy training reorganizes it. Our central hypothesis is that a useful representational prior would structure geometry that separates nuisance information from the in-distribution manifold in advance of training as a policy. We quantify this with nuisance orthogonality; orthogonality of the feature under visual variation outside the empirical in-distribution manifold. Across various image and video backbones, we show that a higher orthogonality from the representational prior is associated with stronger robustness to visual variations after WAM training, enabling policy generalization measurable prospectively for world models. In addition, analyzing the resulting robust WAMs reveals nuisance suppression; relative suppression of feature displacement caused by such variations and robust action generation of the policy. Together, these findings provide a geometric perspective on how the representational prior of the world model shapes the generalization performance of WAMs.

世界行动模型（ WAM ）在基于其生成骨干的泛化性能下表现出明显的差异。 我们通过WAM从其骨干继承的代表性几何结构以及政策培训如何重组来调查这一差距。 我们的中心假设是，一个有用的代表性先验将构建几何结构，在训练作为一种策略之前将滋扰信息与分布流形分开。 我们用讨厌的正交性来量化这一点；在实证分布流形之外的视觉变化下的特征的正交性。 在各种图像和视频骨干中，我们表明，来自表征先验的更高正交性与WAM训练后对视觉变化的更强鲁棒性相关联，从而使世界模型的策略泛化可以前瞻性地测量。 此外，分析所产生的鲁棒WAM揭示了滋扰抑制；由此类变化引起的特征位移的相对抑制和策略的鲁棒动作生成。 总之，这些发现为世界模型的表征先验如何塑造WAM的泛化性能提供了几何视角。

## 9. Self-Improving Agents with World Model Predictive Paths / 具有世界模型预测路径的自我完善代理

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.28945v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28945v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28945v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large language model (LLM) agents are increasingly expected to interact with environments and improve their decisions during deployment. However, how anticipating future outcomes and drawing on accumulated interaction experience can jointly support decision-making remains underexplored. We propose a test-time framework, inspired by Model Predictive Path Integral (MPPI) control, that combines multi-branch predictive analysis with experience-based adaptation. At each step, the agent proposes multiple candidate actions and imagines their outcomes with a world model, so that alternatives can be compared without being executed in the environment. Since language environments provide neither explicit dynamics nor costs, the predicted outcomes are evaluated for task progress and risk, and MPPI-style weights over the resulting costs guide the final decision. A dual-branch experience memory enables self-improvement: the planner branch refines candidate generation and action selection, while the world model branch improves next-observation prediction. Experiments on ALFWorld and ScienceWorld across three backbones demonstrate the strong overall performance of our method and the effectiveness of experience-guided predictive control. Our code will be made publicly available.

## 10. Second Thoughts: Exploring and Planning through Comparative Imagination in World Models / 第二个思考：通过世界模型中的比较想象进行探索和规划

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World models let agents compare imagined outcomes before acting, but an agent must still decide which alternatives to explore and when a proposed plan should replace its learned policy. SDI compares action sequences imagined from the same model state, using repeated rollouts of the actor's own sequence to measure variation under fixed behavior. This reference scales both the spread across candidate returns, which provides an exploration signal, and a proposed plan's predicted advantage, which governs action acceptance. The agent executes a planned action when its advantage exceeds the reference variability by a fixed factor; otherwise, it follows the actor. SDI reuses the base agent's world model, actor, and critic without requiring an additional disagreement ensemble. Exploration and planning guide training data collection, while evaluation uses the learned actor alone. Across Meta-World, Adroit, and DM-Control, SDI achieves a higher observed task-average score than every compared baseline in each suite and matches or exceeds the strongest baseline's performance on 15 of 18 tasks. Paired evaluations from identical simulator states show that the gate improves both recovery from actor failures and preservation of successful behavior relative to ungated planning, while substantially reducing return degradation. Together, these results support comparing imagined alternatives against a shared actor reference as a unified approach to exploring and planning with world models.

## 11. Exploring Temporal Context-Aware Physical Grounding in Latent World Models / 探索潜在世界模型中的时间情境感知物理基础

**Categories**: cs.AI

**Code**: https://github.com/myl11045/G-JEPA.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Learning latent dynamics is fundamental to building action-conditioned world models for planning. Among existing families, Joint Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) offer a natural framework by modeling transitions directly in latent space rather than at the pixel level. However, two issues remain in JEPA-based world models: (i) physical grounding lacks explicit aggregation of temporal context and (ii) action planning always overly relies on generic sampling distributions. To this end, we propose $\mathcal{G}$-JEPA, which incorporates temporal context into physical grounding for latent world models and couples the learned models with prior-guided action planning. Specifically, during learning, we incorporate a shared causal temporal module that aggregates temporal context within observation and prediction sequences under physical state supervision, grounding both in task-relevant physical states. When planning, we further introduce action inference through stepwise conditioning of a generative action distribution on the current and goal latent representations together with their evolving discrepancy, yielding anchored proposals that guide model-based online refinement.
