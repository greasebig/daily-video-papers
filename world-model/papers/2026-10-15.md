## 1. What Matters for World Modeling in Vision-Language-Action Models? / 视觉-语言-动作模型中的世界建模最重要的是什么？

**Date**: 2026-10-14 | **arXiv**: [2610.19825v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19825v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19825v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Integrating world models into Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs) has emerged as a promising direction, enabling policies to anticipate future dynamics beyond reactive observation-to-action mapping. However, existing approaches vary simultaneously in their world representations, integration paradigms, and policy backbones, making it difficult to determine what truly matters for effective world model integration. To disentangle these factors, we categorize existing efforts into four representative paradigms, namely Implicit Future Alignment, Explicit Future Query, MoT Future Expert, and External World Predictor. We instantiate each within a unified framework and systematically evaluate these paradigms with diverse world representations and policy backbones across in-domain, out-of-domain, long-horizon, dynamic, few-shot, and real-world settings. Our study yields four main findings: 1) world representation effectiveness is paradigm-dependent, whereas 2D semantic representations offer robust, cross-paradigm gains; 2) no single paradigm is universally optimal, yet the decoupled MoT Future Expert offers competitive overall performance, particularly in OOD robustness; 3) world modeling consistently benefits policies built on standard VLMs, while decoupled designs exhibit greater compatibility with pretrained VLAs; and 4) world modeling improves performance over action-only baselines across multiple settings, with notable gains on long-horizon and dynamic tasks. Project page and video demonstrations are available at https://dylanmqs.github.io/WM-in-VLAs/.

将世界模型整合到视觉-语言-行动模型（VLA）中已成为一个有前途的方向，使政策能够预测未来的动态，超越反应性观察到行动的映射。然而，现有方法在世界表征、一体化范式和政策支柱方面同时存在差异，因此很难确定什么对于有效的世界模式一体化真正重要。为了理清这些因素，我们将现有的工作分为四种代表性范式，即隐式未来对齐、显式未来查询、MoT 未来专家和外部世界预测器。我们在一个统一的框架内实例化每个范式，并系统地评估这些范式，包括域内、域外、长期、动态、小样本和现实世界环境中的不同世界表征和政策支柱。我们的研究得出了四个主要发现：1）世界表征的有效性是依赖于范式的，而二维语义表征提供了强大的、跨范式的增益； 2) 没有单一范式是普遍最优的，但解耦的 MoT Future Expert 提供了有竞争力的整体性能，特别是在 OOD 鲁棒性方面； 3) 世界建模始终有利于基于标准 VLM 构建的策略，而解耦设计则表现出与预训练 VLA 更大的兼容性； 4) 世界建模提高了跨多个设置的仅行动基线的性能，在长期和动态任务上取得了显着的进步。项目页面和视频演示可在 https://dylanmqs.github.io/WM-in-VLAs/ 获取。

</details>

## 2. StateCraft: Augmenting Video World Models with Abstract-State Reasoning / StateCraft：用抽象状态推理增强视频世界模型

**Date**: 2026-10-14 | **arXiv**: [2610.19795v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19795v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19795v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Video world models aim to simulate complex environments with realistic appearance and dynamics. Maintaining consistency over long interactions requires tracking past actions and understanding how those actions affect the environment. Video world models can only generate partial observations of the entire world at a time. Remembering past observations alone may not be sufficient in every scenario, as they may no longer reflect the world after a sequence of actions. We introduce a paradigm that decouples a persistent, updatable abstract state from the visual state. The abstract state keeps track of the entities' changes as the world evolves, and is used to condition the visual state, which represents the current view. We propose StateCraft, a novel architecture that pairs a language model with a video diffusion model to evolve the dynamic world. We evaluate our approach on generative interfaces, which pose a substantial challenge because a single action may change parts of the state that are not currently visible. To support training and evaluation, we develop a data collection pipeline that produces informative, densely annotated trajectories at scale without human or agent supervision, together with a benchmark measuring generation quality, memory, and controllability. On this benchmark, our approach improves surpasses the state-of-the-art baselines. We demonstrate that StateCraft maintains high self-consistency and strong generation quality over long trajectories. Because the abstract state is explicit, it can be edited directly, and generation follows edits to states never observed in the data. Finally, a proof-of-concept 3D experiment, in which actions change the illumination of an unobserved room, shows that the paradigm can also be applied to spatial environments. Project page: https://insait-institute.github.io/StateCraft/.

视频世界模型旨在模拟具有逼真外观和动态的复杂环境。在长期交互​​中保持一致性需要跟踪过去的行为并了解这些行为如何影响环境。视频世界模型一次只能生成对整个世界的部分观察。在每种情况下，仅记住过去的观察结果可能并不够，因为它们可能不再反映一系列行动之后的世界。我们引入了一种范例，将持久的、可更新的抽象状态与视觉状态解耦。抽象状态跟踪实体随着世界的演变而发生的变化，并用于调节代表当前视图的视觉状态。我们提出了 StateCraft，这是一种新颖的架构，它将语言模型与视频扩散模型配对以发展动态世界。我们评估了我们在生成界面上的方法，这构成了巨大的挑战，因为单个动作可能会改变当前不可见的部分状态。为了支持培训和评估，我们开发了一个数据收集管道，可以在没有人工或代理监督的情况下大规模生成信息丰富、密集注释的轨迹，以及衡量生成质量、内存和可控性的基准。在此基准上，我们的方法改进超过了最先进的基线。我们证明了 StateCraft 在长轨迹上保持了高度的自一致性和强大的生成质量。因为抽象状态是显式的，所以可以直接编辑，并且生成遵循对数据中从未观察到的状态的编辑。最后，概念验证 3D 实验（其中动作改变了未观察到的房间的照明）表明该范例也可以应用于空间环境。项目页面：https://insait-institute.github.io/StateCraft/。

## 3. SceneAE: Scene Generation and Reconstruction in Queryable 3D-Anchored Latent Space / SceneAE：可查询 3D 锚定潜在空间中的场景生成和重建

**Date**: 2026-10-14 | **arXiv**: [2610.19708v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19708v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19708v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent video world models have increasingly adopted 3D memory mechanisms to integrate historical observations into a lightweight and persistent scene representation. This naturally raises a complementary question: if 3D representations can serve as an effective mechanism for memorizing observed scenes, can they also be directly used as the latent space for reconstructing and generating scenes? Motivated by this idea, we represent a scene as a compact and unstructured 3D-anchored latent space (\textbf{SceneAE}). Each latent combines an   explicit 3D position with an implicit content feature and is spatially grounded to describe its local scene region, forming a   sparse latent point cloud. Unlike conventional video-VAE latents that describe how a scene appears from particular viewpoints, our   representation directly describes the scene itself, making it naturally applicable to both reconstruction and generation. For   reconstruction, once the scene has been generated, our persistent 3D representation can be projected and decoded into RGB-D observations by different target cameras, without the need to regenerate a video for each new camera trajectory.   For generation, we leverage the geometry-content separation to first generate camera-controlled 3D geometry and   then synthesize content conditioned on it, reducing camera-appearance entanglement and improving camera   controllability. Experiments show that this 3D latent achieves superior generation quality, computational cost, and camera controllability.

最近的视频世界模型越来越多地采用 3D 内存机制，将历史观察结果集成到轻量级且持久的场景表示中。这自然提出了一个补充问题：如果3D表示可以作为记忆观察场景的有效机制，那么它们是否也可以直接用作重建和生成场景的潜在空间？受这个想法的启发，我们将场景表示为紧凑且非结构化的 3D 锚定潜在空间 (\textbf{SceneAE})。每个潜在点将显式 3D 位置与隐式内容特征相结合，并以空间为基础来描述其局部场景区域，形成稀疏的潜在点云。与描述场景如何从特定视点出现的传统视频 VAE 潜伏不同，我们的表示直接描述场景本身，使其自然适用于重建和生成。对于重建，一旦生成场景，我们的持久 3D 表示就可以通过不同的目标摄像机投影并解码为 RGB-D 观察结果，而无需为每个新摄像机轨迹重新生成视频。   对于生成，我们利用几何-内容分离首先生成相机控制的 3D 几何，然后合成以其为条件的内容，减少相机外观纠缠并提高相机的可控性。实验表明，这种 3D Latent 实现了卓越的生成质量、计算成本和相机可控性。

</details>

## 4. Enhancing Latent World Models with Associative Memory / 用联想记忆增强潜在世界模型

**Date**: 2026-10-14 | **arXiv**: [2610.19683v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19683v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19683v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World models trained via latent dynamics offer a scalable path for embodied planning, yet existing methods suffer from representational collapse, computational overhead, and limited long-horizon capability. We introduce a memory-enhanced latent world model that augments JEPA-style prediction with a content-addressable memory bank. Our approach learns high-level representations directly from observations, operating at multiple temporal scales within a shared latent space, and conditions predictions via adaptive layer normalization. To prevent collapse and ensure effective memory usage, we design two complementary losses that encourage slot diversity and balanced temporal utilization. Experiments on PushT, Tworoom, Reacher, and OGBCube demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance with a lightweight architecture, outperforming conventional latent world models on long-horizon planning tasks.

通过潜在动力学训练的世界模型为具体规划提供了可扩展的路径，但现有方法面临表征崩溃、计算开销和长期能力有限的问题。我们引入了一种记忆增强的潜在世界模型，该模型通过内容可寻址的记忆库增强了 JEPA 式的预测。我们的方法直接从观察中学习高级表示，在共享潜在空间内的多个时间尺度上操作，并通过自适应层归一化进行条件预测。为了防止崩溃并确保有效的内存使用，我们设计了两种互补的损失，以鼓励插槽多样性和平衡的时间利用。在 PushT、Tworoom、Reacher 和 OGBCube 上的实验表明，我们的方法通过轻量级架构实现了最先进的性能，在长期规划任务上优于传统的潜在世界模型。

</details>

## 5. Back on Track: Deployment-time Policy Improvement via World Model Planning / 回到正轨：通过世界模型规划改进部署时策略

**Date**: 2026-10-14 | **arXiv**: [2610.19579v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19579v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19579v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

**Project**: https://policy-improvement-wm.pages.dev/).  <details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

State-of-the-art visuomotor policies are increasingly capable of executing complex tasks, yet can fail during deployment in ways they cannot predict or recover from on their own. Improving their performance and robustness typically requires collecting more data, retraining, or modifying the policy architecture, all of which are time- and resource-intensive in practice. We instead propose an offline-trained monitoring-and-intervention framework that improves a policy during deployment using only black-box query access. From a limited set of labeled on-policy rollouts, we learn a policy-conditioned liveness value that measures the nominal policy's progress toward task completion; we additionally train a compact latent world model using these rollouts together with play data. At deployment, the liveness value continuously monitors execution. When it detects a loss of progress, a short-horizon planner searches the world model for corrective actions using the same value as its planning objective, after which control returns to the nominal policy. We evaluate our method on the LIBERO-Goal task suite and on a Franka Panda manipulator, using the vision-language-action model $π_{0.5}$ and a diffusion policy as nominal policies, and demonstrate significant improvements in task success rates, while supporting real-time control. Additional results and videos are available on the project website (https://policy-improvement-wm.pages.dev/).

最先进的视觉运动策略越来越能够执行复杂的任务，但在部署过程中可能会以无法预测或自行恢复的方式失败。提高其性能和稳健性通常需要收集更多数据、重新训练或修改策略架构，所有这些在实践中都是时间和资源密集型的。相反，我们提出了一种离线训练的监控和干预框架，该框架仅使用黑盒查询访问来改进部署期间的策略。从一组有限的带标签的策略推出中，我们学习了一个策略条件的活跃值，该值衡量名义策略在任务完成方面的进展；我们还使用这些部署和游戏数据来训练一个紧凑的潜在世界模型。在部署时，活跃值会持续监控执行情况。当它检测到进度损失时，短期规划者会使用与其规划目标相同的值在世界模型中搜索纠正措施，之后控制权返回到名义政策。我们使用视觉-语言-动作模型 $π_{0.5}$ 和扩散策略作为名义策略，在 LIBERO-Goal 任务套件和 Franka Panda 操纵器上评估我们的方法，并展示了任务成功率的显着提高，同时支持实时控制。其他结果和视频可在项目网站 (https://policy-improvement-wm.pages.dev/) 上获取。

## 6. One-Step Prediction Fidelity Is a Poor Proxy for Planning Performance in JEPA World Models / 一步预测保真度是 JEPA 世界模型中规划性能的一个较差指标

**Date**: 2026-10-14 | **arXiv**: [2610.19375v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19375v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19375v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Latent world models are routinely selected, checkpointed and ablated on one-step prediction loss, on the assumption that a model which predicts the next latent better is a better model. In model-based RL this assumption is known to be unreliable; we ask whether it also fails for latent world models, where the training target is the model's own embedding and the loss is not expressed in interpretable units. We introduce a frozen-decoder protocol reporting one-step error in physical units, decomposed into the part already present in the encoder's representation and the part the predictor adds. Applying it to a recent end-to-end JEPA world model across three continuous-control environments, we move the metric by fine-tuning and measure goal-conditioned planning on the same checkpoints, with three training seeds and paired planning seeds throughout. Within one environment, a per-epoch ladder shows the relationship saturating after a single epoch: the first epoch improves fidelity by 32% and planning by 4.4 pp, but across the fifteen checkpoints that follow -- a further 15% fidelity improvement, monotone in every training seed -- the correlation between one-step error and planning success is r = -0.010. This is not a task-ceiling artifact: stratifying by a model-independent measure of training-data support, planning in the lowest-support stratum starts at 80%, leaving ample headroom, and is nonetheless flat from epoch 1 onward. Across environments the exchange rate is equally unstable: a 93% fidelity reduction on TwoRoom yields -0.7 pp of planning, while a 40% reduction on PushT yields +4.9 pp [+1.1, +8.9]. All results replicate under a decoder-free measure, latent drift. We also show that a matched continued-training control absorbs the entire fidelity gain we had attributed to an auxiliary objective.

潜在世界模型通常会根据一步预测损失进行选择、检查点和消融，假设能够更好地预测下一个潜在世界的模型是更好的模型。在基于模型的强化学习中，这种假设是不可靠的；我们问它对于潜在世界模型是否也失败了，其中训练目标是模型自己的嵌入，并且损失不以可解释的单位表示。我们引入了一种冻结解码器协议，以物理单位报告单步误差，并将其分解为编码器表示中已存在的部分和预测器添加的部分。将其应用于最近跨三个连续控制环境的端到端 JEPA 世界模型，我们通过在相同检查点上微调和测量目标条件规划来移动指标，并始终使用三个训练种子和配对规划种子。在一个环境中，每个时期的阶梯显示在单个时期后关系饱和：第一个时期将保真度提高了 32%，规划提高了 4.4 pp，但在随后的 15 个检查点中——保真度进一步提高了 15%，每个训练种子都是单调的——单步误差和规划成功之间的相关性是 r = -0.010。这不是一个任务上限工件：通过独立于模型的训练数据支持度量进行分层，最低支持层的规划从 80% 开始，留有充足的空间，但从第 1 轮开始就持平。在各个环境中，汇率同样不稳定：TwoRoom 保真度降低 93% 会产生 -0.7 个百分点的规划，而 PushT 降低 40% 会产生 +4.9 个百分点 [+1.1, +8.9]。所有结果均在无解码器测量（潜在漂移）下复制。我们还表明，匹配的持续训练控制吸收了我们归因于辅助目标的全部保真度增益。

</details>

## 7. Into the LidarVerse: A Real-Time Unified LiDAR World Model Across Tasks and Sensors / 走进 LidarVerse：跨任务和传感器的实时统一 LiDAR 世界模型

**Date**: 2026-10-13 | **arXiv**: [2610.19201v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19201v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19201v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We present LidarVerse, a real-time unified LiDAR world model for sensor simulation across tasks and sensors. Existing LiDAR simulators largely follow one of two complementary paradigms: reconstruction-based methods preserve the geometry of a captured scene but require lengthy per-scene optimization, whereas generative methods amortize learning across data but lack the consistency needed for long-horizon rollouts. LidarVerse brings both paradigms into a single, unified conditional generative framework. A universal tokenizer maps LiDAR range sequences into a shared latent space, where a bidirectional teacher learns a common interface for conditioning on a LiDAR prefix, an HD-Map, a persistent spatial cache, and optionally, text. Varying the available scene evidence turns the same model into a generator of new worlds, a point-cloud forecaster, or a generalizable resimulator of observed scenes without per-scene optimization. We distill the teacher into a four-step autoregressive causal student and maintain both a rolling temporal K/V cache and a world-aligned spatial cache, enabling persistent long-horizon rollouts with real-time inference at 11 FPS on a single H100 GPU. LidarVerse achieves state-of-the-art performance on most reported metrics across all three evaluation protocols, while its tokenizer supports 32-, 64-, and 128-beam LiDAR and generalizes to an unseen scanning pattern. These results demonstrate that generative and reconstruction-grounded LiDAR simulation can be realized within one model, providing the core capabilities for future closed-loop sensor simulation.

我们推出了 LidarVerse，这是一个实时统一的 LiDAR 世界模型，用于跨任务和传感器进行传感器模拟。现有的 LiDAR 模拟器很大程度上遵循两种互补范式之一：基于重建的方法保留捕获场景的几何形状，但需要冗长的每个场景优化，而生成方法分摊跨数据学习，但缺乏长期推出所需的一致性。 LidarVerse 将这两种范式引入到一个统一的条件生成框架中。通用标记器将 LiDAR 范围序列映射到共享潜在空间，双向教师在其中学习用于调节 LiDAR 前缀、高清地图、持久空间缓存和可选文本的通用接口。改变可用的场景证据可以将同一模型转变为新世界的生成器、点云预测器或观察场景的通用重模拟器，而无需针对每个场景进行优化。我们将教师提炼为四步自回归因果学生，并维护滚动时间 K/V 缓存和世界对齐的空间缓存，从而在单个 H100 GPU 上以 11 FPS 的速度实现持久的长视野推出和实时推理。 LidarVerse 在所有三种评估协议的大多数报告指标上均实现了最先进的性能，而其分词器支持 32、64 和 128 束 LiDAR，并可推广到看不见的扫描模式。这些结果表明，基于生成和重建的 LiDAR 仿真可以在一个模型中实现，为未来闭环传感器仿真提供核心功能。

</details>

## 8. Imagining Goals with Geometric Horizon Models for Policy Generalization / 用几何地平线模型想象目标以实现政策泛化

**Date**: 2026-10-13 | **arXiv**: [2610.19129v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19129v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19129v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Training from offline data has allowed for substantial progress in domains such as robotics, leading to general-purpose policies that can be easily applied zero-shot or efficiently finetuned for downstream tasks. However, these policies can still have poor generalization, both due to the choice of modeling objective and from learning from a static dataset. In this work, we focus on the challenging task of zero-shot goal generalization, where a policy is evaluated on unseen tasks that require combining its existing knowledge (compositional generalization). An avenue for improving a policy's generalization is by generating new experience with world models; however, such generation has proven difficult for longer horizons. Thus, to alleviate this issue, we propose TD-Aug, which samples from a geometric horizon model and allows for directly imagining novel outcomes that can be achieved by composing existing knowledge. We demonstrate that training on these future outcomes as goals for goal-conditioned BC policies significantly improves generalization in stitching-based OGBench tasks.

离线数据训练使得机器人等领域取得了实质性进展，从而产生了可以轻松应用零样本或针对下游任务进行有效微调的通用策略。然而，由于建模目标的选择和静态数据集的学习，这些策略的泛化性仍然较差。在这项工作中，我们专注于零样本目标泛化的挑战性任务，其中对需要结合现有知识（组合泛化）的未见任务进行策略评估。提高政策普遍性的一个途径是通过世界模式产生新的经验；然而，从长远来看，这样的一代已被证明是困难的。因此，为了缓解这个问题，我们提出了 TD-Aug，它从几何地平线模型中采样，并允许直接想象通过组合现有知识可以实现的新结果。我们证明，将这些未来结果作为目标条件 BC 策略的目标进行训练，可以显着提高基于拼接的 OGBench 任务的泛化能力。

</details>

## 9. Contact-UAN: Unsupervised Actuator Networks as a Residual World Model for Humanoid Control / 联系-UAN：无监督执行器网络作为人形控制的剩余世界模型

## 25. From Pixels to Executable Physics: Behavior-Grounded World Models for Embodied Action Consequence Prediction / 从像素到可执行物理：用于具体行动后果预测的基于行为的世界模型

**Date**: 2026-10-13 | **arXiv**: [2610.17630v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17630v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.17630v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

In outdoor environments, varying terrain and contact conditions can cause identical action commands to produce different motion outcomes for quadruped robots. Predicting these outcomes is essential for reliable navigation and informed path selection. Existing video world models can generate visually plausible futures, yet often struggle to capture robot dynamics and terrain interactions, leading to overly optimistic predictions of action outcomes. We present DR-WM, a physically consistent world model that incorporates robot-terrain contact dynamics to predict the consequences of quadruped actions. Starting from an initial RGB image, our method reconstructs scene geometry and integrates dense, dimensionless priors over terrain contact properties, calibrated using real-world action-response data. The resulting terrain model supports the simulation of physics with spatially varying contact properties such as friction. Given the initial state of the robot and candidate action sequences, we simulate its future motion and use the resulting camera trajectories and rendered geometry to guide the training of a video world model. The generated future observations are then used to update the terrain reconstruction, enabling iterative model rollouts without additional real-world images. Extensive experiments demonstrate that DR-WM substantially outperforms geometry-only baselines and baselines with uniform physical parameters on key physical metrics characterizing action outcomes, while achieving the best performance among the evaluated baselines. ## 26. D-World: An Explicit World-Language-Action Model with Recursive Self-Improvement under Dual Control / D-World：双重控制下具有递归自我改进的显式世界语言动作模型

**Date**: 2026-10-12 | **arXiv**: [2610.17114v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17114v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.17114v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Frontier large language models (LLMs) now reason about space well enough to program robots, yet the world models behind robot policies are implicit, held in network weights or predicted video, and cannot be inspected or corrected directly. We present D-World, an explicit world-language-action model built on the Sense, Plan and Act decomposition. A perception agent writes the scene as an executable digital twin using generated vision and geometry code. A planning agent searches for actions in simulation and, after each executed action chunk, revises the twin's object states and named physical parameters against what the robot measured. A parameter revision is accepted only if replaying the latest chunk does not increase any measured residual beyond its tolerance, and every chunk is checked in the updated twin before it runs. Structural mismatches return to perception for reconstruction. We call this measurement-grounded cycle of correction and revision recursive self-improvement (RSI), in which the LLM stays frozen while the world it acts in improves. We model the choice of actions that both advance the task and expose model error as a dual control problem and show that the cost of incorporating evidence into the twin sets the threshold at which probing pays, so cheap local revision counteracts the turn-off of cautious controllers. Each episode runs a real-to-sim-to-real loop, and its recorded twins can be refitted and re-rendered offline into higher-fidelity environments for post-training. Because perception and planning communicate only through the twin and language, either can be replaced by a learned model, and since inference dominates the loop, the loop speeds up with faster models. On a dual-arm robot, towel and toaster tasks show local revisions, such as one reducing the bread's rendered-depth residual from 22.4 to 1.5 mm, that let execution continue without reconstruction.

## 27. What Limits Cross-Task Generalization in World Action Models? / 是什么限制了世界行动模型中的跨任务泛化？

**Date**: 2026-10-12 | **arXiv**: [2610.16641v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16641v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.16641v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Cross-task generalization remains difficult for world action models (WAMs), even when their predicted futures depict plausible manipulation. This gap can be attributed directly: we make this gap directly testable by decoupling a WAM into a world model and an inverse-dynamics model (IDM), then holding the IDM fixed while replacing the source of future images. We introduce Align Once, Evaluate Everywhere (AoE), a camera-space inverse-dynamics model pretrained on large-scale robot data, which provides this recovery interface for different future sources across tasks and environments. Through it, we find that actions recovered from recorded futures of unseen tasks execute almost as well as replayed demonstrations, whereas futures predicted by world models collapse across benchmarks. The failures are not generic: they concentrate on the gripper and interaction geometry while generation capacity is spent on task-irrelevant background. This diagnosis suggests that supervision should target exactly these regions. We therefore introduce the vision-to-action (VITA) loss, which passes generated futures through the frozen AoE and penalizes discrepancies in the recovered actions, redirecting generation toward action-relevant detail. Across two world models and three manipulation benchmarks, this simple change raises the execution success of generated futures from near zero to 47.0%. The same loss also transfers to WAMs and yields consistent gains in native control.
