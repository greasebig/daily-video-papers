## 1. Heads of Security: Learned Attention Gates Improve Prompt Injection Robustness / 安全主管：学习注意力门可提高即时注入的稳健性

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37657v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37657v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37657v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.CR

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We introduce Heads of Security (HoS), a prompt injection defense that enforces instruction-data separation via a small set of learned per-head attention gates. HoS learns how each head should redistribute attention between trusted and untrusted tokens, enabling models to reason over untrusted content without following injected instructions. We evaluate HoS on Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct and GPT-OSS-20B. We compare it against SecAlign++, a state-of-the-art finetuning-based defense, and ASIDE, a defense that, like ours, builds instruction-data separation into the model architecture. We show that HoS offers greater robustness to adaptive attacks, better performance on standard utility benchmarks, and matches top performance on standard agentic prompt injection benchmarks. More specifically, the black-box RL-Hammer attack reaches 97% ASR against GPT-OSS-20B and 85% against SecAlign, but only 10% against HoS. Claudini, a state-of-the-art white-box attack, strengthened against HoS through autoresearch, requires 3 times more FLOPs to reach the same ASR on HoS as it does on GPT-OSS-20B or SecAlign. HoS preserves base-model utility across standard multiple-choice benchmarks and prompting setups, whereas SecAlign and ASIDE degrade in few-shot settings. On agentic benchmarks with static attacks, HoS and SecAlign perform similarly and substantially improve the security-utility trade-off over the base model. ASIDE struggles with tool use, achieving only 10% utility. These results show that prompt injection robustness can be achieved and improved through targeted attention interventions.

我们引入了安全头（HoS），这是一种即时注入防御，它通过一小组学习的每头注意力门来强制指令数据分离。 HoS 学习每个头应如何在可信和不可信令牌之间重新分配注意力，使模型能够在不遵循注入指令的情况下推理不可信内容。我们在 Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct 和 GPT-OSS-20B 上评估 HoS。我们将其与 SecAlign++（一种最先进的基于微调的防御）和 ASIDE（一种与我们的防御一样，将指令数据分离构建到模型架构中）进行比较。我们表明，HoS 对自适应攻击提供了更强的鲁棒性，在标准实用程序基准上具有更好的性能，并且在标准代理提示注入基准上具有最佳性能。更具体地说，黑盒 RL-Hammer 攻击针对 GPT-OSS-20B 的 ASR 达到 97%，针对 SecAlign 的 ASR 达到 85%，但针对 HoS 的 ASR 仅为 10%。 Claudini 是一种最先进的白盒攻击，通过自动研究增强了针对 HoS 的攻击能力，在 HoS 上达到与在 GPT-OSS-20B 或 SecAlign 上相同的 ASR 需要 3 倍以上的 FLOPs。 HoS 在标准多项选择基准测试和提示设置中保留了基本模型实用性，而 SecAlign 和 ASIDE 在几次设置中性能下降。在静态攻击的代理基准测试中，HoS 和 SecAlign 的表现类似，并且在基本模型上显着改善了安全性与实用性的权衡。 ASIDE 在工具使用方面遇到了困难，仅实现了 10% 的实用性。这些结果表明，通过有针对性的注意力干预可以实现和提高即时注射的鲁棒性。

## 2. GEPA-Curator: Curated Learning for Self Improving Agents / GEPA-Curator：自我改进代理的策划学习

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37651v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37651v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37651v1)

**Categories**: cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly used in multi-turn agentic workloads that require sustained interaction with complex environments. Existing reflective prompt optimizers such as GEPA learn from failed rollouts, but agentic failures can also come from prompt weaknesses, harness limitations, stochastic execution, or noisy judges. We argue that such failures may not provide useful learning signal, and may even be harmful; and thus it is essential to curate which tasks are provided to the prompt optimizer for better performance. We introduce GEPA-Curator, a framework that augments the prompt optimizer to select which agent experiences should shape prompt optimization. In particular, GEPA-Curator consists of two components: task curation, which filters out tasks that are likely to provide noisy or unreliable learning signals, and prior success retrieval, which selectively retrieves past successful trajectories for a task and provides them as additional context to the prompt optimizer. We evaluate GEPA-Curator on GoBrowse, ALFWorld, AppWorld, and $τ$-bench, using both Gemini 3.1 Pro and Claude Opus 5 as optimizer models. GEPA-Curator matches or exceeds the mean performance of both baselines, with gains of up to 20.7% over GEPA and 21.2% over ACE.

大型语言模型 (LLM) 越来越多地用于需要与复杂环境持续交互的多轮代理工作负载。现有的反射提示优化器（例如 GEPA）可以从失败的部署中学习，但代理失败也可能来自提示弱点、利用限制、随机执行或嘈杂的判断。我们认为，这种失败可能无法提供有用的学习信号，甚至可能是有害的；因此，必须策划向提示优化器提供哪些任务以获得更好的性能。我们引入了 GEPA-Curator，这是一个增强提示优化器的框架，可以选择哪些代理体验应该塑造提示优化。特别是，GEPA-Curator 由两个组件组成：任务管理（过滤掉可能提供嘈杂或不可靠学习信号的任务）和先前成功检索（有选择地检索任务过去的成功轨迹并将其作为提示优化器的附加上下文提供）。我们使用 Gemini 3.1 Pro 和 Claude Opus 5 作为优化器模型，在 GoBrowse、ALFWorld、AppWorld 和 $τ$-bench 上评估 GEPA-Curator。 GEPA-Curator 达到或超过了两个基线的平均性能，比 GEPA 提高了 20.7%，比 ACE 提高了 21.2%。

## 3. EnvSched: Environment-Aware Scheduling for Rollout-Efficient Agent Reinforcement Learning / EnvSched：环境感知调度，用于高效部署代理强化学习

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37609v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37609v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37609v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

As executable environments for language agents scale, generating and verifying their rollouts becomes increasingly expensive. Under finite rollout budgets, this raises an important question: can data management at the environment level make agentic reinforcement learning (RL) more efficient by organizing, evaluating, and scheduling task instances together with the environments that determine their tools, states, transitions, and verification? To address this question, we introduce EnvSched (Environment-aware Scheduling), an online scheduler that learns from rollout history. EnvSched assigns scheduling value through two complementary modeling choices: exploitation prioritizes tasks likely to provide informative reward differences, while coverage spreads each batch across underobserved environments and interaction patterns. EnvSched organizes environment--task pairs as nodes in a dual-relation graph, with within-environment edges capturing task semantics and shared tool, state, and verifier structure, and cross-environment edges capturing abstract execution and reward topology. By propagating historical rollout statistics along both edge types, it predicts within-task reward variance. These predictions guide a scheduler that allocates each batch to tasks with high predicted reward variance and underobserved environments and tasks. Environment quotas and a similarity penalty further limit concentration and redundancy, yielding informative and diverse batches without additional pilot rollouts. In RL on Qwen3-8B, EnvSched achieves an average score of 48.81 across BFCL-v3, tau-Bench, and ACEBench-Agent after 40 training steps, exceeding the highest score attained by random task selection after 120 steps. It also improves downstream performance across model sizes at the same training budget.

随着语言代理可执行环境的扩展，生成和验证其部署变得越来越昂贵。在有限的部署预算下，这提出了一个重要的问题：环境级别的数据管理能否通过组织、评估和调度任务实例以及确定其工具、状态、转换和验证的环境来使代理强化学习（RL）更加高效？为了解决这个问题，我们引入了 EnvSched（环境感知调度），这是一种可以从部署历史记录中学习的在线调度程序。 EnvSched 通过两个互补的建模选择来分配调度值：利用优先考虑可能提供信息奖励差异的任务，而覆盖范围将每个批次分散到观察不足的环境和交互模式中。 EnvSched 将环境-任务对组织为双关系图中的节点，环境内边缘捕获任务语义和共享工具、状态和验证器结构，跨环境边缘捕获抽象执行和奖励拓扑。通过沿两种边缘类型传播历史推出统计数据，它可以预测任务内奖励方差。这些预测指导调度程序将每个批次分配给具有高预测奖励方差和观察不足的环境和任务的任务。环境配额和相似性惩罚进一步限制了集中度和冗余度，无需额外试点即可产生信息丰富且多样化的批次。在 Qwen3-8B 上的 RL 中，经过 40 个训练步骤后，EnvSched 在 BFCL-v3、tau-Bench 和 ACEBench-Agent 上取得了 48.81 的平均分数，超过了 120 个步骤后随机任务选择所获得的最高分数。它还可以在相同的训练预算下提高跨模型大小的下游性能。

</details>

## 4. Gym-Anything-RT: Scaling Real-Time Environments for Vision-Language Agents / Gym-Anything-RT：为视觉语言代理扩展实时环境

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37567v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37567v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37567v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

AI agents have made substantial progress in domains where they have a non-trivial amount of time to deliberate between actions, such as mathematics, software engineering, and performing tasks on desktop applications. It is less clear how such models fare in settings that demand real-time interaction, such as playing a video game or operating a physical device. In these settings, the environment state may evolve as the agent is reasoning, or even as the underlying model is performing a forward pass. To gain insight into the real-time interaction capabilities of vision-language agents, we introduce Gym-Anything-RT. Our key idea is to construct visual minigames that demand a portfolio of capabilities required for interactive real-time settings. Gym-Anything-RT uses an agentic pipeline to automatically generate such environments based on a taxonomy of six capabilities required for real-time interaction, including the ability to perceive the environment and act under an evolving environment state. A key feature of Gym-Anything-RT is that it allows for controlled comparison of real-time interaction capabilities: we can run the same minigame in real time, or pause or slow down the environment to relax the requirement for real-time actions. We use Gym-Anything-RT to construct 100 real-time minigames and evaluate AI agents on them. We find large capability gaps in real-time interaction. For example, GPT-6 Astra (xHigh) achieves a pass rate of only 9.0% on the real-time minigames, which rises to 75.0% when the environment is paused. We also identify intriguing behaviors by the most powerful frontier agents: for instance, when given the ability to write code, these agents write code that bypasses the need to perceive and act in real time. We publicly release Gym-Anything-RT and the generated minigames to spur further research on AI agents in real-time interactive settings.

人工智能代理在有大量时间来考虑各种操作的领域取得了实质性进展，例如数学、软件工程和在桌面应用程序上执行任务。目前还不清楚这些模型在需要实时交互的环境中表现如何，例如玩视频游戏或操作物理设备。在这些设置中，环境状态可能会随着代理的推理而演变，甚至随着底层模型正在执行前向传播而演变。为了深入了解视觉语言代理的实时交互能力，我们引入了 Gym-Anything-RT。我们的主要想法是构建需要交互式实时设置所需的功能组合的视觉迷你游戏。 Gym-Anything-RT 使用代理管道根据实时交互所需的六种功能的分类自动生成此类环境，包括感知环境并在不断变化的环境状态下采取行动的能力。 Gym-Anything-RT 的一个关键特性是它允许实时交互能力的受控比较：我们可以实时运行相同的小游戏，或者暂停或减慢环境以放松对实时操作的要求。我们使用 Gym-Anything-RT 构建 100 个实时迷你游戏并在其上评估 AI 代理。我们发现实时交互方面存在巨大的能力差距。例如，GPT-6 Astra (xHigh) 在实时小游戏上的通过率仅为 9.0%，当环境暂停时，通过率上升到 75.0%。我们还发现了最强大的前沿代理的有趣行为：例如，当被赋予编写代码的能力时，这些代理编写的代码绕过了实时感知和行动的需要。我们公开发布 Gym-Anything-RT 和生成的迷你游戏，以促进实时交互环境中人工智能代理的进一步研究。

## 5. Which Hand Was Late? Diagnosis for Robot Failure Recovery in Bimanual Tool Use / 哪手牌迟到了？双手工具使用中机器人故障恢复诊断

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37533v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37533v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37533v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Failure diagnosers for robot manipulation are increasingly vision--language models (VLMs), trained and evaluated on single-arm failures produced by perturbing a successful demonstration. Bimanual tool use adds failures of other kinds: a tool that slips out of its socket while still held, two correct gestures made at different times, and attempts that fail for two reasons at once. On a simulated bimanual tool-use task whose failure causes are read from the recorded trace, we measure what each sensing channel lets a diagnoser tell. The tool's slip is read on the tool hand; which hand was late is read on both arms, while a sensor on one hand reads its own hand's delay and at most part of the other's; and whether a late attempt was also pushed too weakly is not identifiable from passive sensing when the tool hand is late. Within two attempts, a repair policy that escalates when the cause is not identifiable repairs as many failures as one told the true cause. A VLM's internal state carries the late hand almost perfectly, along a direction no closer to the one its answer reads than a random one; whether the answer reports it depends on the wording of the question.

机器人操作的故障诊断器越来越多地采用视觉语言模型（VLM），对干扰成功演示所产生的单臂故障进行训练和评估。双手使用工具会增加其他类型的失败：工具在握着的情况下从插座中滑出，在不同时间做出两个正确的手势，以及由于两个原因同时失败的尝试。在模拟双手使用工具的任务中，其故障原因是从记录的轨迹中读取的，我们测量每个传感通道让诊断人员告诉的内容。刀具的滑移在刀具手上读取；两只手臂上都会读取哪只手迟到了，而一只手上的传感器会读取自己手上的延迟以及另一只手上的大部分延迟；当工具手迟到时，无法通过被动传感来识别迟到的尝试是否也被推得太弱。在两次尝试内，当原因无法识别时，修复策略会升级，修复的故障数量与真正原因相同。 VLM 的内部状态几乎完美地承载了迟到的手，沿着不比随机方向更接近其答案读取的方向；答案是否报告取决于问题的措辞。

## 6. PerceptRL: Making Visual Perception Rewardable in MLLMs from Answers to Interactions / PerceptRL：从答案到交互，使 MLLM 的视觉感知变得有价值

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Fine-grained visual perception remains a bottleneck during post-training of multimodal large language models (MLLMs), as perceptual errors can constrain gains from reasoning-focused optimization. Although image-caption pre-training improves perception, its influence can weaken as subsequent stages change data mixtures and objectives. Continuing caption supervision during post-training is challenging because subjective, information-dense descriptions complicate verifiable reward design. We present PerceptRL, which strengthens perception at two complementary levels. Perception RL uses a multi-agent pipeline to extract verified errors from dense image captions and convert the corresponding visual facts into QA pairs with fixed answer labels and rule-based rewards. Chain QA links correlated errors, while multiple-choice QA combines unrelated errors. A specialized route targets high-error domains with tailored verification, and a domain-agnostic general route covers errors beyond these domains. Perception Agentic RL addresses fine-grained and dense cases unresolved by single-turn Perception RL through multi-turn inspection with visual tools. We observe coordinate drift even in models trained to use tools, where incorrect crops or annotations distort evidence and propagate errors. Coordinate Injection corrects spatial operations in cold-start trajectories, while a grounding-aware process reward supervises intermediate spatial fidelity alongside final-answer correctness. Training proceeds through shared cold-start SFT, single-turn Perception RL, and multi-turn Perception Agentic RL. Across seven benchmarks under a shared tool-use protocol, PerceptRL achieves 71.6% average accuracy, surpassing the base model with the same tools and the strongest evaluated frontier VLM by 15.7 and 5.2 percentage points, respectively.

## 7. Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning of Co-Adaptive Hip Exoskeleton Control (MARL-Exo) for Metabolic Energy Reduction / 用于减少代谢能量的协同自适应髋部外骨骼控制（MARL-Exo）的多智能体强化学习

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37441v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37441v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37441v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Objective: This study aims to determine whether a hip-exoskeleton policy trained in simulation, by co-adapting with a musculoskeletal human model under a metabolic-energy reward, reduces the metabolic cost of walking in new users without per-user tuning, and how mechanical-power shaping affects the learned assistance. Methods: We developed MARL-Exo, a multi-agent reinforcement learning framework in which a full-body human model (304 muscle actuators; randomized anatomy, gait, and speed) and a bilateral hip-exoskeleton policy are trained concurrently in two stages. The exoskeleton observes only delayed hip kinematics and its past commands; its reward includes muscle metabolic power from a physiologically based energetics model, alone (Metabolic) or with mechanical-power shaping (Metabolic + Power). The Metabolic policy was deployed and tested without retraining in eight healthy adults walking at 1.25 m/s, using indirect calorimetry. Results: In simulation, the Metabolic policy reduced modeled muscle metabolic power by 13.8% relative to no-device walking, versus 8.8% for Metabolic + Power. On hardware, MARL-Exo reduced net metabolic rate by 18.3 $\pm$ 6.6% relative to Passive in all eight participants. Relative to No-Exo, the mean reduction was 7.4 $\pm$ 10.0%; six participants more than offset the 13.1% device-wearing penalty, walking up to 24.2% below their No-Exo cost. Conclusion: A metabolically informed policy co-adapted in simulation transferred to users without per-user tuning and consistently reduced the metabolic cost of walking with the device, while explicit power shaping reduced the simulated benefit. Significance: Unlike per-user human-in-the-loop optimization, this approach yields a single population-level controller and allows assistance objectives to be screened in simulation before human testing.

目的：本研究旨在确定在模拟中训练的髋部外骨骼策略是否通过在代谢能量奖励下与肌肉骨骼人体模型共同适应来降低新用户行走的代谢成本而无需针对每个用户进行调整，以及机械功率塑造如何影响学习到的帮助。方法：我们开发了 MARL-Exo，一种多智能体强化学习框架，其中全身人体模型（304 个肌肉执行器；随机解剖结构、步态和速度）和双边髋部外骨骼策略分两个阶段同时训练。外骨骼仅观察延迟的髋部运动学及其过去的命令；它的奖励包括来自基于生理学的能量学模型的肌肉代谢能力，单独（代谢）或机械功率塑造（代谢+功率）。使用间接量热法，在 8 名以 1.25 m/s 的速度行走的健康成年人中部署和测试了代谢策略，无需重新训练。结果：在模拟中，相对于无设备行走，代谢策略将模型肌肉代谢功率降低了 13.8%，而代谢 + 功率降低了 8.8%。在硬件上，相对于被动式，MARL-Exo 在所有八名参与者中将净代谢率降低了 18.3 $\pm$ 6.6%。相对于 No-Exo，平均减少 7.4 $\pm$ 10.0%；六名参与者抵消了 13.1% 的设备佩戴损失，比 No-Exo 成本低了 24.2%。结论：在模拟中共同适应的代谢知情策略无需针对每个用户进行调整即可转移给用户，并持续降低使用设备行走的代谢成本，而显式功率整形则降低了模拟收益。意义：与每个用户的人机交互优化不同，这种方法产生单个群体级别的控制器，并允许在人体测试之前在模拟中筛选辅助目标。

## 8. Constraining the Commit Boundary: A DIO/ODI-Informed Authorization Guardrail for Agentic Entity Resolution / 约束提交边界​​：用于代理实体解析的 DIO/ODI 知情授权护栏

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37407v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37407v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37407v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CR, cs.DB

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Agentic Entity Resolution (ER) systems iteratively plan actions, retrieve evidence, revise hypotheses, and propose graph mutations. An erroneous identity hypothesis may condition later retrieval and produce apparent confirmation. This paper proposes a Digital Identity Optimization (DIO) and Ontology of Digital Identity (ODI)-informed authorization boundary between evidence available for agent reasoning and evidence eligible to authorize persistent identity-changing mutations.   A domain-governed constraint-binding function maps selected reconstruction-failure distinctions to explicit identity-preserving constraints.
