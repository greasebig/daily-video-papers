## 1. UOPD: Uncertainty-Aware Intervention for On-Policy Distillation of Multi-Turn Agents / UOPD：多轮智能体的策略蒸馏的不确定性感知干预

**Date**: 2026-09-27 | **arXiv**: [2609.34036v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34036v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34036v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student on its own rollouts using dense supervision from a teacher. In multi-turn environments, a mistake at a critical decision step can redirect the subsequent rollout toward poor outcomes. We use low teacher confidence on student actions to select high-uncertainty steps for correction. In a controlled ALFWorld study, a single teacher correction at a low-confidence step improves subsequent student behavior and task success, motivating selective intervention during distillation. We propose UOPD, an uncertainty-aware intervention method for on-policy distillation. At low-uncertainty turns, UOPD executes student actions and applies the standard OPD loss. At high-uncertainty turns, it samples and executes teacher actions and trains the student to imitate them through supervised fine-tuning, which minimizes forward Kullback-Leibler divergence in expectation. UOPD utilizes adaptive uncertainty thresholds to target a scheduled intervention rate. Empirically, we evaluate UOPD across a broad range of agentic tasks, including ALFWorld, WebShop, and Search, demonstrating its superior performance over OPD methods and their variants. UOPD improves WebShop score by up to $15.8\%$ relative to standard OPD.

在策略蒸馏 (OPD) 在老师的密集监督下训练学生自行部署。在多回合环境中，关键决策步骤的错误可能会导致后续的部署转向不良结果。我们利用教师对学生行为的低信心来选择高不确定性的纠正步骤。在一项受控的 ALFWorld 研究中，单个教师在低置信度步骤中进行纠正可以改善随后的学生行为和任务成功率，从而激发蒸馏过程中的选择性干预。我们提出了 UOPD，一种用于在政策蒸馏的不确定性感知干预方法。在低不确定性回合，UOPD 执行学生操作并应用标准 OPD 损失。在高度不确定性的转弯处，它采样并执行教师的动作，并训练学生通过监督微调来模仿它们，从而最大限度地减少期望中的前向 Kullback-Leibler 分歧。 UOPD 利用自适应不确定性阈值来确定预定的干预率。根据经验，我们在广泛的代理任务（包括 ALFWorld、WebShop 和搜索）中评估了 UOPD，证明了其优于 OPD 方法及其变体的性能。相对于标准 OPD，UOPD 将 WebShop 分数提高了高达 $15.8\%$。

</details>

## 2. Maat: Independent Deterministic Contract-Based Governance for Multi-Agent LLM Workflows / Maat：多代理 LLM 工作流程的独立确定性基于合同的治理

**Date**: 2026-09-27 | **arXiv**: [2609.34017v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34017v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34017v1)

**Categories**: cs.SE, cs.AI, cs.MA

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large-language-model multi-agent systems (LLM-MAS) introduce a characteristic reliability problem: an error produced by one agent can be accepted as context by downstream agents and propagate across the workflow. Many proposed safeguards rely on learned or LLM-based judges whose verdicts are themselves probabilistic; we ask whether a deterministic layer can instead stop contract-detectable handoff defects. We present Maat, a runtime governance layer that validates agent-to-agent handoffs against a versioned workflow contract, or anchor, with no language model in the validation or scoring path. We evaluate it in six controlled domain workflows (6-15 agents, 522 trials) with injected data-level defects and a deterministic seven-check rubric. Version 1 reported gains in all six workflows (2.9-26.5%). A post-publication audit found that three benchmark scorers credited any early halt as a prevented defect. On paired trials where the governed run completed or halted on a finding attributable to a verified defect, the rubric score changes by +7.7% to +29.1% in five workflows and is flat in software development; model-call cost falls 17-53% where attributable halts occur early. A hand review of all 94 governed-arm halts found 35 false alarms (37%), caused by validator defects rather than model behaviour; counting those halts as failed work, the governed arm scores below the ungoverned arm in four of six workflows. The results support deterministic handoff validation for contract-expressible defects and show that validator configuration and halt attribution must themselves be tested; they do not establish universal correctness, hallucination detection, or model-independent effectiveness.

大语言模型多代理系统（LLM-MAS）引入了一个典型的可靠性问题：一个代理产生的错误可以被下游代理接受为上下文，并在整个工作流程中传播。许多提议的保障措施依赖于博学或法学硕士的法官，他们的判决本身就是概率性的；我们询问确定性层是否可以阻止合同可检测的切换缺陷。我们提出了 Maat，一个运行时治理层，用于根据版本化工作流合同或锚点验证代理到代理的切换，而验证或评分路径中没有语言模型。我们在六个受控域工作流程（6-15 个代理，522 个试验）中对其进行了评估，并注入了数据级缺陷和确定性的七项检查规则。版本 1 报告了所有六个工作流程的增益 (2.9-26.5%)。出版后审计发现，三名基准评分者将任何提前停止都视为可预防的缺陷。在配对试验中，受控运行因已验证的缺陷而完成或停止，五个工作流程中的评分标准分数变化为 +7.7% 至 +29.1%，而软件开发中则持平；如果归因提前发生，模型调用成本会下降 17-53%。对所有 94 个受控臂停止的手动审查发现 35 个误报 (37%)，这是由验证器缺陷而不是模型行为引起的；将这些停止算作失败的工作，受管控手臂在六个工作流程中的四个中得分低于不受管控手臂。结果支持对合约可表达缺陷的确定性切换验证，并表明验证器配置和停止归因本身必须进行测试；它们没有建立普遍的正确性、幻觉检测或独立于模型的有效性。

</details>

## 3. ICMAPE: In-Context Multiagent Pure Exploration / IMAPE：上下文中的多智能体纯粹探索

**Date**: 2026-09-27 | **arXiv**: [2609.33986v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33986v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33986v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

In some multi-agent systems, the quantity to be optimized is not an externally specified reward but the information acquired about unknown properties of the environment as done in active sequential hypothesis testing (ASHT) problems. However, the ASHT literature tends to focus on finite single-agent problems with well-specified models, while there is currently a gap for practical multi-agent methods that can perform active sequential testing. We fill this gap with ICMAPE, a Bayesian learning-based framework for decentralized multi-agent pure-exploration driven by inference objectives. ICMAPE converts the fixed-confidence identification objective into a reward derived from inference confidence, so that standard reinforcement learning machinery can be applied to decentralized pure exploration. It jointly learns a centralized neural inference network that estimates a posterior distribution over hypotheses from global trajectory data, and decentralized policies that select actions from local observation histories and learn when to stop collecting data once the target confidence is reached. On two synthetic benchmarks and a Maryland nitrate concentration monitoring task based on real-world data, ICMAPE-TD3 achieves target accuracy with fewer exploration steps.

在一些多智能体系统中，要优化的数量不是外部指定的奖励，而是在主动序贯假设检验（ASHT）问题中获取的有关环境未知属性的信息。然而，ASHT 文献倾向于关注具有明确模型的有限单智能体问题，而目前在可以执行主动顺序测试的实用多智能体方法方面存在差距。我们用 IMAPE 填补了这一空白，这是一个基于贝叶斯学习的框架，用于由推理目标驱动的去中心化多智能体纯探索。 IMAPE 将固定置信度识别目标转换为源自推理置信度的奖励，从而使标准强化学习机制可以应用于去中心化的纯探索。它共同学习一个集中式神经推理网络，该网络根据全局轨迹数据估计假设的后验分布，以及分散式策略，该策略从局部观察历史中选择操作，并学习一旦达到目标置信度就何时停止收集数据。在两个综合基准和基于真实世界数据的马里兰州硝酸盐浓度监测任务中，ICMAPE-TD3 以更少的探索步骤实现了目标精度。

</details>

## 4. DynGraphAgentBench: A Benchmark for Agentic Lifecycle Control in Dynamic Graph Anomaly Detection / DynGraphAgentBench：动态图异常检测中代理生命周期控制的基准

**Date**: 2026-09-27 | **arXiv**: [2609.33980v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33980v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33980v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Dynamic graph anomaly detection requires repeated decisions as graph structure and class prevalence drift, yet detector benchmarks usually score a fixed pipeline after current labels are known. We introduce DynGraphAgentBench, an executable benchmark for agentic lifecycle control under delayed feedback. It comprises seven temporal graph datasets with node- and edge-level anomaly tasks, eleven selectable detectors, and eight chronological deployment windows per dataset. In each window, a controller sees only time-causal aggregate context, registered model cards, and its own matured history. It must choose a detector before current-window training or candidate scores exist. A sandboxed executor trains the chosen architecture on mature data, scores a hidden deployment window, and releases the outcome after a one-window delay. A deterministic verifier checks decision timing, leakage guards, legal actions, training scope, and persisted artifacts. We measure detection utility with average precision and capture at fixed review depth, and characterize adaptation through model switches and compute. Complete eight-window trajectories from two primary controllers and a no-memory reference on four datasets, together with three additional controllers on three datasets, expose useful, costly, and ineffective reactions to delayed evidence without granting an exhaustive current-window oracle.

由于图结构和类别流行度漂移，动态图异常检测需要重复决策，但检测器基准通常在已知当前标签后对固定管道进行评分。我们引入了 DynGraphAgentBench，这是延迟反馈下代理生命周期控制的可执行基准。它包含七个时间图数据集，其中包含节点级和边缘级异常任务、十一个可选检测器以及每个数据集八个时间顺序部署窗口。在每个窗口中，控制器只能看到时间因果聚合上下文、注册的模型卡及其自身的成熟历史记录。它必须在当前窗口训练或候选分数存在之前选择一个检测器。沙盒执行器在成熟数据上训练所选架构，对隐藏的部署窗口进行评分，并在一窗口延迟后发布结果。确定性验证者检查决策时间、泄漏防护、法律行动、培训范围和持久工件。我们以平均精度测量检测效用，并以固定的审查深度进行捕获，并通过模型切换和计算来表征适应。来自两个主控制器的完整八窗口轨迹和四个数据集上的无记忆参考，以及三个数据集上的三个附加控制器，暴露了对延迟证据的有用、昂贵和无效的反应，而无需授予详尽的当前窗口预言。

## 6. Prospective Interpretation Risk: Principled Communication Control Between LLMs / 前瞻性解释风险：法学硕士之间的原则性沟通控制

**Date**: 2026-09-27 | **arXiv**: [2609.33885v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33885v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33885v1)

**Categories**: cs.MA, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large language model (LLM) agentic systems increasingly rely on models communicating with one another, yet existing uncertainty and multi-agent methods rarely estimate how a particular receiver will interpret a message before it is sent. This matters in heterogeneous systems, where capable receivers can reconstruct different tasks from the same message. We model this as a sender-receiver problem with a latent receiver type and define prospective interpretation risk (PIR): the probability that a receiver reconstructs a task other than intended. Rather than model an LLM's full input-output behaviour, we use black-box probes relating messages, intended tasks, and receiver-specific reconstructions, yielding scalable supervision while separating interpretation from downstream capability failure. Offline, heterogeneous frozen receivers provide supervision for receiver-conditioned risk and the effects of predefined mutable message features. At deployment, history induces a posterior over receiver types, guiding message revision and selection. We introduce value of interpretation information (VoII), querying for receiver information only when its expected communication benefit exceeds its cost. Our theory characterises when receiver information has decision value and bounds such queries. Empirically, interpretation-failure rates vary by 4-13x across receivers. Receiver information reduces PIR calibration error by 68% relative to a receiver-agnostic predictor, largely by correcting receiver-specific risk levels. PIR-guided revision reduces interpretation failure by 44% relative to the original message and 40% relative to a generic rewrite, mostly through a repair that helps every receiver. VoII outperforms information-gain and random querying at matched cost on the interpretation objective it optimises, lowering interpretation failure from 3.84% to 3.79% while querying 18.2% of episodes.

大型语言模型（LLM）代理系统越来越依赖于相互通信的模型，但现有的不确定性和多代理方法很少估计特定接收者在发送消息之前将如何解释消息。这在异构系统中很重要，有能力的接收者可以从同一消息重建不同的任务。我们将其建模为具有潜在接收者类型的发送者-接收者问题，并定义预期解释风险（PIR）：接收者重建任务而不是预期的概率。我们不是对法学硕士的完整输入输出行为进行建模，而是使用与消息、预期任务和接收者特定的重建相关的黑盒探针，产生可扩展的监督，同时将解释与下游能力失败分开。离线、异构的冻结接收器为接收器条件风险和预定义可变消息特征的影响提供监督。在部署时，历史会导致接收器类型的后验，指导消息修改和选择。我们引入解释信息的价值（VoII），仅当其预期的通信收益超过其成本时才查询接收者信息。我们的理论描述了接收者信息何时具有决策价值并限制了此类查询。根据经验，不同接收者的解读失败率相差 4-13 倍。相对于与接收器无关的预测器，接收器信息可将 PIR 校准误差降低 68%，这主要是通过纠正接收器特定的风险级别来实现的。 PIR 引导的修订相对于原始消息可减少 44% 的解释失败，相对于通用重写可减少 40%，主要是通过对每个接收者都有帮助的修复。 VoII 在其优化的解释目标上以匹配的成本优于信息增益和随机查询，在查询 18.2% 的剧集时将解释失败率从 3.84% 降低到 3.79%。

</details>

## 7. Population Physics, Population Problems: Safety and Emergence in LLM Societies / 人口物理学、人口问题：法学硕士社团的安全性和出现

**Date**: 2026-09-27 | **arXiv**: [2609.33871v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33871v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33871v1)

**Categories**: cs.MA, cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

The collective behaviour of large language model (LLM) societies is not the sum of their individual outputs. It yields statistically distinct, sometimes-unpredictable phenomena, for which the tools we use to study single agents may not scale. Due to recent incidents involving autonomous agentic systems, however, understanding these systems is paramount. For that we introduce a framework for measuring self-organisation in LLM social systems and apply it to three such systems: a Schelling grid, a social network (Moltbook), and a Twitter-like misinformation simulation ('Rogue'). All three exhibit statistically significant self-organisation. Moreover, their relaxation dynamics vary with the environmental information available to the agents, with open-ended systems (Moltbook, Rogue) exhibiting sharp, phase-transition-like dynamics. Further results show that population-level pathologies can emerge even when the LLMs are safety-tuned or monitored, being primarily driven by the coordinated activity of a population subset. We also show when self-organisation does \textit{not} emerge under two additional scenarios (a commons dilemma, GovSim, and a LLM-as-a-judge deliberation scheme, ChatEval). We argue that measuring signatures of this kind offers a lightweight, agent-agnostic diagnostic layer for detecting coordinated collective behaviour in deployed multi-agent systems without relying on natural language or model versioning.

大型语言模型（LLM）社会的集体行为并不是其个体产出的总和。它产生统计上不同的、有时是不可预测的现象，我们用来研究单个代理的工具可能无法扩展。然而，由于最近发生了涉及自主代理系统的事件，了解这些系统至关重要。为此，我们引入了一个用于衡量 LLM 社交系统中自组织的框架，并将其应用于三个这样的系统：谢林网格、社交网络 (Moltbook) 和类似 Twitter 的错误信息模拟 (“Rogue”)。所有这三个都表现出统计上显着的自组织。此外，它们的松弛动态随代理可用的环境信息而变化，开放式系统（Moltbook、Rogue）表现出尖锐的、类似相变的动态。进一步的结果表明，即使法学硕士经过安全调整或监控，人口层面的病症也可能出现，这主要是由人口子集的协调活动驱动的。我们还展示了在另外两种情况下（公共困境，GovSim 和 LLM-as-a-judge 审议方案，ChatEval），自组织何时不出现。我们认为，测量此类签名提供了一个轻量级的、与代理无关的诊断层，用于检测已部署的多代理系统中的协调集体行为，而无需依赖自然语言或模型版本控制。

## 8. CodeActionBench: Evaluating Agentic Code-as-Policy for Embodied Manipulation / CodeActionBench：评估具体操作的代理代码即策略

**Date**: 2026-09-27 | **arXiv**: [2609.33807v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33807v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33807v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

How well can general-purpose multimodal models turn visual understanding and reasoning into embodied manipulation via executable code? We introduce CodeActionBench, a benchmark of 25 manipulation tasks that evaluates this capability through agentic Code-as-Policy. Without task-specific fine-tuning, demonstrations, external specialist perception or grasp modules, privileged scene state, or predefined task policies, agents should select visual evidence, form task-relevant 3D estimates, construct manipulation targets, and iteratively execute and revise their policies. A shared robot API provides RGB observations, calibrated geometric operations, robot feedback, and bounded motion, leaving task-dependent decisions to the evaluated agent. Fixed task instances, resource budgets, and a hidden physical-outcome verifier support controlled comparisons across models and harness configurations. Extensive evaluations across nine configurations and 675 attempts achieve success rates ranging from 2.7% to 73.3%. The strongest configuration, GPT-6 Astra with Codex CLI, solves 22 of 25 tasks at least once in three attempts, demonstrating the best performance while still leaving substantial room for improvement. Trajectory analyses reveal difficulties in spatial alignment, object retention, and completion judgment, including task failures despite successfully completed motions. CodeActionBench provides a controlled testbed for measuring how general-purpose models translate their capabilities into manipulation behavior and for examining typical failure scenarios in that process.

通用多模态模型如何通过可执行代码将视觉理解和推理转化为具体操作？我们引入了 CodeActionBench，这是 25 个操作任务的基准，它通过代理代码即策略来评估此功能。如果没有特定于任务的微调、演示、外部专家感知或掌握模块、特权场景状态或预定义的任务策略，代理应该选择视觉证据，形成与任务相关的 3D 估计，构建操纵目标，并迭代执行和修改其策略。共享机器人 API 提供 RGB 观察、校准的几何运算、机器人反馈和有界运动，将任务相关的决策留给评估的代理。固定任务实例、资源预算和隐藏的物理结果验证器支持跨模型和线束配置的受控比较。对 9 种配置和 675 次尝试进行广泛评估，成功率从 2.7% 到 73.3% 不等。最强大的配置是带有 Codex CLI 的 GPT-6 Astra，在 3 次尝试中至少解决了一次 25 项任务中的 22 项，展示了最佳性能，同时仍然留有很大的改进空间。轨迹分析揭示了空间对齐、物体保留和完成判断方面的困难，包括尽管成功完成运动但任务失败。 CodeActionBench 提供了一个受控测试平台，用于测量通用模型如何将其功能转化为操作行为，并检查该过程中的典型故障场景。

</details>

## 9. Evidence-Inference Reconstruction: When The Evidence Is Recalled But The Reasoning Goes Wrong / 证据推理重建：当证据被召回但推理出错时

**Date**: 2026-09-27 | **arXiv**: [2609.33778v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33778v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33778v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Modern multi-hop LLM agents are equipped with built-in mechanisms to detect errors in intermediate reasoning steps. Such errors trigger corrective actions from these agents, which mostly follow the paradigm of retrying the steps or the reasoning trajectories. ## 49. MORPH: Self-Organising Multi-Robot Task Allocation via Neuroplasticity-Inspired Adaptive Topology / MORPH：通过神经可塑性启发的自适应拓扑进行自组织多机器人任务分配

**Date**: 2026-09-26 | **arXiv**: [2609.32745v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32745v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.32745v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Multi-robot task allocation (MRTA) in dynamic environments faces a fundamental tension: effective coordination requires learned structure, but that structure must adapt when conditions change. Existing methods resolve this by assuming prior task knowledge, a utility function, a cost matrix, or a trained policy making them brittle when deployed without such knowledge or when task distributions shift. We present MORPH(Multi-agent Online Rewiring through Plasticity-guided Hierarchy), a training-free MRTA framework where global allocation quality emerges from 4 local plasticity rules (synaptic, homeostatic, structural, and metaplasticity) applied to a directed pairwise preference matrix updated from runtime co-occurrence and task-completion feedback. MORPH requires no task model, no bid computation, and no offline training; response decisions use learned AGV-to-Picker preferences rather than a fixed proximity rule. Within the Gerkey-Mataric MRTA taxonomy, MORPH is the first method in the single-task, single-robot, instantaneous-assignment class to learn directed pairwise allocation preferences online. Evaluated on the TA-RWARE warehouse benchmark (8-24 agents, 4 maps, 800 steps per episode, 5 seeds), MORPH achieves 110% of all-to-all throughput at N=24 while using only 21% of possible coordination links as an efficiency advantage that grows monotonically with fleet size. Under spatial task distribution shift, MORPH degrades 3x less than proximity-based methods while its learned preferences remain uncorrelated with Manhattan distance. Systematic ablation confirms all four plasticity rules contribute measurably. Two allocation properties emerge without programming: cross-type preference dominance and progressive preference sparsification, mirroring the developmental refinement of biological neural circuits. Learned preferences are driven by task co-occurrence history, not spatial proximity.

## 50. Groupwise Agentic Grading and Advantage Redistribution for Code Agent RL / 代码代理 RL 的分组代理分级和优势重新分配

**Date**: 2026-09-26 | **arXiv**: [2609.32577v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32577v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.32577v1)

**Categories**: cs.CL, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Reinforcement learning (RL) for code agents often uses executable tests to provide binary rewards. With these rewards, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) assigns identical advantages to test-passing trajectories within each rollout group, overlooking differences in implementation quality and adherence to task requirements. This leaves the policy without a learning signal that favors clean, targeted implementations over those containing unnecessary or out-of-scope changes. We introduce GAGAR, a framework for quality-aware credit redistribution in code agent RL. Built on dynamic sampling that retains groups containing both passing and failing trajectories, GAGAR places all trajectories from each group in a shared workspace, where an SFT-trained agentic grader jointly inspects them and ranks the test-passing candidates. Based on this ranking, we downweight lower-ranked trajectories and proportionally rescale the advantages of all test-passing trajectories to restore their original sum. This sum-preserving redistribution retains the relative weights established by quality-based downweighting while shifting credit toward higher-quality implementations. We evaluate GAGAR at industrial scale using pre-RL SFT checkpoints of MiMo-V2.6-Flash (310B total parameters) and MiMo-V2.6-Pro (1.02T total parameters). Controlled code-only Flash experiments show improved code agent performance, reduced trajectory-length growth, and more stable training. We further apply GAGAR in large-scale mixed-task RL with both Flash and Pro. Our results support combining test-based verification with groupwise agentic grading to improve the quality and stability of code agent RL.

代码代理的强化学习 (RL) 通常使用可执行测试来提供二进制奖励。通过这些奖励，组相对策略优化 (GRPO) 为每个推出组内的测试通过轨迹分配相同的优势，而忽略了实施质量和遵守任务要求方面的差异。这使得该政策没有一个学习信号，即有利于干净、有针对性的实施，而不是那些包含不必要或超出范围的变化的政策。我们引入了 GAGAR，一个在代码代理 RL 中进行质量感知信用重新分配的框架。 GAGAR 基于动态采样，保留包含通过和失败轨迹的组，将每个组的所有轨迹放置在共享工作区中，其中受过 SFT 培训的代理评分员将联合检查它们并对通过测试的候选者进行排名。基于这个排名，我们降低排名较低的轨迹的权重，并按比例重新调整所有通过测试的轨迹的优势，以恢复其原始总和。这种保和再分配保留了基于质量的降权所建立的相对权重，同时将信贷转向更高质量的实施。我们使用 MiMo-V2.6-Flash（总参数 310B）和 MiMo-V2.6-Pro（总参数 1.02T）的 RL SFT 前检查点在工业规模上评估 GAGAR。受控的纯代码 Flash 实验表明，代码代理性能得到改善，轨迹长度增长减少，训练更稳定。我们进一步将 GAGAR 应用于 Flash 和 Pro 的大规模混合任务强化学习中。我们的结果支持将基于测试的验证与分组代理评分相结合，以提高代码代理强化学习的质量和稳定性。
