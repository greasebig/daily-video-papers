## 1. Zero-HLM: Generalizable Humanoid Loco-Manipulation without Real Robot Data / 零 HLM：无需真实机器人数据的通用人形机器人操作

**Date**: 2026-10-26 | **arXiv**: [2610.35075v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35075v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35075v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Learning generalizable humanoid loco-manipulation remains bottlenecked by costly real-world demonstrations. Simulation provides a scalable alternative, but requires visually faithful, physics-ready scenes and executable whole-body motions. We present Zero-HLM, a real-to-sim-to-real framework that transforms smartphone captures and simulated source demonstrations into scalable training data for humanoid loco-manipulation. Gaussian-guided agentic real-to-sim reconstruction aligns visual and physical scenes; motion-environment co-adaptation uses rollout feedback to correct whole-body references and interaction geometry. We generate 4,800 physically validated demonstrations for zero-shot transfer across three Unitree G1 tasks. Simulation-trained GR00T N1.7 achieves 66.7% mean full-task success, versus 46.7% with real-data training. On generalized configurations, expanded simulation training raises pooled success from 23% for base-trained real-data policies to 69%, without additional real demonstrations. Project page: https://young-bit.github.io/zero-hlm/

学习通用的人形机器人操作仍然受到昂贵的现实世界演示的瓶颈。模拟提供了一种可扩展的替代方案，但需要视觉忠实、物理就绪的场景和可执行的全身运动。我们提出了 Zero-HLM，这是一个真实到模拟到真实的框架，可将智能手机捕获和模拟源演示转换为用于人形机器人操作的可扩展训练数据。高斯引导的代理真实到模拟重建对齐视觉和物理场景；运动环境协同适应使用推出反馈来纠正全身参考和交互几何形状。我们生成了 4,800 个经过物理验证的演示，用于跨三个 Unitree G1 任务的零样本传输。经过模拟训练的 GR00T N1.7 平均完成任务成功率为 66.7%，而真实数据训练的平均成功率为 46.7%。在通用配置上，扩展的模拟训练将基于基础训练的真实数据策略的总体成功率从 23% 提高到 69%，而无需额外的真实演示。项目页面：https://young-bit.github.io/zero-hlm/

## 2. From Questions to Queries to Insights: A Schema-Grounded Agentic Workflow for Natural-Language Crash Data Analysis / 从问题到查询再到见解：用于自然语言崩溃数据分析的基于模式的代理工作流程

**Date**: 2026-10-26 | **arXiv**: [2610.35054v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35054v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35054v1)

**Categories**: cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Transportation agencies maintain extensive crash databases to identify safety priorities, evaluate countermeasures, and guide resource allocation. Making these data accessible to practitioners requires flexible ways to translate analytical questions into reliable results. Existing dashboards support predefined analyses, while additional requests often depend on specialists who understand database queries, coded fields, and agency conventions. This study presents CrashDB-QA, a fully local, schema-grounded agentic workflow that enables natural-language analysis of crash databases. Specialized components interpret questions, generate and validate read-only SQL, execute queries, and explain the results. A shared knowledge base grounds query construction in the agency's data dictionary and safety-analysis conventions, while recorded intermediate outputs support inspection and auditing. An optional extension, CrashDB-QA+, checks the alignment among the question, query, and execution result and supplies evidence for iterative repair. We evaluate the workflow on a frozen snapshot of 4.4 million Wisconsin crash records using a new benchmark of 300 questions across ten analytical categories and nine local open-weight models. Domain grounding substantially improves query accuracy, and a code-specialized 7B model provides the best accuracy-latency balance among the tested models. Compositional analyses remain more difficult than routine retrieval, particularly comparisons between groups or periods. Verification improves performance when the Judge uses authoritative field definitions and the Refiner receives execution evidence, whereas additional checking alone offers no consistent benefit. These findings demonstrate the potential of locally deployed, domain-grounded workflows to broaden access to crash analysis and identify the reasoning and verification capabilities needed for reliable use.

运输机构维护着广泛的事故数据库，以确定安全优先事项、评估对策并指导资源分配。让从业者能够访问这些数据需要灵活的方法将分析问题转化为可靠的结果。现有的仪表板支持预定义的分析，而额外的请求通常取决于了解数据库查询、编码字段和机构惯例的专家。本研究提出了 CrashDB-QA，这是一个完全本地的、基于模式的代理工作流程，可以对崩溃数据库进行自然语言分析。专用组件解释问题、生成和验证只读 SQL、执行查询并解释结果。共享知识库为该机构的数据字典和安全分析惯例中的查询构建奠定了基础，而记录的中间输出则支持检查和审计。可选扩展 CrashDB-QA+ 检查问题、查询和执行结果之间的一致性，并为迭代修复提供证据。我们使用涵盖 10 个分析类别和 9 个本地开放权重模型的 300 个问题的新基准，在 440 万条威斯康星州事故记录的冻结快照上评估工作流程。域接地极大地提高了查询准确性，并且代码专用的 7B 模型在测试模型中提供了最佳的准确性-延迟平衡。成分分析仍然比常规检索更加困难，特别是组间或时期之间的比较。当 Judge 使用权威的字段定义并且 Refiner 收到执行证据时，验证可以提高性能，而单独进行额外检查并不能带来一致的好处。这些发现证明了本地部署、基于领域的工作流程的潜力，可以扩大崩溃分析的范围，并确定可靠使用所需的推理和验证功能。

## 3. VISTA: Active Exploration for Self-Improvement in Embodied Agents / VISTA：积极探索具身智能体的自我提升

**Date**: 2026-10-26 | **arXiv**: [2610.35051v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35051v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35051v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Agentic embodied intelligence plays a foundational role in open-ended, dynamic physical environments. Rather than treating policy learning as an end-to-end mapping from instructions to actions, recent studies reveal that embodied systems depend not only on pre-trained foundation models, but also on harness. Prior approaches largely treat rollouts as static logs for post-hoc reflection, producing harness updates without autonomously testing and refining them through subsequent embodied interaction. To address this challenge, we propose VISTA, a meta-agent framework that transforms post-hoc rollout reflection into active self-exploration for embodied harness improvement. Specifically, VISTA maintains a harness graph that records the lineage of proposed harnesses, which enables the meta-agent to retrieve prior hypotheses, build on effective mechanisms, and avoid repeatedly exploring failed directions. Each iteration begins from a retained harness version and its execution evidence. Rather than directly committing an update from these historical trajectories, the meta-agent actively explores in the environment. It autonomously generates new rollouts, probes execution failures and recovery behaviors, and iteratively modifies the harness based on the resulting evidence. The refined harness is then evaluated afresh and added to the harness graph. We evaluate VISTA on the LIBERO-Pro, RoboCasa, and RoboTwin benchmarks. Experimental results demonstrate that VISTA progressively improves task success and is more rollout-efficient than static reflection, achieving higher success rates with fewer simulator rollouts.

主体体现智能在开放式动态物理环境中发挥着基础作用。最近的研究表明，具体化系统不仅依赖于预先训练的基础模型，还依赖于利用，而不是将政策学习视为从指令到行动的端到端映射。先前的方法主要将推出视为事后反射的静态日志，生成线束更新，而无需通过后续的具体交互进行自主测试和细化。为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了 VISTA，这是一个元代理框架，可将事后部署反思转变为积极的自我探索，以实现具体的利用改进。具体来说，VISTA 维护了一个线束图，记录了所提出的线束的谱系，这使得元智能体能够检索先前的假设，建立有效的机制，并避免重复探索失败的方向。每次迭代都从保留的线束版本及其执行证据开始。元代理不是直接根据这些历史轨迹提交更新，而是在环境中积极探索。它自动生成新的部署，探测执行故障和恢复行为，并根据结果证据迭代地修改工具。然后重新评估优化后的线束并将其添加到线束图中。我们在 LIBERO-Pro、RoboCasa 和 RoboTwin 基准测试上评估 VISTA。实验结果表明，VISTA 逐步提高了任务成功率，并且比静态反射的部署效率更高，可以通过更少的模拟器部署实现更高的成功率。

## 4. PatentAgentBench: Benchmarking LLM Agents with Professional Patent Task Suites / PatentAgentBench：通过专业专利任务套件对法学硕士代理人进行基准测试

**Date**: 2026-10-26 | **arXiv**: [2610.35031v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35031v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35031v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Patent practice lies at the intersection of technical, legal, and linguistic work, making it challenging yet crucial to technological innovation. Recently, rapid advances in large language models (LLMs) and agentic systems have demonstrated the potential to complete realistic business workflows and even patent works. This raises a question: can current AI systems perform patent work reliably? Existing benchmarks cover a limited range of patent tasks and simplify evidence acquisition by providing reference materials or candidate documents. In this paper, we introduce PatentAgentBench, a benchmark comprising 510 instances across eight task suites: novelty search, freedom-to-operate (FTO) search, design FTO, invalidity search, claim charting, patent drafting, office-action response, and patent translation. Curated from real patent records and professional materials, the benchmark combines task-specific metrics with expert-reviewed annotations and rubrics. Retrieval tasks such novelty search, FTO, etc., require agents to search and compare patent collections without a supplied candidate pool. The drafting and OAR tasks assess the quality of agents' work given technical disclosure and office action notice. We evaluate seven LLMs across four agent harnesses on PatentAgentBench, finding that current systems still struggle to identify novelty, assess risk, and understand practitioner intent throughout patent workflows.

专利实践处于技术、法律和语言工作的交叉点，这使其具有挑战性，但对技术创新至关重要。最近，大型语言模型（LLM）和代理系统的快速发展已经证明了完成现实业务工作流程甚至专利工作的潜力。这就提出了一个问题：当前的人工智能系统能否可靠地完成专利工作？现有基准涵盖有限范围的专利任务，并通过提供参考材料或候选文件来简化证据获取。在本文中，我们介绍了 PatentAgentBench，这是一个包含 8 个任务套件的 510 个实例的基准测试：新颖性检索、自由操作 (FTO) 检索、设计 FTO、无效性检索、权利要求图表、专利起草、审查意见答复和专利翻译。该基准根据真实的专利记录和专业材料进行整理，将特定于任务的指标与专家审查的注释和规则相结合。新颖性搜索、FTO 等检索任务要求代理在没有提供候选池的情况下搜索和比较专利集合。起草和 OAR 任务根据技术披露和审查意见通知来评估代理人的工作质量。我们在 PatentAgentBench 上评估了四个代理工具中的七个法学硕士，发现当前的系统仍然难以在整个专利工作流程中识别新颖性、评估风险并理解从业者的意图。

## 5. ClinVerse: A Realistic Multi-Agent Diagnostic Environment for Training and Evaluating Medical Agents / ClinVerse：用于培训和评估医疗代理的现实多代理诊断环境

**Date**: 2026-10-26 | **arXiv**: [2610.34878v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34878v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34878v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.MA

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Disease diagnosis is a key application of large language model (LLM) agents in healthcare. However, existing medical agents are primarily trained and evaluated with static question-answering data, providing limited opportunities to learn active information acquisition, examination decision-making, and dynamic clinical interaction. To address this limitation, we present ClinVerse, an interactive, real-data-grounded multi-agent environment designed for training and evaluating medical diagnostic agents. Built on ClinPatient, a scalable dataset comprising 150k diagnostic cases derived from real-world electronic health records, ClinVerse supports coordinated interactions among six specialized clinical roles. Specifically, a doctor agent starts from an initial patient complaint, progressively gathers clinical evidence through patient interviews and examination requests, and ultimately produces a diagnosis. For each interaction trajectory, a rule-based scoring framework quantifies information acquisition, examination ordering, and diagnostic accuracy. These trajectory-level scores serve as reward signals for agent training while also enabling scalable evaluation. In experiments, we evaluate various general-purpose and medical-domain models and show that nine self-evolution methods across three categories consistently improve agents' diagnostic capabilities within ClinVerse. We further analyze the interplay among model capability, environment feedback, and self-evolution methods.

疾病诊断是大语言模型（LLM）代理在医疗保健中的关键应用。然而，现有的医疗代理主要通过静态问答数据进行训练和评估，为学习主动信息获取、检查决策和动态临床交互提供了有限的机会。为了解决这一限制，我们推出了 ClinVerse，这是一种交互式、基于真实数据的多智能体环境，旨在训练和评估医疗诊断智能体。 ClinVerse 建立在 ClinPatient（一个可扩展的数据集，包含来自真实世界电子健康记录的 15 万个诊断病例）的基础上，支持六个专业临床角色之间的协调互动。具体来说，医生代理人从最初的患者投诉开始，通过患者访谈和检查请求逐步收集临床证据，并最终做出诊断。对于每个交互轨迹，基于规则的评分框架量化信息获取、检查顺序和诊断准确性。这些轨迹级分数充当代理训练的奖励信号，同时还支持可扩展的评估。在实验中，我们评估了各种通用和医疗领域模型，并表明跨越三个类别的九种自我进化方法持续提高了 ClinVerse 中代理的诊断能力。我们进一步分析模型能力、环境反馈和自我进化方法之间的相互作用。

## 6. Adaptive Retrieval, Selective Answers: Evaluating Conformal Factuality in Agentic RAG / 自适应检索，选择性答案：评估 Agentic RAG 中的共形事实性

**Date**: 2026-10-26 | **arXiv**: [2610.34872v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34872v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34872v1)

**Categories**: cs.CL, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) can improve factual grounding without guaranteeing that every generated claim is supported. Previous work has studied claim-level conformal factuality in single-stage RAG and, more recently, multi-hop RAG. We examine its behavior in agentic RAG, where a language-model policy adaptively issues follow-up retrieval queries or terminates retrieval before response generation. We retain the retrieval-based relevance-scoring formulation and post-generation conformal filtering and evaluate six model-dataset configurations: GPT-6 Luna and Llama 3.1 8B on HotpotQA, Natural Questions, and TriviaQA. Each configuration uses 60 fixed questions, 100 seeded 30/30 calibration-test partitions, five conformal target levels, and a no-conformal-prediction baseline. Across the six configurations, higher target levels are accompanied by higher observed full-support rates and reduced answer availability. At the 95% target, non-empty response coverage ranges from 17.23% to 60.17%, claim retention from 10.72% to 43.16%, and full support among non-empty outputs from 89.14% to 95.56%. We formulate the adaptive retrieval trajectory explicitly and characterize the reliability-informativeness trade-off when conformal factuality is applied after agent termination.

检索增强生成 (RAG) 可以改善事实基础，但不能保证每个生成的主张都得到支持。之前的工作研究了单阶段 RAG 以及最近的多跳 RAG 中的声明级共形事实性。我们在代理 RAG 中检查其行为，其中语言模型策略自适应地发出后续检索查询或在响应生成之前终止检索。我们保留基于检索的相关性评分公式和后生成共形过滤，并评估六种模型数据集配置：HotpotQA、Natural Questions 和 TriviaQA 上的 GPT-6 Luna 和 Llama 3.1 8B。每个配置使用 60 个固定问题、100 个种子 30/30 校准测试分区、五个共形目标级别和一个非共形预测基线。在这六种配置中，更高的目标水平伴随着更高的观察到的完全支持率和更低的答案可用性。在 95% 的目标下，非空响应覆盖范围为 17.23% 至 60.17%，声明保留范围为 10.72% 至 43.16%，非空输出的完全支持范围为 89.14% 至 95.56%。我们明确地制定了自适应检索轨迹，并描述了在代理终止后应用共形事实时的可靠性与信息性权衡。

## 7. Semantic Atoms for Scalable Text Analysis in Data Analytics in the Era of LLM-Based Agents / 基于 LLM 的代理时代数据分析中可扩展文本分析的语义原子

**Date**: 2026-10-26 | **arXiv**: [2610.34860v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34860v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34860v1)

**Categories**: cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Much of the information needed for data analysis is buried in text columns: an analyst may infer customer frustration from complaint text and use it to explain resolution time. We call workloads in which processing text columns is central to subsequent data analysis TADA (\emph{Text Analysis in Data Analytics}). In an agentic setting, the user's query dynamically specifies this processing, and answering it interleaves LLM-based semantic steps with exact SQL or Python operations. This mixed execution raises two design questions: which work should be neural and which symbolic, and which semantic outputs later steps can reuse. We address them for agent-planned TADA workflows with two complementary, query-driven semantic atoms. Each atom takes a sequence of text items and a natural-language query specifying the semantic task: \textbf{Tagging} returns one string per input item, while \textbf{Categorization} returns $M$ category objects over $N$ input items. Tagging results are materialized as a row-aligned column and Categorization results as a category table; SQL or Python then performs exact computation over this exposed state. We realize this interface in TADA-Agent, whose atoms run inside generated Python programs and internally handle inputs beyond a reliable context window. We evaluate TADA-Agent on TABench, a benchmark of 40 complete analysis requests over spreadsheet tables spanning workflow composition, input--output structure, and literature-grounded analytical scenarios. Against a semantic-operator baseline and relative to a held-out TADA reference run, TADA-Agent delivers comparable row-level semantic quality, has higher complete-artifact preservation estimates, and uses over an order of magnitude fewer semantic-model requests under the evaluated native execution paths.

数据分析所需的大部分信息都隐藏在文本列中：分析师可以从投诉文本中推断出客户的挫败感，并用它来解释解决时间。我们将处理文本列作为后续数据分析的核心的工作负载称为 TADA（\emph{数据分析中的文本分析}）。在代理设置中，用户的查询动态指定此处理，并回答它，将基于 LLM 的语义步骤与精确的 SQL 或 Python 操作交织在一起。这种混合执行提出了两个设计问题：哪些工作应该是神经性的，哪些是符号性的，以及哪些语义输出后续步骤可以重用。我们通过两个互补的、查询驱动的语义原子来解决代理规划的 TADA 工作流程。每个原子采用一系列文本项和一个指定语义任务的自然语言查询：\textbf{Tagging}为每个输入项返回一个字符串，而\textbf{Categorization}则返回$N$个输入项上的$M$个类别对象。标记结果具体化为行对齐列，分类结果具体化为类别表；然后，SQL 或 Python 对此公开的状态执行精确计算。我们在 TADA-Agent 中实现了这个接口，它的原子在生成的 Python 程序内运行，并在内部处理超出可靠上下文窗口的输入。我们在 TABench 上评估 TADA-Agent，这是一个对电子表格表进行 40 个完整分析请求的基准，涵盖工作流程组成、输入输出结构和基于文献的分析场景。根据语义运算符基线并相对于保留的 TADA 参考运行，TADA-Agent 提供可比较的行级语义质量，具有更高的完整工件保留估计，并且在评估的本机执行路径下使用的语义模型请求少一个数量级。

## 8. DecoyCount: Evaluating Visual-Reasoning Misalignment in Counting / DecoyCount：评估计数中的视觉推理偏差

**Date**: 2026-10-26 | **arXiv**: [2610.34855v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34855v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34855v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Visual counting benchmarks report whether vision-language models produce the correct number, but not whether errors arise from perception or reasoning. We introduce DecoyCount, a benchmark built from 1,660 real-world images with designed decoys across seven mechanisms, along with paired prompts: a neutral question, a matched alignment cue, and a novel multi-hop, multi-image question that situates the same counting problem in a realistic scenario. Using this design, we find that counting failures are induced partly by visual misalignment: models can execute the intended counting process when cue-guided but fail to select it unaided, and we term this difference in accuracy the reasoning-alignment gap. Despite being highly capable on general reasoning, frontier models remain below 50\% accuracy on our benchmark, while alignment gaps range from 11.8-22.1 percentage points; strikingly, we observe that more capable models, greater thinking budgets, and agentic processes often induce larger gaps. Because humans may bring context that models lack, we test whether textual underspecification contributes to misalignment using a scenario-based multi-hop condition without explicit hinting, but this added context does not improve aggregate performance. Our analysis concludes that stronger general reasoning does not by itself generalize to aligned visual reasoning, leaving misalignment with human visual expectations as a substantial component of the remaining deficit.

## 39. EnvLunch: The Environment as a Free Lunch for Learning to Interact / EnvLunch：环境作为学习互动的免费午餐

**Date**: 2026-10-24 | **arXiv**: [2610.33817v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33817v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.33817v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.DB

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

For agentic data analysis, language-model agents often require a large number of annotated tasks to learn how to interact effectively with executable environments (i.e., databases). While high-quality annotated tasks require heavy manual effort, an executable environment already provides rich interaction structure and verifiable outcomes. In this sense, the environment itself offers a free lunch, an additional set of verifiable tasks that require no human annotation. We propose EnvLunch, a warm-up stage that turns this environmental signal into agent training without human-written or model-generated task descriptions, before downstream agentic reinforcement learning. EnvLunch samples valid action trajectories, executes them to obtain final observations, and trains an agent to recover a functionally equivalent trajectory given only its final observation. This label-free warm-up enables the model to outperform the baseline by over 9 points on average across all downstream training budgets, and with few labels, the baseline needs 8 to 32 times as many annotated examples to match the accuracy that the EnvLunch model reaches at its smallest training budget. Letting the environment alone define the task yields a larger gain than having the model write its own tasks over the same environments, and this warm-up also improves fine-tuning on benchmarks beyond data analysis. EnvLunch therefore saves annotated tasks and provides a better starting point for fine-tuning agentic models when such tasks are limited.

对于代理数据分析，语言模型代理通常需要大量带注释的任务来学习如何与可执行环境（即数据库）有效交互。虽然高质量的带注释的任务需要大量的手动工作，但可执行环境已经提供了丰富的交互结构和可验证的结果。从这个意义上说，环境本身提供了一顿免费午餐，一组额外的不需要人工注释的可验证任务。我们提出了 EnvLunch，这是一个预热阶段，在下游代理强化学习之前，将这种环境信号转化为代理训练，而无需人工编写或模型生成的任务描述。 EnvLunch 对有效的动作轨迹进行采样，执行它们以获得最终观察结果，并训练代理在仅给出最终观察结果的情况下恢复功能等效的轨迹。这种无标签预热使模型在所有下游训练预算中平均优于基线 9 个点以上，并且在标签很少的情况下，基线需要 8 到 32 倍的带注释示例才能匹配 EnvLunch 模型在其最小训练预算下达到的准确性。让环境单独定义任务比让模型在相同环境中编写自己的任务产生更大的收益，并且这种预热还改进了数据分析之外的基准的微调。因此，EnvLunch 保存了带注释的任务，并在此类任务受到限制时为微调代理模型提供了更好的起点。

## 40. The Hitchhiker's Guide to System 1 Models: Fast Decision-Making Beyond LLMS / 系统 1 模型漫游指南：超越 LLMS 的快速决策

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large Language Models excel at reasoning and generation but their autoregressive, token-by-token inference makes them unnecessarily slow, expensive, and poorly calibrated for the many routine decisions (routing, gating, scoring, classification) that modern AI systems require at sub-second latency. A growing class of models addresses this gap: System 1 decision models, architectures that map an input directly to a typed, structured decision in a single forward pass.
