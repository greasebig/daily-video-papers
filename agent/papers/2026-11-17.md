## 1. VIVID: A Visual Harness for Agentic Video Editing / VIVID：用于代理视频编辑的视觉工具

**Date**: 2026-11-16 | **arXiv**: [2611.20688v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20688v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.20688v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CV, cs.MM

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Video editing requires agents to make editing decisions grounded in audiovisual observations and precise temporal localization, and to evaluate their effects in rendered outputs. General-purpose coding agents possess strong comprehension, reasoning, and code execution capabilities, while media interaction and evidence management mechanisms tailored to video tasks can further unlock their editing potential. We introduce VIVID, a visual harness that supports multiple native coding agents in video post-production without additional model training. VIVID provides tools for on-demand video inspection, audio analysis, and transcription, and preserves observations alongside their corresponding media versions and temporal coordinates. Agents can actively retrieve these observations as they reason, perform edits using their native coding and shell tools, and inspect rendered outputs to guide subsequent revisions. On AgenticVBench, comprising 100 tasks across Assembly, Repair, Sequencing, and Repurpose, Codex with VIVID achieves an average score of 58.6%, an improvement of 20.2 percentage points over native Codex. The same harness also integrates with Claude Code and OpenCode, achieving average scores of 55.1% and 37.1%, respectively, both substantially exceeding their native baselines. These results highlight VIVID's potential as a reusable visual harness for extending the capabilities of general-purpose coding agents to video editing.

视频编辑要求代理根据视听观察和精确的时间定位做出编辑决策，并评估其在渲染输出中的效果。通用编码代理拥有强大的理解、推理和代码执行能力，而针对视频任务定制的媒体交互和证据管理机制可以进一步释放其编辑潜力。我们推出了 VIVID，一种视觉工具，支持视频后期制作中的多个本机编码代理，无需额外的模型训练。 VIVID 提供用于按需视频检查、音频分析和转录的工具，并保留观察结果及其相应的媒体版本和时间坐标。代理可以在推理时主动检索这些观察结果，使用其本机编码和 shell 工具执行编辑，并检查渲染的输出以指导后续修订。在 AgenticVBench 上，包含 100 项涉及组装、修复、排序和重新利用的任务，使用 VIVID 的 Codex 平均得分为 58.6%，比原生 Codex 提高了 20.2 个百分点。该工具还与 Claude Code 和 OpenCode 集成，平均得分分别为 55.1% 和 37.1%，均大大超过了其原生基准。这些结果凸显了 VIVID 作为可重复使用的视觉工具的潜力，可将通用编码代理的功能扩展到视频编辑。

</details>

## 2. Learning and Verifying Progress: Closed-Loop Agentic Planning with Progress-Enhanced Execution for Long-Horizon Vision-Language Navigation / 学习和验证进度：闭环代理规划与进度增强执行，用于长视野视觉语言导航

**Date**: 2026-11-16 | **arXiv**: [2611.20676v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20676v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.20676v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Long-horizon vision-language navigation (VLN) requires agents to track instruction progress and recover from local errors before they propagate across subtasks. However, executors trained on expert trajectories encounter policy-induced state shifts during deployment, and their stop signals do not reliably indicate subtask completion. We introduce Learning and Verifying Progress for VLN (LeaP-VLN), an agentic VLN system that couples progress-aware execution with independent progress verification through plan--execute--verify--recover cycles. LeaP-VLN leverages progress both explicitly, through a progress verifier that verifies subtask completion before the agent advances and triggers dual-phase recovery upon route deviation, and implicitly, by co-training the executor on progress reasoning and action prediction to enhance the executor's progress awareness and corrective control. To adapt the executor to this closed-loop interface, we propose a two-stage progress-enhanced curriculum: behavior cloning on expert demonstrations, followed by joint training on demonstrations, expert action corrections, and progress-reasoning supervision. Policy-generated trajectories expose the executor to both intra-subtask drift and inter-subtask state shifts, aligning training with the deviations encountered during closed-loop execution. Extensive experiments on R2R-CE and RxR-CE demonstrate state-of-the-art success rates among monocular methods trained without additional navigation datasets, while real-world experiments further validate the effectiveness of LeaP-VLN in long-horizon navigation.

长视野视觉语言导航（VLN）要求智能体在跨子任务传播之前跟踪指令进度并从本地错误中恢复。然而，接受专家轨迹训练的执行器在部署过程中会遇到策略引起的状态转变，并且它们的停止信号不能可靠地指示子任务完成。我们介绍了 VLN 的学习和验证进度（LeaP-VLN），这是一种代理 VLN 系统，通过计划-执行-验证-恢复周期将进度感知执行与独立进度验证结合起来。 LeaP-VLN 显式地利用进度，通过进度验证器在代理前进之前验证子任务完成情况，并在路线偏离时触发双阶段恢复；隐式地利用进度推理和动作预测来共同训练执行器，以增强执行器的进度意识和纠正控制。为了使执行器适应这种闭环界面，我们提出了一个两阶段的进度增强课程：专家演示的行为克隆，然后是演示的联合训练，专家动作纠正和进度推理监督。策略生成的轨迹使执行器面临子任务内漂移和子任务间状态变化，从而使训练与闭环执行期间遇到的偏差保持一致。 R2R-CE 和 RxR-CE 上的大量实验证明了在没有额外导航数据集的情况下训练的单目方法中最先进的成功率，而现实世界的实验进一步验证了 LeaP-VLN 在长视距导航中的有效性。

</details>

## 3. MASTA: A Feedback-Scheduled Multi-Agent System for End-to-End Tamarin Modeling and Analysis / MASTA：用于端到端狨猴建模和分析的反馈调度多智能体系统

**Date**: 2026-11-16 | **arXiv**: [2611.20671v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20671v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.20671v1)

**Categories**: cs.CR, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Tamarin-based formal verification of security protocols requires expert-authored multiset rewriting rules and temporal-logic lemmas. Translating a natural-language protocol description into a complete formal model with an LLM is hard because syntactic, reachability, and property-level errors compound across the multi-step modeling process, and a single agent that both authors and self-evaluates cannot reliably localize them. We present MASTA, a multi-agent system that addresses these difficulties through three innovations. First, an operational--evaluative role decomposition assigns rule modeling to an Architect, property modeling to an Auditor, and pure dispatch to a dedicated Arbiter that holds no authoring duties. Second, the Arbiter implements a Tamarin-anchored scheduler that dispatches the next agent based on the latest verifier verdict and routes problem-tagged fixes through a modification list, with a six-class problem taxonomy and an LLM deduplication check preventing routing deadlock. Third, we construct a benchmark of 10 protocols paired with 139 mutation variants and five fine-grained metrics that jointly assess model correctness ($M_1$--$M_3$) and completeness ($M_4$--$M_5$). On this benchmark, MASTA on Qwen 3.5-plus improves over OpenCode on the same backbone by up to $+0.27$ on individual metrics and ranks first on both the correctness and completeness averages, surpassing Codex (GPT-5.5) and Claude Code (Opus 4.7) despite using a weaker LLM. Ablations on five protocols confirm that removing the Arbiter collapses property and verification scores to zero.

基于 Tamarin 的安全协议形式验证需要专家编写的多集重写规则和时态逻辑引理。使用法学硕士将自然语言协议描述转换为完整的形式模型很困难，因为语法、可达性和属性级错误在多步骤建模过程中复合在一起，而作者和自我评估的单一代理无法可靠地定位它们。我们推出了 MASTA，这是一个多智能体系统，它通过三项创新解决了这些困难。首先，操作评估角色分解将规则建模分配给架构师，将属性建模分配给审计员，并将纯粹调度分配给不承担创作职责的专用仲裁员。其次，Arbiter 实现了一个 Tamarin 锚定的调度程序，该调度程序根据最新的验证程序判决调度下一个代理，并通过修改列表路由带有问题标记的修复程序，并使用六类问题分类法和 LLM 重复数据删除检查来防止路由死锁。第三，我们构建了 10 个协议的基准，搭配 139 个突变变体和 5 个细粒度指标，共同评估模型的正确性 ($M_1$--$M_3$) 和完整性 ($M_4$--$M_5$)。在此基准测试中，Qwen 3.5+ 上的 MASTA 在各个指标上比同一主干上的 OpenCode 提高了 0.27 美元，并且在正确性和完整性平均值上均排名第一，尽管使用较弱的 LLM，但超过了 Codex (GPT-5.5) 和 Claude Code (Opus 4.7)。对五个协议的消融证实，删除仲裁者会将财产和验证分数降至零。

</details>

## 4. GranularGPT: Natural-Language-Driven End-to-End Discrete Element Method Simulations / GranularGPT：自然语言驱动的端到端离散元方法模拟

**Date**: 2026-11-16 | **arXiv**: [2611.20551v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20551v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.20551v1)

**Categories**: physics.comp-ph, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Discrete element method (DEM) simulations are widely used to investigate granular and particulate systems, but their application often requires specialized expertise in particle physics, numerical modelling, and simulation post-processing. Large language models (LLMs) offer new opportunities to simplify these workflows by enabling users to interact with simulation tools through natural language. This study offers GranularGPT, an LLM-assisted agentic framework for end-to-end DEM simulation workflows, from natural-language problem descriptions to simulation execution and automated post-processing. The framework enables users to generate DEM simulation cases, execute and monitor simulations, and automatically extract and report physical and process-level metrics. Evaluation across 60 normal-condition cases covering 12 DEM scenarios and parameter variations achieve a simulation success rate of 91.7%. In the error-handling evaluation, all 10 repairable errors are detected and 9 are successfully resolved, while all 5 non-repairable cases are correctly identified and reported. The framework automatically extracts a range of DEM-relevant metrics, including particle size distributions, packing and density measures, velocity profiles, discharge rates, mixing indices, contact force characteristics, and thermal quantities. GranularGPT provides a foundation for improving the accessibility and efficiency of DEM simulation workflows while reducing the specialized expertise required for their operation.

离散元法 (DEM) 模拟广泛用于研究颗粒和颗粒系统，但其应用通常需要粒子物理、数值建模和模拟后处理方面的专业知识。大型语言模型 (LLM) 使用户能够通过自然语言与模拟工具进行交互，从而提供了简化这些工作流程的新机会。本研究提供了 GranularGPT，这是一个 LLM 辅助的代理框架，用于端到端 DEM 仿真工作流程，从自然语言问题描述到仿真执行和自动后处理。该框架使用户能够生成 DEM 模拟案例、执行和监控模拟，并自动提取和报告物理和过程级指标。对涵盖 12 个 DEM 场景和参数变化的 60 个正常条件案例进行评估，模拟成功率达到 91.7%。在错误处理评估中，检测到所有 10 个可修复错误，并成功解决了 9 个错误，同时正确识别和报告了所有 5 个不可修复错误。该框架自动提取一系列与 DEM 相关的指标，包括粒度分布、堆积和密度测量、速度分布、排放速率、混合指数、接触力特征和热量。 GranularGPT 为提高 DEM 模拟工作流程的可访问性和效率提供了基础，同时减少了操作所需的专业知识。

## 5. VAHBench: A Harness Benchmark for Agentic Long-Video Generation / VAHBench：代理长视频生成的利用基准

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

AI agents are commonly used for everyday information retrieval and coding. We ask whether they can also interpret human requests and turn them into complete videos. We introduce VAHBench, a benchmark that tests this ability through five short-drama briefs specifying story, characters, dialogue and duration. Agents plan and carry out production using shared generation tools. Evaluation combines video completeness and basic quality with checks of production reliability and reporting accuracy. 人工智能代理通常用于日常信息检索和编码。我们询问他们是否也可以解释人类的请求并将其转化为完整的视频。我们引入了 VAHBench，这是一个基准测试，通过五个指定故事、人物、对话和持续时间的短剧简介来测试这种能力。代理使用共享生成工具计划和执行生产。评估将视频完整性和基本质量与生产可靠性和报告准确性检查结合起来。在涵盖 48 种通用和视频专用代理配置的 240 次运行中，更高的质量分数并不一定意味着更完整的交付。缺失的剧集、重复的镜头和静态填充揭示了制作视频文件和完成任务之间的差距。添加的组合工具可以帮助某些代理，但不能持续提高性能。 VAHBench 衡量代理将用户意图转化为最终视频内容的效果，并帮助识别该过程的问题所在。

## 6. TrainExplorer: Autonomous LLM Post-Training via Plan-and-Decouple Exploration / TrainExplorer：通过计划和解耦探索进行自主法学硕士培训后

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

LLM-based agents can automate model post-training, accelerating progress toward recursive self-improvement. ## 7. Ontology-Compiled Control Barrier Functions for Heterogeneous Robot Teams / 本体编译的异构机器人团队控制屏障函数

**Date**: 2026-11-15 | **arXiv**: [2611.20306v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20306v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.20306v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Safety filters for heterogeneous robot teams fail because of missing constraints, overestimated braking capabilities and conflicts between individually satisfiable constraints. We present OntoCBF, which compiles a domain ontology with a class taxonomy, typed relations, safety axioms and a dynamics profile per robot class into a control barrier function filter with one quadratic program per agent. Description-logic entailment supplies the constraints of any team online, including those that follow from chains of relations and from classes defined by what a robot carries, so the existing safety axioms cover new robot classes, payload classes and combinations. We prove that a subclass may reuse the braking capability of its parent whenever it can produce every velocity of the parent, a property separate from subsumption. For box-bounded double integrators this property is also necessary, and every loss of safety then begins with a conflict. Once three robots interact, the safe set of pairwise barriers contains states from which a collision is unavoidable, and such states persist in larger teams under any fixed capability reduction. In a simulated warehouse where humans ignore the robots, a filter written for the design classes violates safety in 89-100 % of the episodes with unseen classes. In all scenarios, including one with 32 robots, OntoCBF violates safety in 20-70 % of the episodes, each time after a conflict, with a median deepest intrusion of 10-17 % of the keep-out distance. A multi-agent PPO policy trained with the filter in the loop lowers the rate of infeasible quadratic programs, nearly all of them conflicts, by 18-41 %.

异构机器人团队的安全过滤器由于缺少约束、高估制动能力以及单独可满足的约束之间的冲突而失败。我们提出了 OntoCBF，它将具有类分类、类型化关系、安全公理和每个机器人类的动力学概况的领域本体编译成每个代理一个二次程序的控制障碍函数过滤器。描述逻辑蕴涵提供了任何在线团队的约束，包括来自关系链和机器人携带的定义的类的约束，因此现有的安全公理涵盖了新的机器人类、有效负载类和组合。我们证明，只要子类能够产生父类的每个速度，子类就可以重用其父类的制动能力，这是一个与包含无关的属性。对于盒界双积分器来说，这个属性也是必要的，并且每次安全性的丧失都以冲突开始。一旦三个机器人交互，成对障碍的安全集合包含碰撞不可避免的状态，并且在任何固定能力降低的情况下，这些状态在较大的团队中持续存在。在人类忽略机器人的模拟仓库中，为设计类编写的过滤器在 89-100% 的未见过类的事件中违反了安全性。在所有场景中，包括 32 个机器人的场景，每次冲突后，OntoCBF 在 20-70% 的事件中都会违反安全规定，中位最深入侵为禁止距离的 10-17%。使用循环过滤器训练的多智能体 PPO 策略将不可行的二次规划（几乎所有二次规划都发生冲突）的发生率降低了 18-41%。

</details>

## 8. DAPPT: Domain-Aware Policies from Procedural Trajectories for Sim-to-Real Dexterous Tool Use / DAPPT：来自程序轨迹的领域感知策略，用于模拟到真实的灵巧工具使用

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Sim-to-real RL has achieved impressive results in dexterous manipulation but remains limited to tasks with accurate rigid-body simulation. However, these methods cannot be applied to skills that involve deformable and particles, with which the simulation speed will become too slow for large-scale RL learning. We present DAPPT, a sim-to-real framework for end-to-end dexterous tool-use manipulation skills, e.g., sand scooping, play-dough rod-rolling, without simulating non-rigid materials. ## 9. Infrastructure-Based Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning with Attention for Highway Ramp Merging / 基于基础设施的多智能体强化学习，关注高速公路匝道合并

**Date**: 2026-11-15 | **arXiv**: [2611.20078v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20078v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.20078v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.MA, eess.SY

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

On-ramp merging is a typical safety and throughput problem on highways. Without proper coordination, human drivers and self-interested autonomous vehicles may make risky manoeuvres and cause accidents or congestion. With recent advances in connected autonomous vehicles (CAVs) and multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), vehicles can be trained to coordinate for safe and efficient merging. However, in dense traffic, forming an effective observation of the traffic state is challenging. For example, observing only the neighbouring vehicles may lead to locally optimal decisions, while observing the entire traffic can introduce much irrelevant information. In this paper, we propose a transformer-based MARL vehicle control model to address these problems. <details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

## 19. A Hierarchical Statistical Framework for Evaluating Agent Reliability / 评估代理可靠性的分层统计框架

**Categories**: cs.AI, cond-mat.stat-mech, math.PR

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Agents operating in complex scenarios often face uncertainty due to incomplete information about the environment they operate in, ambiguous user queries, complex business rules and the inherently stochastic dynamics of the governing policy. Several new benchmarks have been proposed to evaluate LLM-based agents on complex tasks in multi-tool environments, including settings with policy constraints and varying levels of intent ambiguity. These conditions make it important to evaluate agentic systems systematically and quantify their performance under different conditions to assess their reliability in production. This need is evident in the recently introduced Thinkingbox benchmark, where even the strongest models struggle to perform consistently across diverse business scenarios. This paper presents a statistical framework for evaluating uncertainty and reliability in agentic systems. ## 20. CLEW: Log-Anchored Backward Tracing for Root-Cause Code Localization / CLEW：用于根本原因代码本地化的日志锚定向后跟踪

**Date**: 2026-11-14 | **arXiv**: [2611.19448v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19448v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.19448v1)

**Categories**: cs.SE, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Failures in industrial software systems may involve multiple components and be difficult to reproduce outside the production environment. Root-cause code localization therefore often relies on issue descriptions and runtime logs. However, log evidence may be noisy or incomplete, and the code reporting a symptom may differ from the code containing the underlying defect. These conditions make it challenging to identify relevant log evidence and determine which functions could have caused the reported failure. To address this gap, we introduce CLEW, an agentic approach that combines rule-based log-to-code anchoring with LLM-guided backward code tracing. CLEW organizes selected observations into diagnostic states, guides source exploration from anchored symptom functions, and returns ranked candidate functions, an affected module, and an explanation comprising a proposed mechanism path and associated log events.
