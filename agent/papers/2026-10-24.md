## 1. Can an Agent Debug a Web Corpus? Agentic Auditing of Slovak FineWeb2 / 代理可以调试网络语料库吗？斯洛伐克 FineWeb2 的代理审核

**Date**: 2026-10-22 | **arXiv**: [2610.31636v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31636v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31636v1)

**Categories**: cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Multilingual pipelines such as FineWeb2 apply one recipe across a thousand languages, leaving the failure modes of individual languages unanticipated. We use an LLM once, as a corpus debugger rather than a classifier: a tool-using agent explores the corpus through a read-only, budgeted API and compiles each finding into an executable test, which a native speaker then validates. On the 26.5M-document Slovak portion of FineWeb2, three runs costing \$2.21-\$2.84 each found seven findings over four phenomena. The largest is a machine-generated content farm of 1,408,437 documents (5.32%) that passed the pipeline's filters, 86.6% of it passing even stricter thresholds. Blinded labels support 6 of the 7 findings (defect rates .53-1.00 against .08 in a random control) and do not support one. The compiled suite scans the full corpus in 0.7 s of wall-clock time without a GPU, against \$12k-\$90k per LLM classification pass. The best static sampling baseline validates more selectors than any agent run, at under half the cost. The agent contributes exact prevalence, a precision estimate checked against sampled documents, an account of why the pipeline missed each slice, and a reusable test. We release the framework, Slovak test suite, validation data and agent trajectories.

FineWeb2 等多语言管道在 1000 种语言中应用一种方法，从而使个别语言的失败模式无法预料。我们使用 LLM 一次，作为语料库调试器而不是分类器：使用工具的代理通过只读、预算 API 探索语料库，并将每个发现编译成可执行测试，然后由母语人士验证。在 FineWeb2 的 2650 万文档斯洛伐克部分，进行了 3 次运行，每次运行成本为 2.21 美元至 2.84 美元，发现了四种现象的 7 个结果。最大的是机器生成的内容场，其中有 1,408,437 个文档 (5.32%) 通过了管道的过滤器，其中 86.6% 通过了更严格的阈值。盲法标签支持 7 项发现中的 6 项（缺陷率为 0.53-1.00，随机对照中的缺陷率为 0.08），但不支持一项。编译后的套件无需 GPU，即可在 0.7 秒的挂钟时间内扫描完整的语料库，而每次 LLM 分类过程的成本为 12,000 美元至 90,000 美元。最佳静态采样基线可以验证比任何代理运行更多的选择器，而成本却不到一半。该代理提供准确的流行率、根据采样文档检查的精确估计、解释管道错过每个切片的原因以及可重复使用的测试。我们发布了框架、斯洛伐克测试套件、验证数据和代理轨迹。

</details>

## 2. Social-HUMINT: An LLM-Augmented Unified Framework for Measuring Social Media Exposure / Social-HUMINT：用于衡量社交媒体曝光度的法学硕士增强统一框架

**Date**: 2026-10-22 | **arXiv**: [2610.31573v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31573v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31573v1)

**Categories**: cs.CR, cs.AI, cs.SI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

In the post-API landscape, social-media profiling has become inexpensive and automated: Large Language Models (LLMs) can extract structured information from unstructured content and deduce attributes users never disclosed. A person's true exposure is not what they post but what can be inferred from it. As these practices become routine, exposure to social engineering attacks becomes an operational certainty, demanding a shared framework for assessment and mitigation.   We make this exposure measurable by quantifying how accurately a target can be profiled, and how far that profile enables social engineering attacks. Because no existing model fully spans this continuum, we unify Social Media Intelligence (SOCMINT) and Cyber Human Intelligence (Cyber-HUMINT), two individual-focused disciplines that evolved separately, into a single doctrine-grounded taxonomy of 38 capabilities across the Cyber Threat Intelligence lifecycle, where user profiling bridges passive collection and active engagement. Domain experts validated the taxonomy (Fleiss' $κ$ = 0.92).   We operationalize its core path (extraction, inference, and susceptibility assessment) as an LLM-augmented multi-agent pipeline, evaluated on real participants' public data. Susceptibility is assessed indirectly: the pipeline predicts how each target would rank personalized engagement options, matching their actual choices significantly above chance. This ranking capacity captures the personalization on which tailored attacks depend. The underlying profiles are accurate: extraction reaches F1 = 97.3%, and inferred attributes score 4.73/5, both far above rule-based and NER-based baselines at negligible cost. The framework augments security analysts' tradecraft rather than replacing it and enables defenders to gauge their own exposure before an adversary does.

在后 API 时代，社交媒体分析变得廉价且自动化：大型语言模型 (LLM) 可以从非结构化内容中提取结构化信息，并推断出用户从未披露过的属性。一个人的真实曝光率不是他们发布的内容，而是从中可以推断出的内容。随着这些做法变得司空见惯，暴露于社会工程攻击成为一种必然的操作，需要一个共享的评估和缓解框架。   我们通过量化对目标进行分析的准确程度以及该分析对社会工程攻击的影响程度来衡量这种暴露程度。由于现有模型没有完全跨越这个连续体，因此我们将社交媒体情报 (SOCMINT) 和网络人类情报 (Cyber​​-HUMINT) 这两个单独发展的以个人为中心的学科统一为一个基于理论的分类法，涵盖整个网络威胁情报生命周期的 38 种功能，其中用户分析连接了被动收集和主动参与。领域专家验证了分类法（Fleiss 的 $κ$ = 0.92）。   我们将其核心路径（提取、推理和敏感性评估）作为 LLM 增强的多代理管道进行操作，并根据真实参与者的公共数据进行评估。敏感性是间接评估的：管道预测每个目标如何对个性化参与选项进行排名，将他们的实际选择远远高于机会。这种排名能力捕捉了定制攻击所依赖的个性化。底层配置文件是准确的：提取达到 F1 = 97.3%，推断属性得分为 4.73/5，均远高于基于规则和基于 NER 的基线，且成本可以忽略不计。该框架增强了安全分析师的情报技术，而不是取代它，并使防御者能够在对手之前评估自己的暴露程度。

## 3. Same Luck, Different Choice: Twin-World Credit Assignment for Agentic Reinforcement Learning / 同样的运气，不同的选择：代理强化学习的双世界信用分配

**Date**: 2026-10-22 | **arXiv**: [2610.31544v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31544v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31544v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Agentic reinforcement learning rewards a complete episode, yet the policy needs credit for every decision inside it. Counterfactual branching estimates this credit by re-running the episode after an alternative decision, but the branch draws its sampling noise independently of the factual episode, so the outcome difference mixes the effect of the decision with the luck of everything that follows. We observe that in agentic search the retriever, the corpus, and the grader are deterministic, so an episode is a deterministic function of the policy's decisions and its sampling noise. Twin worlds are therefore realizable: the same noise, a different decision. We introduce TWICE (TWIn-world Credit Estimation), which keys the Gumbel noise of every token by turn and line, rolls out counterfactual twins that inherit the factual episode's downstream noise, and turns their paired outcome differences into turn-level advantages for policy-gradient updates on actor-sampled trajectories; only the actor is deployed. Twin credit is unbiased for any coupling quality, its variance is that of an independent counterfactual scaled by $1-ρ$, where $ρ$ is the twins' outcome correlation, and under coupling the number of rollouts needed to resolve an advantage of size $Δ$ falls from $Θ(Δ^{-2})$ to $Θ(Δ^{-1})$. A square-root rule spends the twin budget where outcomes are predicted to disagree per unit cost. On seven question-answering benchmarks, TWICE improves the average exact match of GRPO by 5.3 and 4.5 points on Qwen2.5-3B and 7B, outperforms tree-search and counterfactual-branching baselines, and uses 68% fewer auxiliary tokens than VinePPO.

代理强化学习奖励一个完整的情节，但该策略需要对其内部的每个决策进行信用。反事实分支通过在替代决策后重新运行情节来估计此信用，但分支独立于事实情节提取采样噪声，因此结果差异将决策的效果与随后发生的一切的运气混合在一起。我们观察到，在代理搜索中，检索器、语料库和评分器都是确定性的，因此情节是策略决策及其采样噪声的确定性函数。因此，孪生世界是可以实现的：同样的噪音，不同的决定。我们引入了 TWICE（TWIn-world Credit Estimation），它通过回合和线路对每个标记的 Gumbel 噪声进行关键控制，推出继承事实情节下游噪声的反事实双胞胎，并将它们的配对结果差异转化为回合水平优势，以对参与者采样轨迹进行策略梯度更新；仅部署演员。双胞胎信用对于任何耦合质量都是无偏的，其方差是按 $1-ρ$ 缩放的独立反事实方差，其中 $ρ$ 是双胞胎的结果相关性，并且在耦合下，解决 $Δ$ 大小优势所需的推出次数从 $θ(Δ^{-2})$ 下降到 $θ(Δ^{-1})$。平方根规则花费双预算，其中预计每单位成本的结果不一致。在七个问答基准上，TWICE 在 Qwen2.5-3B 和 7B 上将 GRPO 的平均精确匹配提高了 5.3 和 4.5 分，优于树搜索和反事实分支基线，并且使用的辅助标记比 VinePPO 少 68%。

</details>

## 4. Conservative Meta-Agent Search: Architecture Adaptation under Non-Stationarity / 保守元代理搜索：非平稳性下的架构适应

**Date**: 2026-10-22 | **arXiv**: [2610.31436v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31436v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31436v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Automated agentic-system design is typically evaluated on static task suites with immediate scalar rewards. Deployed systems face persistent shift, delayed dependent feedback, correlated component failures, and costly architecture changes. We formalize meta-agent architecture search as sequential incumbent replacement with switching costs and hard risk constraints and introduce Conservative Meta-Agent Search (CMAS). CMAS retrieves archived architectures and proposes typed graph edits, then scores each against the incumbent by paired shadow replay under common random numbers, with interventional component credit. Promotion requires an empirically calibrated, variance-penalized simultaneous score above a pre-specified margin, and is followed by a simulated canary stage and pre-specified rollback. Since backtests cannot certify meta-level skill, we introduce MetaInvest-Bench, a regime-switching simulator whose specified worker competence makes a surrogate-optimal architecture computable, with leakage-controlled replay, adversarial governance tracks, and five negative controls. Financial streams serve as a risk-sensitive testbed for meta-level generalization; we claim no alpha. Across 96 streams, CMAS cuts normalized mean surrogate dynamic-oracle regret from 0.116 to 0.104 and raises held-out utility from 0.44 to 0.48 over the strongest adaptive baseline; when extra workers add no value, it contracts to near-single-agent teams. Its deployment inference cost is ~6% lower, though its search-inclusive token cost is the highest of the methods compared. The observed per-update false-promotion rate is 3.4% against a nominal 5% target, and 4.8% in a sealed rerun. The calibration is empirical: it concerns an update-level replay statistic on an author-designed benchmark, and guarantees neither full-utility improvement, nor absence of simulated canary-stage harm, nor sequence-level error control.

自动化代理系统设计通常在具有即时标量奖励的静态任务套件上进行评估。已部署的系统面临持续的转变、延迟的相关反馈、相关组件故障以及代价高昂的架构变更。我们将元代理架构搜索形式化为具有转换成本和硬风险约束的连续现有替代，并引入保守元代理搜索（CMAS）。 CMAS 检索存档的架构并提出类型化的图形编辑，然后通过在常见随机数下的配对影子重放以及干预组件信用来对现有架构进行评分。升级需要经过经验校准、方差惩罚的同时得分高于预先指定的裕度，然后是模拟金丝雀阶段和预先指定的回滚。由于回测无法证明元级别的技能，因此我们引入了 MetaInvest-Bench，这是一种政权切换模拟器，其指定的工作人员能力使替代最优架构可计算，具有泄漏控制重放、对抗性治理轨道和五个负面控制。金融流充当元级泛化的风险敏感测试平台；我们声称没有阿尔法。在 96 个流中，CMAS 将标准化平均代理动态预言后悔从 0.116 降低到 0.104，并将保留效用从最强自适应基线的 0.44 提高到 0.48；当额外的工人没有增加任何价值时，它就会承包给近乎单一代理的团队。尽管其搜索包含的令牌成本是比较方法中最高的，但其部署推理成本降低了约 6%。观察到的每次更新错误促销率为 3.4%（名义目标为 5%），而密封重运行则为 4.8%。校准是经验性的：它涉及作者设计的基准的更新级别重放统计数据，并且既不保证完全效用改进，也不保证不存在模拟金丝雀阶段损害，也不保证序列级错误控制。

## 5. Certified Execution Harness for Agentic AI: Safe and Least-Intrusive Petri-Net Supervisory Control Between Reasoning and Action / 代理人工智能的认证执行工具：推理与行动之间安全且侵入性最小的 Petri-Net 监督控制

**Date**: 2026-10-22 | **arXiv**: [2610.31376v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31376v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31376v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, eess.SY

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

This paper studies the problem of safe tool execution for large language model (LLM) agents under prompt injection. Using Petri nets, we formalize the execution harness between the LLM and its tools as a supervisory control problem, in which marks represent capabilities, approvals and budgets. Since a transition fires only if the marks it needs are present, an injected request whose preconditions are unmet or already spent is refused regardless of its wording. Treating tool outcomes as uncontrollable, we enforce safety by the conventional maximally permissive supervisor, and this safety transfers to the running system under four assumptions. Moreover, to capture an agent that may propose again after a refusal, we introduce the notion of a replanning closed loop, in which each retry costs an inference call. We show that, in this loop, the conventional supervisor can be arbitrarily more intrusive than a stricter safe one. Furthermore, for a given proposer model, the maximally permissive least-intrusive supervisor is characterized as a value-threshold refinement of the conventional one. Under precise conditions, this refinement orders two concurrent actions by at most one monitor place. On an autonomous release agent, we find by synthesis that safe deployment requires a step that the supervisor can refuse after a rollout starts.

本文研究了即时注入下大型语言模型（LLM）代理的安全工具执行问题。使用 Petri 网，我们将 LLM 及其工具之间的执行工具形式化为监督控制问题，其中标记代表能力、批准和预算。由于仅当需要的标记存在时才会触发转换，因此无论其措辞如何，先决条件未满足或已花费的注入请求都会被拒绝。将工具结果视为不可控，我们通过传统的最大许可监管者强制执行安全性，并且这种安全性在四个假设下转移到运行系统。此外，为了捕获在拒绝后可能再次提出建议的代理，我们引入了重新规划闭环的概念，其中每次重试都会花费一次推理调用。我们证明，在这个循环中，传统的监管者可能比更严格的安全监管者更具侵入性。此外，对于给定的提议者模型，最大允许最少侵入性监督者的特征是传统监督者的值阈值细化。在精确的条件下，这一改进最多由一个监视位置命令两个并发操作。在自主发布代理上，我们综合发现安全部署需要一个主管在部署开始后可以拒绝的步骤。

## 6. Muffin - A Shared Memory Buffer for Asynchronous AI Agents / Muffin - 异步 AI 代理的共享内存缓冲区

**Date**: 2026-10-22 | **arXiv**: [2610.31355v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31355v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31355v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.MA

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

The rise of Agentic AI systems has changed the landscape of artificial intelligence by enabling strong reasoning capabilities through specialized multi-agent interaction with structured self-reflection. However, in target-driven reasoning tasks, single- and multi-agent systems are often on par in terms of performance gains. A major challenge is that failures often arise from an ineffective exchange of critical information during inter-agent interaction and coordination. Different from existing multi-agent debate paradigms that rely on synchronous turn-taking and sharing information across different agents, we propose an inter-agent protocol based on shared Memory bUFFer INference - referred to as MUFFIN - in an asynchronous agentic framework. In the first step, an orchestrator routes queries based on their reasoning demands and delegates demanding ones to a multi-agent setting, where a shared memory buffer provides asynchronous exchange of intermediate findings while an agent's private reasoning remains local. Our experiments show that our framework Muffin outperforms established competitors, achieving the best accuracy in 17 out of 20 of our benchmark settings, and provides a better quality-cost trade-off than multi-agent debate: Muffin improves accuracy by 19.2 accuracy points while using 77.4% fewer tokens, without requiring full-trace sharing.

Agentic AI 系统的兴起改变了人工智能的格局，通过专门的多智能体交互和结构化自我反思来实现强大的推理能力。然而，在目标驱动的推理任务中，单代理系统和多代理系统在性能提升方面通常不相上下。一个主要挑战是，故障通常是由于代理间交互和协调期间关键信息的无效交换而引起的。与依赖于同步轮流和跨不同代理共享信息的现有多代理辩论范式不同，我们在异步代理框架中提出了一种基于共享内存缓冲区 INference（称为 MUFFIN）的代理间协议。第一步，编排器根据推理需求路由查询，并将要求较高的查询委托给多代理设置，其中共享内存缓冲区提供中间结果的异步交换，而代理的私有推理保留在本地。我们的实验表明，我们的框架 Muffin 优于成熟的竞争对手，在 20 个基准设置中的 17 个中实现了最佳准确度，并且提供了比多智能体辩论更好的质量成本权衡：Muffin 将准确度提高了 19.2 个百分点，同时使用的令牌减少了 77.4%，且不需要全跟踪共享。

## 7. Automating Feature Flag Cleanup at Scale with Multi-Agent LLM Systems: An Industrial Experience at DoorDash / 使用多代理 LLM 系统大规模自动化功能标记清理：DoorDash 的行业经验

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Feature flags are runtime configuration mechanisms used for gradual feature rollouts, experimentation, and kill switches. Once a feature is fully rolled out, the flag and its gated code become technical debt and can introduce operational risk through inadvertently enabling dormant code paths. At DoorDash, over 56,000 feature flags span 623 repositories, with 1,659 currently stale as of May 2026, with internal estimates of one to two hours per manual cleanup. Existing rule-based cleanup tools proved insufficient in DoorDash's codebases. Our contribution is an end-to-end automated LLM-based feature flag cleanup system that integrates querying live feature flag rollout data using MCP, Jira ticket processing, and end-to-end pull request creation. 功能标志是用于逐步功能推出、实验和终止开关的运行时配置机制。一旦某个功能全面推出，该标志及其门控代码就会成为技术债务，并且可能会因无意中启用休眠代码路径而引入操作风险。在 DoorDash，超过 56,000 个功能标记跨越 623 个存储库，截至 2026 年 5 月，其中 1,659 个功能标记目前已过时，内部估计每次手动清理需要一到两个小时。事实证明，现有的基于规则的清理工具在 DoorDash 的代码库中是不够的。我们的贡献是一个基于 LLM 的端到端自动化功能标记清理系统，该系统集成了使用 MCP 查询实时功能标记推出数据、Jira 票证处理和端到端拉取请求创建。我们的系统成功地为所评估的 50 个陈旧标志中的 45 个（90%）生成了工程师批准的标志清除 PR，证明了系统的大规模操作可行性，每次清除平均只需 4.79 美元和 13.8 分钟。我们根据成功率、每次清理成本和时间指标评估了系统的质量和可靠性边界。此外，我们还讨论了在构建该系统时吸取的经验教训，为开发类似的多代理系统提供实用指导。

## 8. Beyond Accuracy: Disentangling Decomposition from Typed-State Quality Assurance in Multi-Agent LLM Pipelines / 超越准确性：从多代理 LLM 管道中的类型化状态质量保证中解脱出来

**Date**: 2026-10-22 | **arXiv**: [2610.31280v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31280v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31280v1)

**Categories**: cs.SE, cs.AI, cs.ET, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Multi-agent LLM pipelines are often credited with gains in software engineering tasks, but it is rarely clear whether a gain stems from the typed contract between agents or from the specialized prompts that decomposition enables. We study this attribution problem in Requirements Engineering, comparing three architectures for classifying requirements as functional or non-functional and then prioritizing them with MoSCoW: a single-call baseline; a two-call variant that reuses the pipeline's specialized prompts but passes an unvalidated intermediate; and a pipeline with a Pydantic-validated typed state. The 18 conditions cover PROMISE NFR+ (625 requirements), three locally hosted small language models, and two languages. The F1 gain over the baseline is associated with the two-call, specialized-prompt variant (up to +0.075), whereas the typed state adds a small difference (-0.003 to +0.013) that is significant in one of six comparisons. Beyond accuracy, the typed pipeline contains out-of-range model outputs at the agent boundary, while the unvalidated variant fails later and drops the requirement, and it shows a small, reproducible increase in inter-model MoSCoW consistency whose cause our design cannot isolate. The study is associative: the contrasts bundle prompts, call count, and staged context, and the results come from three 7--8B models on one dataset. The implementation and replication package are archived on Zenodo (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23380444).

## 9. CoWriter: Guiding Methodological and Synergetic Human-LLM Co-Authoring / 共同作者：指导方法论和协同人类法学硕士共同创作

**Categories**: cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.HC

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

While LLMs have transformed text generation, standard chat-and-canvas interfaces often reduce human authoring to quite passive supervision, causing review fatigue, over-reliance on AI, and the sidelining of the human role in long-form, source-grounded writing. Prior systems typically address isolated writing phases, leaving comprehensive end-to-end human-LLM collaboration largely unexplored. In this paper, we introduce CoWriter, a proactive agentic scheme that adapts expert human (co-)authoring methodologies from cognitive science to flexibly steer the entire writing workflow while actively encouraging the user to engage. In an exploratory user study, compared to a standard LLM baseline, authors using CoWriter better expressed their perspectives and maintained a higher sense of document ownership, supported quantitatively by $2.2\times$ more successful agent engagements. Furthermore, external reader judges rated the resulting texts higher in "human touch" qualities like storytelling and integrating unique personal experiences. Overall, our results demonstrate that proactively engaging users through established human practices is beneficial for effective LLM-supported co-authoring, providing useful methodologies, techniques and insights for future research on collaborative agents.

## 10. RIPPLE: Recovery Internalization through Process-Level Policy Learning and Effect-Aware Credit for Long-Horizon Agentic Coding / RIPPLE：通过流程级策略学习和效果感知信用实现长期代理编码的恢复内化

**Date**: 2026-10-22 | **arXiv**: [2610.31276v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31276v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31276v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Long-horizon coding agents can observe a failure and generate a plausible critique yet continue along the same incorrect trajectory. We call this the Critique-to-Action Handoff Gap. We introduce RIPPLE, a recovery-oriented training framework that internalizes process-level guidance into a single self-recovering policy. A prefix-only teacher provides evidence-grounded recovery supervision without access to future actions or terminal outcomes. After lightweight distillation, reinforcement learning assigns stage-specific credit to reflection triggering, diagnosis, and corrective action. Local validity signals gate trajectory advantages so that intermediate recovery decisions do not indiscriminately inherit terminal outcomes. Across four repository-level software-engineering benchmarks, RIPPLE improves the base policy by an arithmetic mean of 4.8 score points, with no auxiliary critic at deployment. Controlled ablations isolate the benefits of local, stage-aligned credit, and matched-prefix interventions show that critique semantics affect subsequent correction. Recovery gains concentrate where the prefix contains sufficient diagnostic evidence and the repository state remains reversible within the remaining budget.
