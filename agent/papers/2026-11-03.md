## 1. Principled Human-AI Collaboration in Agentic Workflows / 代理工作流程中的有原则的人机协作

**Date**: 2026-11-01 | **arXiv**: [2611.01566v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01566v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01566v1)

**Categories**: stat.ML, cs.AI, cs.LG, stat.ME

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Organizations adopting AI face the challenge of deciding which outputs can be released without human review while meeting the quality standards of human experts. We develop a statistical framework for delegating tasks between experts and black-box generate-verify workflows, in which AI-generated answers are verified and revised over multiple rounds. Before deployment, human experts evaluate selected outputs, and their judgments are used to calibrate the verifier threshold and number of attempts. Our method selects the policy with the highest estimated automation rate among statistically approved candidates, without modifying the generator or verifier. We establish finite-sample control of the error rate among released outputs, characterize the expert evaluations sufficient to attain a target automation rate, and derive a minimax lower bound establishing rate optimality in the quality margin and required confidence. In a MultiHiertt financial question-answering case study, average held-out automation increases from 2.50% to 24.17% as expert evaluations accumulate, while released-output error rates remain around 1.20%. In contrast, heuristics that release answers after consistently high verifier scores either violate the 5% error tolerance or automate at most 1.30% of tasks. Synthetic experiments further examine these theoretical predictions under controlled settings.

采用人工智能的组织面临着决定哪些输出可以在没有人工审查的情况下发布，同时满足人类专家的质量标准的挑战。我们开发了一个统计框架，用于在专家和黑盒生成验证工作流程之间委派任务，其中人工智能生成的答案经过多轮验证和修改。在部署之前，人类专家评估选定的输出，他们的判断用于校准验证器阈值和尝试次数。我们的方法在统计批准的候选者中选择估计自动化率最高的策略，而不修改生成器或验证器。我们对发布的输出之间的错误率建立有限样本控制，表征足以达到目标自动化率的专家评估，并推导出在质量裕度和所需置信度中建立速率最优性的极小极大下界。在 MultiHiertt 金融问答案例研究中，随着专家评估的积累，平均保留自动化从 2.50% 增加到 24.17%，而发布输出错误率保持在 1.20% 左右。相比之下，在验证者得分持续较高后发布答案的启发式方法要么违反 5% 的容错率，要么最多自动化 1.30% 的任务。综合实验在受控设置下进一步检验这些理论预测。

</details>

## 2. OntoDoc: Automatically Generating Ontology Resource Descriptions / OntoDoc：自动生成本体资源描述

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.01276v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01276v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01276v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.IR

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Knowledge graphs and ontologies form the backbone of structured, machine-readable knowledge representation, yet ontology terms often lack natural language descriptions, limiting their accessibility and reuse. Existing ontology verbalization approaches tend to rely on rule-based or template-driven techniques that operate on strict, formal schema axioms, resulting in rigid verbalizations that fail to convey richer contextual semantics. We present OntoDoc, a multi-agent framework for automatically generating natural language descriptions for any Internationalized Resource Identifier (IRI) within a SPARQL endpoint or RDF file. OntoDoc operates through a four-stage pipeline: (1) a Database Context agent that adaptively explores the knowledge graph via SPARQL to collect semantically informative triples about the target IRI; (2) a Web Context agent that retrieves and re-ranks encyclopedic background knowledge from Wikipedia and the open web; (3) an Assumption Generation agent that synthesizes both contexts into a structured, evidence-grounded list of intermediate assumptions; and (4) a Description Generation agent that produces the final natural language description from the grounded assumptions. We evaluate OntoDoc on three knowledge graphs spanning biomedical, agricultural, and cross-domain settings, using both automatic scoring via Large Language Models (LLMs) and human evaluation with 11 annotators. OntoDoc consistently matches or exceeds a naive LLM baseline and a template-based ontology verbalizer in accuracy. Furthermore, it vastly outperforms both baselines in the completeness of the generated descriptions, delivering an average 12% improvement in completeness over the naive baseline across all domains, and improving completeness scores by as much as 2.8 points on a 5-point scale relative to the ontology verbalizer.

知识图和本体构成了结构化、机器可读知识表示的支柱，但本体术语通常缺乏自然语言描述，限制了它们的可访问性和重用。现有的本体语言化方法往往依赖于基于规则或模板驱动的技术，这些技术在严格的、形式化的模式公理上运行，导致僵化的语言化，无法传达更丰富的上下文语义。我们推出了 OntoDoc，这是一个多代理框架，用于自动为 SPARQL 端点或 RDF 文件中的任何国际化资源标识符 (IRI) 生成自然语言描述。 OntoDoc 通过四阶段管道进行操作：(1) 数据库上下文代理，通过 SPARQL 自适应地探索知识图，以收集有关目标 IRI 的语义信息三元组； (2) Web Context 代理，用于从维基百科和开放网络检索百科全书式背景知识并重新排序； (3) 假设生成代理，将两种上下文合成为结构化的、基于证据的中间假设列表； (4) 描述生成代理，根据有根据的假设生成最终的自然语言描述。我们使用大型语言模型 (LLM) 的自动评分和 11 个注释器的人工评估，在涵盖生物医学、农业和跨领域设置的三个知识图上评估 OntoDoc。 OntoDoc 在准确性方面始终匹配或超过朴素的 LLM 基线和基于模板的本体语言描述器。此外，它在生成描述的完整性方面远远优于两个基线，在所有领域的完整性方面比朴素基线平均提高了 12%，并且相对于本体语言描述器（5 分制），完整性分数提高了 2.8 分。

</details>

## 3. Jet-QSparse: Accelerating Agentic LLM Inference with 4-Bit Sparse Attention / Jet-QSparse：利用 4 位稀疏注意力加速代理 LLM 推理

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.01022v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01022v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01022v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Long-horizon reasoning and agentic workloads strain KV-cache capacity and decode attention throughput. We present Jet-QSparse, a training-free method that combines compact NVFP4 cache storage with native FP4 query-key computation on Blackwell tensor cores. A dense scan scores the full key history and selects 128-token blocks for sparse FP16 value aggregation with FP32 accumulation, preserving higher precision in the output-forming step while enabling contiguous tile loads. Calibrated key centering and at most two BF16 pages per sequence and layer address quantization error; FP8 prefill uses a temporary arena while the persistent cache is packed directly from BF16 activations. Across four hybrid LLMs, a budget of 128 blocks per KV head shows no detected accuracy loss on the evaluated mathematical reasoning, coding, and agentic benchmarks, although some smaller budgets incur losses. With 64 blocks, Qwen3.8-27B achieves a $1.94\times$ end-to-end decode speedup over dense BF16 at 128K context and batch size 16 on one GB300, and the measured Qwen KV pools provide $2.58$-$2.76\times$ BF16's token capacity.

长视野推理和代理工作负载对 KV 缓存容量和解码注意力吞吐量造成压力。我们提出了 Jet-QSparse，这是一种免训练方法，它将紧凑的 NVFP4 缓存存储与 Blackwell 张量核心上的本机 FP4 查询键计算相结合。密集扫描对完整的密钥历史记录进行评分，并选择 128 个令牌块进行稀疏 FP16 值聚合和 FP32 累积，从而在输出形成步骤中保持更高的精度，同时启用连续的切片加载。校准键居中，每个序列最多两个 BF16 页和层地址量化误差； FP8 预填充使用临时区域，而持久缓存则直接从 BF16 激活中打包。在四个混合 LLM 中，每个 KV 头 128 个块的预算显示在评估的数学推理、编码和代理基准上没有检测到准确性损失，尽管一些较小的预算会产生损失。凭借 64 个块，Qwen3.8-27B 在 1 GB300 上以 128K 上下文和批量大小 16 的方式实现了比密集 BF16 1.94\times$ 的端到端解码加速，并且测量的 Qwen KV 池提供 2.58$-$2.76\times$ BF16 的代币容量。

## 4. GURU: A Hierarchical Neuro-Symbolic Multi-Agent System for Verification-Aware Automation of Mission-Critical Digital Engineering and Operations / GURU：一种分层神经符号多代理系统，用于关键任务数字工程和运营的验证感知自动化

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.01001v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01001v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01001v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CE, cs.MA, eess.SY

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

An AI can find a safe action faster than any human, but it must never decide what counts as safe. We present GURU, a hierarchical neuro-symbolic multi-agent system for mission-critical digital engineering and operations, built on one partition of authority: learned models propose, rank, and accelerate; a symbolic rules engine checks every consequential action against hard limits before it executes; and validated physics confirms and decides. GURU runs a workflow as a closed control loop, with Navigation, Action, and Planning agents sharing one Common Internal Language and one training factory. Nuclear, aviation, and national-security guidance now requires this pattern; a harnessed language model keeps the model at the center, GURU puts a validated reference there. In computational fluid dynamics, the workflow raised the converged-simulation rate on unseen aircraft geometries from under 1 percent to 74 percent over 100 single-attempt cases, ran 54 live cases to Mach 6 on three facilities, and an ablation put the symbolic layer at 13 points of boundary-layer capture. Knowledge-graph retrieval and role adapters raised ParaView control success from 55 to 75 percent. In space domain awareness, where a language model cannot be trusted to decide, a frozen self-supervised world model under validated propagation scored highest of five methods at conjunction risk, including fully fine-tuned language models, with several-times-lower seed variance: the trustworthy design gives up nothing in accuracy, and the recipe transferred to reactor anomaly detection. A radar-to-avoidance debris pipeline was demonstrated in stages on a 2026 reentry; the hierarchy coordinates simulated teams of 200 aircraft. Training scaled near-linearly past 1,000 Aurora nodes and ran at 8,000. The approach extends to a control-grade microreactor digital twin with validated physics, not a learned model, as the safety basis.

人工智能可以比任何人类更快地找到安全的行动，但它永远不能决定什么才是安全的。我们提出了 GURU，这是一种用于关键任务数字工程和操作的分层神经符号多智能体系统，它建立在一个权限划分上：学习模型提出、排序和加速；符号规则引擎在执行之前检查每个后续操作是否符合硬限制；并经过验证的物理学确认并决定。 GURU 将工作流程作为闭环控制运行，导航、行动和规划代理共享一种通用内部语言和一个培训工厂。核、航空和国家安全指导现在需要这种模式；利用的语言模型将模型置于中心位置，GURU 在那里放置经过验证的参考。在计算流体动力学方面，该工作流程在 100 个单次尝试案例中将未见过的飞机几何形状的聚合模拟率从不到 1% 提高到 74%，在三个设施上以 6 马赫的速度运行了 54 个实际案例，并且消融将符号层置于边界层捕获的 13 个点处。知识图检索和角色适配器将 ParaView 控制成功率从 55% 提高到 75%。在空间领域意识中，语言模型不能被信任来做出决定，经过验证的传播下的冻结自我监督世界模型在联合风险的五种方法中得分最高，包括完全微调的语言模型，其种子方差要低几倍：值得信赖的设计在准确性上不会放弃任何东西，并且配方转移到反应堆异常检测。 2026 年再入时分阶段演示了雷达躲避碎片管道；该层次结构协调由 200 架飞机组成的模拟团队。训练规模近乎线性地超过 1,000 个 Aurora 节点，并以 8,000 个节点运行。该方法扩展到控制级微反应器数字孪生，以经过验证的物理原理（而不是学习模型）作为安全基础。

## 5. Illusion: Safeguarding Topology Confidentiality in LLM Multi-Agent Systems via Behavior-Preserving Obfuscation / 错觉：通过行为保护混淆保护 LLM 多代理系统中的拓扑机密性

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.00923v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00923v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.00923v1)

**Categories**: cs.CR, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

The communication topology of Large Language Model Multi-Agent Systems (LLM-MASs) governs how information propagates among agents and encodes critical domain knowledge and expert workflow design. It is valuable intellectual property and plays a decisive role in system utility and safety, yet remains vulnerable to inference attacks that stealthily reconstruct it from agent outputs. Existing defenses detect or mitigate such attacks but do not proactively conceal the topology itself. We propose \textit{Illusion}, a proactive topology obfuscation defense for LLM-MAS. \textit{Illusion} injects fake edges and fake messages to present attackers with a deceptive topology, while selecting low-impact candidates that preserve output-distribution equivalence and task-accuracy non-inferiority. The resulting fake edges are designed to be indistinguishable from genuine messages under black-box observation. Extensive experiments show that \textit{Illusion} reduces edge-level F1 from over 0.87 to 0.62 and graph similarity from over 0.94 to 0.71. It also largely preserves output distributions and task performance, and its fake edges resist both similarity-based and LLM-judge filtering, even under adaptive pruning.

大型语言模型多代理系统 (LLM-MAS) 的通信拓扑控制着信息如何在代理之间传播以及如何对关键领域知识和专家工作流程设计进行编码。它是有价值的知识产权，在系统实用性和安全性方面发挥着决定性作用，但仍然容易受到从代理输出中秘密重建它的推理攻击。现有的防御措施可以检测或减轻此类攻击，但不会主动隐藏拓扑本身。我们提出 \textit{Illusion}，一种针对 LLM-MAS 的主动拓扑混淆防御。 \textit{Illusion} 注入虚假边缘和虚假消息，向攻击者呈现欺骗性拓扑，同时选择保持输出分布等效性和任务准确性非劣性的低影响候选者。由此产生的假边缘被设计为在黑盒观察下与真实消息无法区分。大量实验表明，\textit{Illusion} 将边缘级别 F1 从 0.87 以上降低到 0.62，并将图形相似性从 0.94 以上降低到 0.71。它还在很大程度上保留了输出分布和任务性能，并且即使在自适应剪枝下，它的假边缘也能抵抗基于相似性和 LLM 判断的过滤。

## 6. When 200 OK Is Not an Answer: Silent Fetch Failures in Agentic Web Content Acquisition / 当 200 OK 不是答案时：代理 Web 内容获取中的静默获取失败

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.00884v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00884v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.00884v1)

**Categories**: cs.IR, cs.CL, cs.NI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Web agents and answer engines fetch pages at question time, and liveness checks of cited sources treat an HTTP 200 response as success. We measure how often that assumption fails and what it does to measurements built on fetched pages. We fetched 10,307 URLs in three days with a polite static client and a rendered browser client: pages from 1,507 sites in five strata, random non-existent paths on every site, and the 4,795 URLs cited by expert-verified claims in ExpertQA. We define a taxonomy of what a 200 response contains, label the static 200 responses automatically, and check the labels against 300 items reviewed by an LLM-based assistant; a human check of 60 of them retained every reviewed usability label after discussion. By the automatic labels, 20.9% of the pages that a static client receives with status 200 do not contain the page's main content: app shells, bot challenges, block pages, error and placeholder pages, and partial content. On the held-out test split, the reviewed sample lowers this estimate from 25.5% to 20.0%. 13.1% of the sites whose random-path probe was answered returned 200. The error propagates: for the cited sources, treating 200 as content overstates the share of claim-source pairs that can be checked against live content by 12.2 points and inflates the apparent silent citation-drift rate from 5.8% to 21.9%. Cheap heuristics catch most of it. Text length, probe comparison and redirect-to-root, combined and tuned on held-out sites, recover 86% of unusable reviewed responses at a false-alarm rate near 5%. Content-reading detectors perform comparably: the best, the text classifier, gains five to seven points of recall at more than twice the false-alarm rate. We argue that agents and their evaluations should report fetch outcomes explicitly instead of inferring them from the status line and will release the labelled data and code.

Web 代理和应答引擎在提问时获取页面，引用来源的活跃度检查将 HTTP 200 响应视为成功。我们测量该假设失败的频率以及它对基于获取的页面构建的测量的影响。我们在三天内使用礼貌的静态客户端和渲染的浏览器客户端获取了 10,307 个 URL：来自五个分层的 1,507 个站点的页面、每个站点上随机不存在的路径以及 ExpertQA 中经过专家验证的声明引用的 4,795 个 URL。我们定义了 200 个响应包含的内容的分类法，自动标记静态 200 个响应，并根据法学硕士助理审查的 300 个项目检查标签；经过人工检查，其中 60 个在讨论后保留了所有经过审查的可用性标签。通过自动标签，静态客户端收到的状态为 200 的页面中有 20.9% 不包含页面的主要内容：应用程序 shell、机器人挑战、阻止页面、错误和占位符页面以及部分内容。在保留的测试中，经过审查的样本将该估计值从 25.5% 降低到 20.0%。随机路径探测得到答复的网站中有 13.1% 返回了 200。错误会传播：对于引用来源，将 200 视为内容会夸大可根据实时内容进行检查的声明来源对的份额 12.2 个点，并将明显的沉默引用漂移率从 5.8% 夸大到 21.9%。廉价的启发法抓住了大部分。文本长度、探测比较和重定向到根，在保留的站点上进行组合和调整，以接近 5% 的误报率恢复 86% 的不可用审核响应。内容阅读检测器的性能相当：最好的文本分类器以两倍以上的误报率获得 5 到 7 个召回点。我们认为代理及其评估应该明确报告获取结果，而不是从状态行推断结果，并将发布标记的数据和代码。

</details>
