## 1. Track4D: Representing Dense 3D Tracking for Video Diffusion Models / Track4D：代表视频扩散模型的密集 3D 跟踪

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15287v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15287v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15287v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

**Project**: https://nirvanalan.github.io/projects/track4d  <details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We study how to repurpose video diffusion models (VDMs), pretrained on millions of videos, for dense 3D point tracking, where training data is scarce. Doing so is non-trivial, as it requires representing 3D tracks as video-like signals that may lie outside the natural-video distribution on which the VDM was trained. We thus systematically assess the effectiveness of two such video representations. The first, Residual 3D Tracking Video (RTV), encodes per-pixel 3D offsets relative to a query frame and allows recovery of 3D trajectories directly from the generated video. The second, Normalized Coordinate Map Video (NCMV), labels each pixel with a canonical coordinate invariant through time. This is closer to a natural video and easier for the VDM to learn, but requires additional post-processing with an off-the-shelf 2D tracker to recover 3D tracks. RTV tracks more accurately, while NCMV shows that a VDM can generate dense correspondence as a video. We instantiate both representations with LoRA adaptation of a frozen Wan backbone, conditioned on point maps from an off-the-shelf 3D reconstructor as a geometric scaffold. Trained with a single dynamic 3D tracking dataset, Track4D achieves competitive zero-shot tracking on four real and synthetic benchmarks. A regression readout that bypasses the video VAE and uses additional training data brings further improvement, demonstrating the potential of this direction for dense 3D tracking. We hope our analysis will inspire future work that leverages VDM priors for dense 3D tracking and 4D reconstruction. Project page: https://nirvanalan.github.io/projects/track4d

我们研究如何重新利用在数百万个视频上进行预训练的视频扩散模型 (VDM)，以在训练数据稀缺的情况下进行密集的 3D 点跟踪。这样做并不简单，因为它需要将 3D 轨道表示为类似视频的信号，这些信号可能位于训练 VDM 的自然视频分布之外。因此，我们系统地评估了两个此类视频表示的有效性。第一个是残差 3D 跟踪视频 (RTV)，对相对于查询帧的每像素 3D 偏移进行编码，并允许直接从生成的视频恢复 3D 轨迹。第二种是标准化坐标图视频 (NCMV)，它使用随时间变化的规范坐标来标记每个像素。这更接近自然视频，并且更容易让 VDM 学习，但需要使用现成的 2D 跟踪器进行额外的后处理才能恢复 3D 轨道。 RTV 跟踪更准确，而 NCMV 表明 VDM 可以生成密集的对应关系作为视频。我们通过 LoRA 适应冻结的 Wan 主干来实例化这两种表示，以来自现成 3D 重建器的点图作为几何支架。 Track4D 使用单个动态 3D 跟踪数据集进行训练，在四个真实和合成基准上实现了有竞争力的零样本跟踪。绕过视频 VAE 并使用额外训练数据的回归读数带来了进一步的改进，展示了该方向在密集 3D 跟踪方面的潜力。我们希望我们的分析能够激发未来利用 VDM 先验进行密集 3D 跟踪和 4D 重建的工作。项目页面：https://nirvanalan.github.io/projects/track4d

## 2. Can MLLMs Reason over Surround-View Driving Videos? A Multi-View Autonomous Driving QA Benchmark / MLLM 可以对环视驾驶视频进行推理吗？多视图自动驾驶 QA 基准

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15225v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15225v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15225v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Surround-view video understanding in autonomous driving is a crucial capability for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) to achieve reliable driving reasoning. Real-world driving often requires information beyond the front-view camera. Existing MLLMs primarily focus on front-view information, while their spatio-temporal understanding and reasoning abilities remain unknown. To address this gap, we propose MVDriveBench, a high-information-density video QA benchmark for autonomous driving, characterized by synchronized surround-view inputs, spatio-temporal understanding, and reasoning-chain-oriented evaluation. We design 12 categories of QA tasks that cover the complete reasoning pipeline from low-level visual perception to high-level driving decision-making, enabling a systematic evaluation of MLLMs surround-view spatio-temporal understanding and reasoning capabilities in complex driving environments. We conduct a comprehensive evaluation of 23 open-source, closed-source, and expert MLLMs on MVDriveBench. The results show that current MLLMs still exhibit insufficient surround-view spatio-temporal reasoning ability. Meanwhile, closed-loop evaluations of specialist models in AlpaSim reveal a correlation between surround-view spatio-temporal reasoning capabilities and closed-loop driving performance, highlighting the importance of comprehensive multi-view understanding for reliable vehicle control.

自动驾驶中的环视视频理解是多模态大语言模型（MLLM）实现可靠驾驶推理的关键能力。现实世界的驾驶通常需要前视摄像头之外的信息。现有的 MLLM 主要关注前视信息，而其时空理解和推理能力仍然未知。为了解决这一差距，我们提出了 MVDriveBench，这是一种用于自动驾驶的高信息密度视频 QA 基准，其特点是同步环视输入、时空理解和面向推理链的评估。我们设计了 12 类 QA 任务，涵盖了从低级视觉感知到高级驾驶决策的完整推理流程，从而能够系统评估 MLLM 在复杂驾驶环境中的环视时空理解和推理能力。我们在 MVDriveBench 上对 23 个开源、闭源和专家 MLLM 进行了全面评估。结果表明，当前的MLLM仍然表现出不足的环视时空推理能力。同时，AlpaSim中专业模型的闭环评估揭示了环视时空推理能力与闭环驾驶性能之间的相关性，凸显了全面的多视图理解对于可靠车辆控制的重要性。

## 3. CineMME: From Dialogue Grounding to Narrative Reasoning in Multimodal Video Understanding / CineMME：多模态视频理解中从对话基础到叙事推理

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15161v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15161v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15161v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Narrative video understanding requires more than recognizing visible events: models must extract evidence from dialogue, bind it to speakers, and use it to infer narrative meaning. Existing video benchmarks evaluate aspects of this problem, including video question answering, audio-visual reasoning, and temporal grounding, but rarely test whether these abilities form a coherent dialogue-to-plot evidence chain within the same clips. We introduce \textbf{CineMME}, a human-verified benchmark designed to diagnose dialogue-grounded plot understanding, supported by approximately $1,600$ expert person-hours of annotation. CineMME contains $975$ curated short narrative video clips and two linked tracks: \textit{CineMME-Grounding}, with $13,554$ timestamped speech segments and $33,083$ speaker face boxes for temporal dialogue localization, transcription, and active-speaker grounding; and \textit{CineMME-Reasoning}, with $1,600$ multiple-choice and $316$ open-ended questions spanning perception, audio, narrative, social, and scene-presentation understanding. Our main evaluation compares $20$ MLLMs on reasoning and $9$ omni-modal models on grounding. The results reveal a pronounced who-what-when decoupling: Gemini-3 Pro localizes dialogue in time ($68.34\%$ tIoU) and transcribes it with a WER of $28.99\%$, yet achieves only $7.74\%$ vIoU for active-speaker grounding while achieving $67.66\%$ MCQ accuracy on questions with extractable answers. Modality ablations further show that vision is not uniformly beneficial: it improves performance on perception-heavy questions but can hurt dialogue-centric reasoning. CineMME provides a compact diagnostic testbed for evaluating how multimodal video models connect dialogue, speakers, visual evidence, and narrative meaning.

叙事视频理解需要的不仅仅是识别可见事件：模型必须从对话中提取证据，将其与说话者绑定，并用它来推断叙事意义。现有的视频基准评估了该问题的各个方面，包括视频问答、视听推理和时间基础，但很少测试这些能力是否在同一剪辑中形成连贯的对话到情节证据链。我们推出 \textbf{CineMME}，这是一个经过人工验证的基准，旨在诊断基于对话的情节理解，并由大约 1,600 美元的专家工时注释支持。 CineMME 包含价值 975 美元的精心策划的短叙事视频剪辑和两个链接轨道：\textit{CineMME-Grounding}，其中包含价值 13,554 美元的带时间戳的语音片段和价值 33,083 美元的说话者面部框，用于时间对话定位、转录和主动说话者接地；和 \textit{CineMME-Reasoning}，包含 1,600 美元的多项选择题和 316 美元的开放式问题，涵盖感知、音频、叙事、社交和场景呈现理解。我们的主要评估比较了 20 美元的 MLLM 的推理能力和 9 美元的全模态模型的接地能力。结果揭示了明显的“谁-什么-何时”解耦：Gemini-3 Pro 及时定位对话 ($68.34\%$ tIoU)，并以 $28.99\%$ 的 WER 进行转录，但主动发言者接地仅获得 $7.74\%$ vIoU，同时在可提取答案的问题上实现 $67.66\%$ MCQ 准确度。模态消融进一步表明，视觉并不总是有益的：它提高了感知重问题的表现，但可能损害以对话为中心的推理。 CineMME 提供了一个紧凑的诊断测试台，用于评估多模态视频模型如何连接对话、扬声器、视觉证据和叙述意义。

## 4. From Concepts to Interactions: Dynamic Graph Semantic Recomposition for Safe Visual Generation / 从概念到交互：用于安全视觉生成的动态图语义重组

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15149v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15149v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15149v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent advances in flow-matching Transformers have greatly improved Text-to-Image (T2I) and Text-to-Video (T2V) generation, but unconstrained generation risks producing illegal or unethical content. Safety alignment must therefore suppress unsafe content while preserving legitimate generation. A concept's safety depends on context: a knife used for cooking is safe, whereas its use in violence is harmful. Yet most concept erasure methods indiscriminately suppress static, isolated representations, overlooking evolving semantic interactions. Consequently, safe scenes containing potentially sensitive terms may be unintentionally suppressed, compromising generation quality. To address this, we propose Dynamic Graph Semantic Recomposition (DGSR), which dynamically constructs and updates a semantic interaction graph from evolving text visual states during sampling. Context aware graph reasoning captures semantic dependencies, while paired safe/unsafe supervision distinguishes harmful from legitimate interactions, enabling precise identification of and selective intervention on high risk semantic relationships while preserving benign semantics. DGSR freezes the pretrained backbone and supports both image and video generation. Experiments show that DGSR achieves the lowest Attack Success Rate (ASR) in 10 of 12 T2I evaluations and reduces the average T2V unsafe frame rate by 41.2\% relative to the strongest baseline, while maintaining high generation fidelity and temporal consistency.

流匹配 Transformer 的最新进展极大地改进了文本到图像 (T2I) 和文本到视频 (T2V) 的生成，但不受约束的生成存在产生非法或不道德内容的风险。因此，安全对齐必须抑制不安全内容，同时保留合法生成。概念的安全性取决于上下文：用于烹饪的刀是安全的，而用于暴力的刀则是有害的。然而，大多数概念擦除方法不加区别地抑制静态、孤立的表示，忽视不断发展的语义交互。因此，包含潜在敏感术语的安全场景可能会被无意中抑制，从而影响生成质量。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了动态图语义重组（DGSR），它根据采样过程中不断变化的文本视觉状态动态构建和更新语义交互图。上下文感知图推理捕获语义依赖性，而配对安全/不安全监督区分有害交互和合法交互，从而能够精确识别和选择性干预高风险语义关系，同时保留良性语义。 DGSR 冻结预训练的主干并支持图像和视频生成。实验表明，DGSR 在 12 次 T2I 评估中的 10 次中实现了最低的攻击成功率 (ASR)，并将平均 T2V 不安全帧率相对于最强基线降低了 41.2%，同时保持了高生成保真度和时间一致性。

## 5. From Seeing to Remembering: Brain-Informed Agentic Text-to-Video Generation for Enhancing Memorability / 从看到到记住：大脑知情的代理文本到视频生成以增强记忆力

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15107v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15107v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15107v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Generating videos that faithfully convey intended semantics while remaining memorable to viewers is valuable for applications such as advertising, education, and storytelling. However, memorability-oriented text-to-video generation remains underexplored, as human memory involves complex neural processes that are difficult to model and translate into generation guidance. In this paper, we propose MemCrafter, brain-informed approach that enhances video memorability through iteratively agentic prompt refinement. The agentic framework contains two key components. First, we construct a cortical region-based neuro-memorability signal that predicts video memorability from estimated neural responses, providing both an optimization objective and interpretable region-level information. Second, we develop a sensitivity-aware feedback module, motivated by a conceptual sensitivity analysis of the optimization objective, to derive actionable guidance for prompt refinement while preserving semantic fidelity. Extensive experiments demonstrate that MemCrafter consistently improves predicted video memorability while maintaining semantic alignment and perceptual quality.

生成既忠实传达预期语义又让观众难忘的视频对于广告、教育和讲故事等应用很有价值。然而，面向记忆的文本到视频的生成仍未得到充分探索，因为人类记忆涉及复杂的神经过程，难以建模并转化为生成指导。在本文中，我们提出了 MemCrafter，一种大脑知情方法，通过迭代代理提示细化来增强视频记忆力。代理框架包含两个关键组件。首先，我们构建了一个基于皮质区域的神经记忆信号，该信号根据估计的神经反应预测视频记忆能力，提供优化目标和可解释的区域级信息。其次，我们开发了一个敏感度感知反馈模块，在优化目标的概念敏感度分析的推动下，得出可操作的指导，以在保持语义保真度的同时进行快速细化。大量实验表明，MemCrafter 能够持续提高预测视频的记忆力，同时保持语义对齐和感知质量。

## 6. BirdDreams: Joint Perspective and BEV Video Generation for a Plausible, Reactive Driving World Model / BirdDreams：联合视角和 BEV 视频生成，打造合理的反应式驾驶世界模型

**Date**: 2026-11-10 | **arXiv**: [2611.14815v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14815v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14815v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Driving world models aim to simulate how a scene unfolds under different ego actions. This requires plausible motion of surrounding vehicles as well as precise ego-trajectory control. Learning vehicle motion from perspective video alone is challenging because motion is entangled with appearance and vehicles can be occluded. Prior work therefore conditions video generation on layouts that specify vehicle motion, providing control over surrounding vehicles but requiring their future trajectories as inputs. We introduce BirdDreams, an autoregressive driving world model that jointly generates perspective video and a schematic bird's-eye-view (BEV) video, making scene layout part of the predicted future. The BEV, rendered from scene annotations for training, depicts roads and vehicles as simple shapes at a fixed metric scale, providing a top-down view of their positions and motion with reduced occlusion between vehicles. Perspective video, by contrast, retains scene details that the BEV omits, such as traffic signals and object appearance. Joint attention between two video diffusion transformers, one per stream, lets each stream inform the other during generation. The predicted BEV also provides a geometric interface for ego control: we translate and rotate its latest frame along an input ego trajectory and use the resulting frames as a soft condition that shows how the scene would appear to move if only the ego vehicle moved. At inference, BirdDreams generates both videos from a single camera image without requiring an HD map or vehicle-state inputs. On PhysicalAI-AV and nuScenes, BirdDreams follows input ego trajectories more faithfully than prior driving world models and generates more plausible ego motion without ego-action conditioning. Human evaluation shows that BirdDreams outperforms a perspective-only baseline in generating realistic surrounding-vehicle behavior.

驾驶世界模型旨在模拟场景在不同的自我行为下如何展开。这需要周围车辆的合理运动以及精确的自我轨迹控制。仅从透视视频学习车辆运动具有挑战性，因为运动与外观纠缠在一起，并且车辆可能被遮挡。因此，之前的工作将视频生成置于指定车辆运动的布局上，提供对周围车辆的控制，但需要它们未来的轨迹作为输入。我们引入了 BirdDreams，这是一种自回归驾驶世界模型，可联合生成透视视频和示意性鸟瞰 (BEV) 视频，使场景布局成为预测未来的一部分。 BEV 根据训练场景注释进行渲染，将道路和车辆描绘为固定公制比例的简单形状，提供其位置和运动的自上而下视图，同时减少车辆之间的遮挡。相比之下，透视视频保留了 BEV 忽略的场景细节，例如交通信号和物体外观。两个视频扩散变压器（每个流一个）之间的联合关注让每个流在生成过程中通知另一个流。预测的 BEV 还为自我控制提供了一个几何接口：我们沿着输入的自我轨迹平移和旋转其最新帧，并将生成的帧用作软条件，以显示如果仅自我车辆移动，场景将如何移动。据推断，BirdDreams 从单个摄像机图像生成两个视频，无需高清地图或车辆状态输入。在PhysicalAI-AV和nuScenes上，BirdDreams比之前的驾驶世界模型更忠实地遵循输入的自我轨迹，并且在没有自我动作调节的情况下生成更合理的自我运动。人类评估表明，BirdDreams 在生成真实的周围车辆行为方面优于仅透视基线。

</details>
