## 1. Unlocking Cross-Attention in Label-Scarce Regimes: A Weakly Supervised Approach for Video Semantic Segmentation / 在标签稀缺的体系中解锁交叉注意力：一种用于视频语义分割的弱监督方法

**Date**: 2026-11-13 | **arXiv**: [2611.18324v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18324v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18324v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Weakly Supervised Video Semantic Segmentation (WS-VSS) aims to learn temporally consistent semantic object masks in videos using only video-level labels. While fully supervised models successfully learn temporal relationships by means of cross-attention, training these temporal weights under weak supervision is unstable and even pushes the model below its single-frame baseline. Therefore, existing WS-VSS methods exploit temporal consistency by relying on external priors, such as optical flow. Unfortunately these cues are non-semantic, computationally expensive and noisy, degrading the supervisory signal. Instead of supervising attention weights directly or using external priors, we propose MemCAM, a WS-VSS transformer framework that learns temporal cross-attention directly through feature-space self-supervision. MemCAM introduces a CAM Propagation Algorithm that synthesizes a supervisory signal by propagating past CAMs according to patch-level feature similarities in the latent space. Specifically, we constrain their induced CAMs through a consistency objective between predicted and propagated maps, stabilizing temporal attention learning. Experiments on the YouTube-Objects v2.2 and WS$^2$ datasets demonstrate that MemCAM consistently outperforms the temporal localization and segmentation performance of WS-VSS alternatives, showing that cross-attention can be effectively trained in label-scarce video settings through internal self-supervision.

弱监督视频语义分割（WS-VSS）旨在仅使用视频级标签来学习视频中时间上一致的语义对象掩码。虽然全监督模型通过交叉注意力成功地学习了时间关系，但在弱监督下训练这些时间权重并不稳定，甚至会将模型推至其单帧基线以下。因此，现有的 WS-VSS 方法通过依赖外部先验（如光流）来利用时间一致性。不幸的是，这些线索是非语义的、计算成本高且存在噪声，从而使监督信号降级。我们提出了 MemCAM，这是一个 WS-VSS Transformer 框架，它不是直接监督注意力权重或使用外部先验，而是通过特征空间自监督直接学习时间交叉注意力。MemCAM引入了一种CAM传播算法，该算法根据潜在空间中块级特征的相似性传播过去的CAM，从而合成监督信号。具体而言，我们通过预测图和传播图之间的一致性目标来约束其诱导的CAM，从而稳定时间注意力学习。在 YouTube-Objects v2.2 和 WS$^2$ 数据集上进行的实验表明，MemCAM 在时间定位和分割性能方面始终优于 WS-VSS 的替代方案，这表明在标签稀缺的视频环境中，可以通过内部自监督有效地训练交叉注意力。

## 2. TelePhysica: Agentic Reconstruction of Physically Interactive Scenes / TelePhysica：物理交互场景的智能体重建

**Date**: 2026-11-13 | **arXiv**: [2611.18078v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18078v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18078v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Direct image-to-video diffusion models entangle pixel synthesis with implicit latent motion, precluding persistent 3D mechanical equilibrium, mid-rollout intervention, and counterfactual force control. Conversely, single-pass programmatic 3D reconstruction from an uncalibrated photograph leaves monocular depth and support clearance underconstrained, causing reconstructed scenes to interpenetrate or collapse under gravity. We present TelePhysica, a closed-loop framework that reconstructs an executable, multi-regime physical world from a single photograph to drive stateful 3D interaction and trajectory-grounded video synthesis. A vision-language coding agent synthesizes separable visual meshes and declarative physical models within a two-stage verification loop that pairs pixel-aligned silhouette critique with a zero-force gravitational equilibrium preflight. The verified world compiles into a unified solver coupling rigid bodies, articulated chains, co-dimensional cloth shells, and 3D tetrahedral elastodynamics, whose simulated rollouts condition pretrained video diffusion models through pixel-aligned structural streams. On a 100-scene single-image benchmark, closed-loop execution raises equilibrium-verified world recovery from $0\%$ in single-pass synthesis to $81\%$. Controlled constitutive ablations show over an order-of-magnitude larger localized corner displacement and out-of-plane pleating in finite-element cloth than in matched rigid-plate proxies. Conditioning video diffusion on simulated trajectories increases dynamic motion-mask overlap by $2{\times}\text{--}5{\times}$ and preserves motion direction under counterfactual force reversals where direct image-to-video models fail.

直接的图像到视频扩散模型将像素合成与隐式潜在运动纠缠在一起，从而排除了持续的3D机械平衡、展开过程中的干预以及反事实力控制。相反，从未校准的照片进行单次编程式3D重建，会使单目深度和支撑间隙受到欠约束，导致重建的场景相互穿透或在重力作用下坍塌。我们推出了TelePhysica，这是一个闭环框架，能够从一张照片中重建一个可执行的多机制物理世界，以驱动有状态的3D交互和基于轨迹的视频合成。视觉-语言编码代理在一个两阶段验证循环中合成可分离的视觉网格和声明式物理模型，该循环将像素对齐的轮廓评判与零力重力平衡预检相结合。经过验证的世界被编译成一个统一的求解器，将刚体、铰接链、同维布料壳和3D四面体弹性动力学耦合在一起，其模拟的展开过程通过像素对齐的结构流对预训练的视频扩散模型进行条件化。在包含100个场景的单幅图像基准测试中，闭环执行将平衡性经过验证的世界恢复率从单次合成的$0\%$提高到$81\%$。受控的本构消融显示，与匹配的刚性板代理相比，有限元布料的局部角位移和面外褶皱大了一个数量级。条件视频
