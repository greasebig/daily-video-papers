## 1. Images Talk Back: Guiding MMDiTs by Suppressing Image-to-Text Feedback / 图像回话：通过抑制图像到文本的反馈来指导 MMDiT

**Date**: 2026-10-09 | **arXiv**: [2610.13941v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13941v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13941v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

**Code**: https://github.com/yyc1215lll-creator/SFG.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Multimodal diffusion transformers (MMDiTs) allow the evolving image to update internal text representations through joint attention. These representations then guide subsequent image updates, raising a question: does this feedback help correct deviations from the prompt, or preserve them? We find that image-to-text (I2T) feedback can preserve the objects and attributes already represented in the evolving image, including errors. Transplanting I2T messages can rescue failures or disrupt successes. At matched intervention strengths, suppressing the influence of I2T feedback improves generation, whereas opposite controls degrade it. These findings motivate Semantic Feedback Guidance (SFG), which suppresses the influence of I2T feedback by guiding away from an auxiliary branch that strengthens this feedback and weakens text-to-image conditioning. The in-place variant, iSFG, directly suppresses I2T feedback and strengthens text-to-image conditioning, requiring no extra model evaluations. Temporal interventions motivate running the SFG branch during the first 20% of image sampling steps, requiring about $1.2\times$ the base NFE without classifier-free guidance (CFG). Experiments across three image-generation backbones show gains in alignment and preference-model scores. On FLUX, adding SFG to CFG raises GenEval from $0.6661$ to $0.7479$; on SD3.5M, iSFG adds $+1.33$ HPSv2.1 over CFG at zero extra NFE. The control also transfers to text-to-video and image-to-video generation, improving VBench scores. Code is available at https://github.com/yyc1215lll-creator/SFG.

多模态扩散变换器（MMDiT）允许不断演变的图像通过联合注意力来更新内部文本表示。然后，这些表示指导后续的图像更新，并提出一个问题：这种反馈是否有助于纠正与提示的偏差，或保留它们？我们发现图像到文本（I2T）反馈可以保留不断演变的图像中已经表示的对象和属性，包括错误。移植 I2T 消息可以挽救失败或破坏成功。在匹配的干预强度下，抑制 I2T 反馈的影响可以改善生成，而相反的控制则会降低生成。这些发现激发了语义反馈引导（SFG），它通过引导远离加强这种反馈并削弱文本到图像调节的辅助分支来抑制 I2T 反馈的影响。就地变体 iSFG 直接抑制 I2T 反馈并增强文本到图像调节，无需额外的模型评估。时间干预会激励在图像采样步骤的前 20% 期间运行 SFG 分支，在没有无分类器指导 (CFG) 的情况下，需要大约 1.2 美元\乘以基本 NFE 的费用。跨三个图像生成主干的实验显示了对齐和偏好模型分数的提高。在 FLUX 上，将 SFG 添加到 CFG 中，GenEval 从 0.6661$ 提高到 0.7479$；在 SD3.5M 上，iSFG 在零额外 NFE 的情况下比 CFG 添加了 $+1​​.33$ HPSv2.1。该控件还可以转移到文本到视频和图像到视频的生成，从而提高 VBench 分数。代码可在 https://github.com/yyc1215lll-creator/SFG 获取。

## 2. HorizonNav: Geometry-Guided Video Diffusion for Anticipatory 3D Robot Navigation / Horizo​​nNav：用于预期 3D 机器人导航的几何引导视频扩散

**Date**: 2026-10-09 | **arXiv**: [2610.13849v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13849v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13849v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We present HorizonNav, a video diffusion-based framework for anticipatory robot navigation that transforms a single RGB image and a text prompt into a geometrically aligned representation of plausible surrounding 3D space. Given the initial observation and a textual description of the reconstruction objective, a vision-language model, guided by designed instructions, interprets the scene and constructs structured prompts for a video diffusion model. The resulting video sequence provides plausible future observations of the environment, which are subsequently processed using monocular ORB-SLAM3 for camera pose estimation and Video Depth Anything for dense depth prediction. Our central contribution is a geometry-guided depth alignment stage that combines the estimated camera poses with triangulated visual landmarks to constrain dense depth predictions across multiple views. This alignment improves the geometric consistency of the reconstructed 3D environment, addressing inconsistencies that temporal coherence of the generated video alone cannot resolve. The experimental evaluation of navigation in imagined 3D environment shows that across a 19-scene horizon study, planning-feasible scenes increase from 13/19 at P20 to 15/19 at P81, while successful RRT trials increase from 62/90 to 75/90. In a separate corridor study, 6 of 25 evaluable generated-camera trajectories violate the reconstructed collision constraints; RRT planner recovers feasible alternatives in 5 of those 6 cases. A larger archive contains 58 usable reconstructions from 67 attempted cases, of which 36 support a downstream plan and 22 do not, exposing reconstruction and free-space failure modes rather than hiding them without lidar. Across 15 corridor reconstructions, the mean reconstruction error is approximately 0.30 m at matched structural landmarks.

我们提出了 Horizo​​nNav，一种基于视频扩散的预期机器人导航框架，可将单个 RGB 图像和文本提示转换为合理的周围 3D 空间的几何对齐表示。给定重建目标的初始观察和文本描述，视觉语言模型在设计指令的指导下解释场景并为视频扩散模型构建结构化提示。生成的视频序列提供了未来对环境的合理观察，随后使用单目 ORB-SLAM3 进行处理以进行相机姿态估计，并使用 Video Depth Anything 进行密集深度预测。我们的核心贡献是几何引导的深度对齐阶段，它将估计的相机姿势与三角视觉地标相结合，以约束跨多个视图的密集深度预测。这种对齐提高了重建 3D 环境的几何一致性，解决了生成视频的时间一致性无法单独解决的不一致问题。想象的 3D 环境中导航的实验评估表明，在 19 个场景的地平线研究中，规划可行的场景从 P20 的 13/19 增加到 P81 的 15/19，而成功的 RRT 试验从 62/90 增加到 75/90。在一项单独的走廊研究中，25 个可评估的摄像机生成轨迹中有 6 个违反了重建的碰撞约束； RRT 规划者在这 6 种情况中的 5 种中恢复了可行的替代方案。更大的档案包含来自 67 个尝试案例的 58 个可用重建，其中 36 个支持下游计划，22 个不支持，暴露重建和自由空间故障模式，而不是在没有激光雷达的情况下隐藏它们。在 15 个走廊重建中，匹配结构地标处的平均重建误差约为 0.30 m。

## 3. SmolWAM: A Latent World-Action Model for Robotic Manipulation on Edge Devices / SmolWAM：边缘设备上机器人操作的潜在世界动作模型

**Date**: 2026-10-09 | **arXiv**: [2610.13740v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13740v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13740v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World-action models (WAMs) predict a robot's actions together with the observations those actions will produce. However, the strongest models rely on video diffusion transformers that are far too computationally expensive for resource-constrained edge devices. We present SmolWAM, a latent WAM that we derive from the 2B-parameter Cosmos Policy by keeping its pretrained trunk and rebuilding its inference interface: single-frame visual latents, cached text features that preserve the trunk's attention sinks, 12 of 28 transformer blocks, and 1,008 tokens from which actions and future visual latents are read out in one rectified-flow step. Trained in 3.6 GPU-hours, SmolWAM succeeds in 95.5% of 400 LIBERO episodes at 57 ms and 2.3 TFLOP per 16-action chunk with 2.4 GiB of GPU memory, 11x faster than its teacher with 18.5x fewer FLOPs and 3.2x less memory, and its world head still forecasts scene motion.

世界动作模型 (WAM) 预测机器人的动作以及这些动作将产生的观察结果。然而，最强大的模型依赖于视频扩散变压器，这对于资源受限的边缘设备来说计算成本太高。我们提出了 SmolWAM，一种潜在的 WAM，是从 2B 参数 Cosmos 策略中衍生出来的，通过保留其预训练的主干并重建其推理接口：单帧视觉潜在、保留主干注意力接收器的缓存文本特征、28 个变压器块中的 12 个以及 1,008 个令牌，在一个校正流步骤中可以从中读出动作和未来的视觉潜在。经过 3.6 个 GPU 小时的训练，SmolWAM 在 400 个 LIBERO 场景中成功完成了 95.5%，每 16 个动作块的耗时为 57 毫秒，GPU 内存为 2.3 TFLOP，比老师快 11 倍，FLOP 次数减少了 18.5 倍，内存减少了 3.2 倍，而且它的世界头仍然可以预测场景运动。

</details>

## 4. KVeil: First-Place Solution to ECCV 2026 LifeGenIP Challenge on Unlearnable Videos / KVeil：ECCV 2026 LifeGenIP 不可学习视频挑战赛的第一名解决方案

**Date**: 2026-10-09 | **arXiv**: [2610.13733v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13733v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13733v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

**Code**: https://github.com/AntigoneRandy/KVeil.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

The rapid development of personalized video generation has raised serious concerns over identity privacy, as a few facial videos of a person now suffice to reproduce their identity in synthetic videos. This report presents the first-place solutions to both phases of the ECCV 2026 LifeGenIP Challenge on Unlearnable Videos, which aims to protect facial videos from unauthorized customization under a limited and imperceptible perturbation budget. For Phase 1, we introduce Reference Capacity Collapse, a pair of complementary losses that degrade the information carried by the reference condition while keeping subsequent video representations dependent on it, together with a Motion-Guided EOT mechanism that improves robustness across unknown inference conditions. Phase 2 further considers tuning-based customization and unseen generative models. To address both threats within a single perturbation, we design a two-layer framework that protects the facial and background regions separately, constructing structure-aligned perturbations from a synthetic target identity in the former and strengthening the white-box disruption of Phase 1 in the latter. Our method achieves nearly perfect protection under white-box reference-based customization, and remains effective under other customization paradigms, various models, and temporal attacks. Both solutions are built upon our recent work LETHE. We hope our solutions can inspire future work on protecting facial videos against unauthorized customization. Our code is available at https://github.com/AntigoneRandy/KVeil.

个性化视频生成的快速发展引起了对身份隐私的严重关注，因为现在一个人的一些面部视频就足以在合成视频中重现其身份。本报告介绍了 ECCV 2026 LifeGenIP 不可学习视频挑战赛两个阶段的第一名解决方案，旨在在有限且难以察觉的扰动预算下保护面部视频免遭未经授权的定制。对于第一阶段，我们引入了参考容量崩溃，这是一对互补损失，可降低参考条件所携带的信息，同时保持后续视频表示依赖于它，以及运动引导 EOT 机制，可提高未知推理条件下的鲁棒性。第二阶段进一步考虑基于调优的定制和看不见的生成模型。为了解决单个扰动中的这两种威胁，我们设计了一个两层框架，分别保护面部和背景区域，从前者的合成目标身份构建结构对齐的扰动，并加强后者第一阶段的白盒破坏。我们的方法在基于白盒参考的定制下实现了近乎完美的保护，并且在其他定制范式、各种模型和时间攻击下仍然有效。这两种解决方案都是基于我们最近的工作 LETHE 构建的。我们希望我们的解决方案能够激发未来保护面部视频免遭未经授权定制的工作。我们的代码可在 https://github.com/AntigoneRandy/KVeil 获取。

## 5. PhysForge: Material-Controllable 3D Reconstruction with Separable Components / PhysForge：具有可分离组件的材料可控 3D 重建

**Date**: 2026-10-09 | **arXiv**: [2610.13681v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13681v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13681v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent advances in physical 3D generation have enabled visually faithful assets with simulatable mechanical behavior. However, existing methods provide limited control over material behavior beyond appearance-based inference and struggle to estimate physical properties from complex interactions between objects and semantic parts. Therefore, we present PhysForge, a Video Diffusion Model (VDM)-based framework for addressing these challenges, comprising two complementary modules for material-controllable physical fitting and componentlevel interaction modeling. The first, PhysFit, leverages material-conditioned VDM dynamics as references for physical-property fitting, introducing windowed fitting to focus on material-revealing interactions and Hallucinated Forcing to disentangle nonphysical motion hallucinated by the VDM from material responses. The second, PhysComp decomposes complex interactions into individual objects and semantic components, enabling their physical parameters to be optimized independently and flexibly composed for joint simulation. Experiments on PhysAssets and Google Scanned Objects demonstrate that, compared with existing methods, PhysForge provides stronger control over material-dependent physical responses while supporting component-level physical modeling for complex interactions. It achieves the best CLIP and physical controllability scores on both common and uncommon materials, with qualitative comparisons, component-level ablations, and a user study further supporting these improvements. Our project page is available at https://masterwanna.github.io/PhysForge.html .

物理 3D 生成的最新进展使视觉上忠实的资产具有可模拟的机械行为。然而，现有方法对超出基于外观的推理的材料行为提供有限的控制，并且难以从对象和语义部分之间的复杂交互中估计物理属性。因此，我们提出了 PhysForge，一个基于视频扩散模型 (VDM) 的框架，用于解决这些挑战，包括两个用于材料可控物理拟合和组件级交互建模的互补模块。第一个，PhysFit，利用材料条件的 VDM 动力学作为物理属性拟合的参考，引入窗口拟合来关注材料显示的相互作用，并引入幻觉强迫来将 VDM 幻觉的非物理运动与材料响应分开。其次，PhysComp将复杂的交互分解为单个对象和语义组件，使其物理参数能够独立优化并灵活组合以进行联合仿真。 PhysAssets 和 Google Scanned Objects 上的实验表明，与现有方法相比，PhysForge 可以对依赖于材料的物理响应提供更强的控制，同时支持复杂交互的组件级物理建模。它在常见和不常见的材料上均获得了最佳的 CLIP 和物理可控性分数，并进行了定性比较、组件级消融以及进一步支持这些改进的用户研究。我们的项目页面位于 https://masterwanna.github.io/PhysForge.html 。

## 6. ELMA: Benchmarking Anaphoric Compositions for Long-Term Text-to-Motion Generation / ELMA：长期文本到动作生成的照应组合基准测试

**Date**: 2026-10-09 | **arXiv**: [2610.13549v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13549v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13549v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Text-guided long-term human motion generation is essential for applications such as continuously operating digital avatars. Despite recent progress on short clips, existing models struggle with multi-segment instructions that involve anaphora, where constraints established in earlier segments must be preserved over time. In this paper, we introduce a novel task of long-term motion generation from such anaphoric compositions. To mitigate this, we present ELMA, an episodic long-term motion dataset with dense segment-level annotations that preserve cross-segment dependencies. Built via a fully automated collection and filtering pipeline, ELMA extracts high-quality, continuous 30-second clips from in-the-wild videos, capturing the episodic narratives necessary for long-range state modeling. Leveraging this dataset, we fine-tune state-of-the-art motion generation models to incorporate long-range context. Extensive experiments reveal fundamental shortcomings in current models when handling context-dependent instructions. We demonstrate that ELMA enables significant improvements in both global physical realism and semantic alignment, setting a robust new baseline for long-term motion synthesis.

文本引导的长期人体运动生成对于连续操作的数字化身等应用至关重要。尽管最近在短片方面取得了进展，但现有模型仍难以应对涉及回指的多段指令，随着时间的推移，必须保留早期段中建立的约束。在本文中，我们介绍了一项从此类照应组合中生成长期运动的新颖任务。为了缓解这个问题，我们提出了 ELMA，这是一个情景式长期运动数据集，具有密集的分段级注释，可以保留跨分段依赖性。 ELMA 通过全自动收集和过滤管道构建，从野外视频中提取高质量、连续 30 秒的剪辑，捕获远程状态建模所需的情景叙述。利用该数据集，我们微调最先进的运动生成模型以纳入远程上下文。大量的实验揭示了当前模型在处理上下文相关指令时的根本缺陷。我们证明 ELMA 能够显着改善全局物理真实性和语义对齐，为长期运动合成设定稳健的新基线。

## 7. Beyond Successful Robot Demonstrations: Learning from Human Interactions and Modeling Failure Dynamics / 超越成功的机器人演示：从人类交互中学习并建模故障动态

**Date**: 2026-10-09 | **arXiv**: [2610.13522v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13522v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13522v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Action-conditioned world models offer a promising foundation for generalist robotic intelligence by predicting the consequences of actions. However, existing robotic datasets provide limited coverage of the diverse interaction dynamics required for generalizable world modeling. Large-scale egocentric human videos offer complementary interaction experience, yet the embodiment gap between human hands and robotic manipulators hinders effective cross-embodiment learning. Meanwhile, the predominance of successful demonstrations leaves failure dynamics substantially underrepresented, limiting the ability of world models to anticipate unsuccessful executions. To address these limitations, we introduce HF-WM, an action-conditioned world model that combines Human interaction learning with Failure-aware post-training. To bridge the embodiment gap, we develop a dual-branch framework that couples video generation with auxiliary pointflow prediction, using stabilized pointflow as a unified motion representation across human and robot interactions while suppressing egocentric camera motion. To improve failure modeling, we further perform Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) post-training on perturbed actions derived from successful demonstrations, guided by action alignment and physical plausibility rewards, enabling the model to learn plausible failure dynamics without paired ground-truth videos. Extensive experiments demonstrate that HF-WM achieves state-of-the-art performance in action-conditioned video generation and exhibits strong utility for policy evaluation in downstream robotic tasks.

行动条件世界模型通过预测行动的后果，为通用机器人智能提供了有希望的基础。然而，现有的机器人数据集对可概括的世界建模所需的各种交互动态的覆盖范围有限。大规模以自我为中心的人类视频提供了互补的交互体验，但人手和机器人操纵器之间的体现差距阻碍了有效的跨体现学习。与此同时，成功示范的主导地位使得失败动态的代表性大大不足，限制了世界模型预测不成功执行的能力。为了解决这些限制，我们引入了 HF-WM，这是一种动作条件世界模型，它将人类交互学习与故障感知后培训相结合。为了弥补实施差距，我们开发了一个双分支框架，将视频生成与辅助点流预测结合起来，使用稳定的点流作为人类和机器人交互的统一运动表示，同时抑制以自我为中心的相机运动。为了改进故障建模，我们进一步对成功演示中产生的扰动动作进行组相对策略优化（GRPO）后训练，以动作对齐和物理合理性奖励为指导，使模型能够在没有配对地面实况视频的情况下学习合理的故障动态。大量实验表明，HF-WM 在动作条件视频生成方面实现了最先进的性能，并在下游机器人任务中的策略评估中表现出强大的实用性。

## 8. TextVBench: Evaluating Text Rendering and Preservation in Video Generation / TextVBench：评估视频生成中的文本渲染和保存

**Date**: 2026-10-09 | **arXiv**: [2610.13412v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13412v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13412v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Modern video generators can synthesize convincing scenes while rendering a few characters incorrectly, omitting requested text, or changing it across frames. Measuring these failures is harder than applying OCR independently to each frame: when a scene contains multiple text targets and multiple noisy OCR regions, the evaluator must first determine which observation belongs to which intended text. We introduce TextVBench, a benchmark and evaluation framework that formulates visual-text evaluation as a multi-target, multi-instance spatiotemporal correspondence problem. TextVBench contains two complementary tracks: TextVBench-T2V measures the creation and maintenance of prompt-specified text, while TextVBench-Ref2V measures preservation of text from reference images. Both tracks use target-conditioned candidate selection rather than unconstrained VLM transcription, support one target mapping to multiple OCR regions, and separate frame-level spatial fidelity from cross-frame temporal fidelity. A length-weighted harmonic score summarizes these factors, while extra-text diagnostics expose failure modes hidden by a single OCR score. The benchmark is organized around controlled text, layout, carrier, and motion attributes so that aggregate scores can be traced back to specific capability limits. Across the evaluated model runs, MiniMax-H3 reaches a T2V TextVScore of 68.32, while the strongest Ref2V score is 56.51; attribute slices reveal a pronounced drop as the number of simultaneous reference text regions increases.

现代视频生成器可以合成令人信服的场景，同时错误地渲染一些字符、忽略请求的文本或跨帧更改它。测量这些失败比独立地将 OCR 应用于每个帧更困难：当场景包含多个文本目标和多个嘈杂的 OCR 区域时，评估者必须首先确定哪个观察属于哪个预期文本。我们引入了 TextVBench，一个基准和评估框架，它将视觉文本评估制定为多目标、多实例时空对应问题。 TextVBench 包含两个互补的轨道：TextVBench-T2V 测量提示指定文本的创建和维护，而 TextVBench-Ref2V 测量参考图像中文本的保存。两种轨道都使用目标条件候选选择而不是无约束的 VLM 转录，支持一个目标映射到多个 OCR 区域，并将帧级空间保真度与跨帧时间保真度分开。长度加权调和分数总结了这些因素，而文本外诊断则揭示了单个 OCR 分数隐藏的故障模式。该基准测试围绕受控文本、布局、载体和运动属性进行组织，以便可以将总分追溯到特定的功能限制。在评估的模型运行中，MiniMax-H3 的 T2V TextVScore 达到 68.32，而最强的 Ref2V 得分为 56.51；随着同时参考文本区域数量的增加，属性切片显示出明显的下降。

</details>
