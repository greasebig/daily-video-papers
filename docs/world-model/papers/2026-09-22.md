## 1. A Patient World Model for Early Forecasting of Digital Health Campaign Outcomes: Capabilities and Limits / 用于早期预测数字健康运动结果的患者世界模型：能力和局限性

**Date**: 2026-09-20 | **arXiv**: [2609.23333v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23333v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.23333v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Digital direct-to-consumer (DTC) health campaigns are usually measured after the fact. In-flight forecasting commonly relies on a separate classifier for every cutoff and horizon. We treat this task as a dynamic-system problem and build a compact patient world model. The architecture maintains a latent state per patient, learns exposure-conditioned state dynamics jointly with a weekly conversion hazard, and rolls forward into future conversion curves. We evaluate it on a US campaign dataset with 147{,}173 patients and 5.2 million at-risk person-weeks. In a retrospective evaluation conditioned on recorded future exposures, the model forecasts the remaining new-to-brand prescription volume through week 52 with a relative error of 2.9\% from a week-4 cutoff and 0.8--2.6\% from cutoffs at weeks 8--26. The strongest non-recurrent baseline, a pooled-hazard gradient boosting model given the same survival rollout and information, has relative errors of 13.6--33.1\%. Per-horizon classifiers perform substantially worse. A Fisher-information analysis motivates dense next-exposure supervision when conversions are rare. Removing this auxiliary objective increases prescription-volume error by approximately $2$--$14\times$, while providing no consistent disadvantage on the more common specialist-visit outcome. We also evaluate scenario simulation. Switching all future exposure off raises predicted conversion from 0.31 to 0.89, a pattern consistent with selection effects in observational exposure data. This result highlights the limits of interpreting exposure-conditioned rollouts causally.

数字化直接面向消费者 (DTC) 的健康活动通常是在事后进行衡量。飞行中预测通常依赖于每个截止点和范围的单独分类器。我们将此任务视为动态系统问题，并构建一个紧凑的患者世界模型。该架构维护每个患者的潜在状态，学习暴露条件状态动态以及每周的转换风险，并向前滚动到未来的转换曲线。我们在美国活动数据集上对其进行了评估，该数据集包含 147{,}173 名患者和 520 万高危人群周。在以记录的未来暴露为条件的回顾性评估中，该模型预测了第 52 周剩余的新品牌处方量，与第 4 周截止值的相对误差为 2.9%，与第 8--26 周截止值的相对误差为 0.8--2.6%。最强的非经常性基线，即给定相同生存推出和信息的混合风险梯度提升模型，相对误差为 13.6--33.1%。每视野分类器的性能要差得多。当转化很少时，费舍尔信息分析会激发密集的下一次曝光监督。删除这个辅助目标会使处方量误差增加约 $2$--$14\times$，同时不会对更常见的专科医生就诊结果带来持续的不利影响。我们还评估场景模拟。关闭所有未来暴露会将预测转化率从 0.31 提高到 0.89，这一模式与观察暴露数据中的选择效应一致。这一结果凸显了因果解释暴露条件推出的局限性。

## 2. Robot World Models Are Not Invariant to How the Actions Are Written / 机器人世界模型并不随着动作的编写方式而变化

**Date**: 2026-09-19 | **arXiv**: [2609.23252v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23252v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.23252v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

A robot policy is trained with one of two action parameterizations: absolute joint targets, or deltas relative to the current state. The choice is a live engineering decision in robot learning, and a world model conditioned on actions inherits it silently. We show the inheritance is catastrophic. A latent dynamics model trained on one parameterization and handed the identical commanded trajectory written in the other collapses: retrieval degrades by 2.6-13.4x across three robot datasets and two morphologies, goal-conditioned action selection falls from 53% to 15%, and on PushT the two beliefs about the same future are near-orthogonal (cos = 0.067, worst case -0.377), so the predictor does not degrade gracefully, it answers a different question. This is not a distribution-shift artifact in the usual sense: the two encodings are mutually reconstructible at R^2 = 0.996 given the joint input, so no information is lost, and we give the test that separates a valid re-parameterization from a lossy summary or a sensor swap. The test rejected three of the four axes we proposed. The defect lives in the action channel, which the invariance literature for visual models does not examine: work there concerns crops, jitter and camera pose, while the parameterization of the commands goes unaudited. The repair is averaging over the two encodings, and where it goes matters. Averaging the objective restores task performance by itself; averaging the outputs, safe for probabilities by concavity, is not available for direction-valued prediction, where the normalized mean can score below every member of the orbit. What objective-averaging leaves behind is the tail: worst-case agreement stays at 0.78, a disagreement penalty closes it to 0.995, and over a latent rollout it is the difference between a worst case that erodes and one that holds. On PushT, averaging alone does not repair the axis.

机器人策略使用两种动作参数化之一进行训练：绝对关节目标或相对于当前状态的增量。这个选择是机器人学习中的实时工程决策，而以动作为条件的世界模型会默默地继承它。我们证明继承是灾难性的。在一个参数化上训练的潜在动力学模型，并传递在其他崩溃中写入的相同命令轨迹：在三个机器人数据集和两种形态中，检索性能下降了 2.6-13.4 倍，目标条件动作选择从 53% 下降到 15%，并且在 PushT 上，关于同一未来的两个信念几乎是正交的（cos = 0.067，最坏情况 -0.377），因此预测器不会降级它优雅地回答了另一个问题。这不是通常意义上的分布偏移伪影：在给定联合输入的情况下，两种编码在 R^2 = 0.996 处是可相互重构的，因此不会丢失任何信息，并且我们给出了将有效的重新参数化与有损摘要或传感器交换分开的测试。该测试拒绝了我们提出的四个轴中的三个。该缺陷存在于动作通道中，视觉模型的不变性文献没有检查该通道：那里的工作涉及裁剪、抖动和相机姿势，而命令的参数化未经审核。修复是对两种编码进行平均，修复的位置很重要。对目标进行平均可以自行恢复任务绩效；对输出进行平均（通过凹性保证概率是安全的）不适用于方向值预测，在方向值预测中，归一化平均值的得分可能低于轨道的每个成员。目标平均留下的是尾部：最坏情况的一致性保持在 0.78，分歧惩罚将其接近 0.995，并且在潜在的推出过程中，最坏情况的消失与维持的情况之间的差异。在 PushT 上，单独求平均值并不能修复轴。
