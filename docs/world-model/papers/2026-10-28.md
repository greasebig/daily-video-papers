## 1. ThinkWorld: World Imagination Reinforces Chain-of-Dynamics Reasoning / ThinkWorld：世界想象力强化动力链推理

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37666v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37666v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37666v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have made substantial progress in robotic manipulation, but their generalization remains constrained by the diversity and coverage of action-labeled robot demonstrations. Human videos provide much broader behavioral and environmental experience, while learning from them faces a fundamental trade-off between scalable data and explicit action grounding. We introduce ThinkWorld, a VLA learning framework that grounds action reasoning in world dynamics and strengthens it through reinforcement learning with world imagination. ThinkWorld represents each action thought as a Chain-of-Dynamics (CoD), a sequence of discrete motion tokens that captures the intermediate dynamics connecting the current world to a future state and serves as a common action representation across embodiments. We first initialize CoD with action-labeled demonstrations, then predict the future induced by each CoD and reinforce those whose predictions better match the observed outcome. This enables videos without action annotations to provide direct supervision from observed state transitions, allowing human and robot experience to refine the same reasoning space. Experiments in simulation and on real robots demonstrate effective human-to-robot transfer and strong generalization to unseen tasks and environments.

视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型在机器人操作方面取得了实质性进展，但其泛化仍然受到动作标记机器人演示的多样性和覆盖范围的限制。人类视频提供了更广泛的行为和环境体验，而从中学习面临着可扩展数据和明确行动基础之间的基本权衡。我们推出 ThinkWorld，这是一个 VLA 学习框架，它将行动推理建立在世界动态的基础上，并通过具有世界想象力的强化学习来强化它。 ThinkWorld 将每个动作思想表示为动态链 (CoD)，这是一系列离散的运动标记，捕获连接当前世界与未来状态的中间动态，并充当跨实施例的通用动作表示。我们首先用动作标记的演示来初始化 CoD，然后预测每个 CoD 引发的未来，并强化那些预测与观察到的结果更匹配的预测。这使得没有动作注释的视频能够从观察到的状态转换中提供直接监督，从而使人类和机器人的经验能够完善相同的推理空间。模拟实验和真实机器人实验证明了人机之间的有效迁移以及对未见过的任务和环境的强大泛化能力。

</details>

## 2. Understanding the limits of latent world models / 了解潜在世界模型的局限性

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37588v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37588v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37588v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World models are widely proposed as core components for enabling agents to reason over long temporal and spatial horizons. Yet current evaluations often emphasize task performance while providing limited insight into whether learned world models acquire predictive latent dynamics that reflect the underlying structure of the environment. Here we introduce the concept of faithful latent world models as an equivariance condition between the environment's transition map and the learned latent dynamics. We propose latent rollout consistency (LRC) as a metric to evaluate action-conditioned latent prediction. Strikingly, we find that across the studied state of the art latent world models, none solves the task by actually faithfully modeling the dynamics of the environment.

世界模型被广泛认为是使智能体能够在长时空范围内进行推理的核心组件。然而，当前的评估通常强调任务绩效，同时对学习的世界模型是否获得反映环境底层结构的预测潜在动态提供有限的洞察。在这里，我们引入忠实潜在世界模型的概念，作为环境转换图和学习的潜在动态之间的等方差条件。我们提出潜在推出一致性（LRC）作为评估动作条件潜在预测的指标。引人注目的是，我们发现，在所研究的最先进的潜在世界模型中，没有一个能够通过真正忠实地模拟环境的动态来解决任务。

## 3. World Dreamer v5-lite: A Multi-Robot World Action Model with One Dreamer / World Dreamer v5-lite：一个梦想家的多机器人世界动作模型

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37518v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37518v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37518v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Multi-robot control requires integrating distributed camera views while predicting separate action sequences. Most vision-language-action and world-action models target one robot. Combining multiple views alone does not establish spatial correspondence between them. World Dreamer v5-lite is a multi-robot world-action model built around a shared world state for robot-specific action prediction. Calibrated features from a pretrained video encoder populate this state in workspace coordinates; its spatial size remains fixed as views are added. A vision-language model provides scene context and one query per robot. The queries read the shared state and exchange pose-aware information, while their action streams remain separate. Masked-view cooperative training withholds selected camera views from both visual branches while retaining all action targets. This encourages the use of observations shared by other robots. Pretraining uses more than 10,000 hours of trajectories from 20 simulated robot types, RoboFactory, and real robots. On RoboFactory, World Dreamer v5-lite achieves 74.0% mean success, 23.7 percentage points above the strongest evaluated baseline. Across four real-robot tasks, it reaches 78.8% mean success, a margin of 13.8 points. It also reaches 58.08% overall on RoboCasa365.

多机器人控制需要集成分布式摄像机视图，同时预测单独的动作序列。大多数视觉-语言-动作和世界-动作模型都以一个机器人为目标。单独组合多个视图并不能在它们之间建立空间对应关系。 World Dreamer v5-lite 是一个围绕共享世界状态构建的多机器人世界动作模型，用于机器人特定的动作预测。来自预训练视频编码器的校准特征在工作空间坐标中填充此状态；随着视图的添加，其空间大小保持固定。视觉语言模型为每个机器人提供场景上下文和一个查询。这些查询读取共享状态并交换姿势感知信息，而它们的操作流保持独立。掩模视图合作训练保留来自两个视觉分支的选定摄像机视图，同时保留所有动作目标。这鼓励使用其他机器人共享的观察结果。预训练使用来自 20 种模拟机器人类型、RoboFactory 和真实机器人的超过 10,000 小时的轨迹。在 RoboFactory 上，World Dreamer v5-lite 的平均成功率为 74.0%，比最强评估基线高出 23.7 个百分点。在四项真实机器人任务中，平均成功率达到 78.8%，领先 13.8 个百分点。 RoboCasa365 上的总体利用率也达到 58.08%。

## 4. Representing the Unseen: Rethinking Visual Imagination for Spatial Reasoning / 代表看不见的事物：重新思考空间推理的视觉想象力

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37437v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37437v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37437v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Visual world models increasingly support spatial reasoning by generating RGB views of unobserved states. However, RGB has become the default interface largely because it is well suited for visual generation, rather than necessarily being the most effective representation for downstream reasoning. Different spatial questions may benefit from different representations of the same imagined state: top-down geometry can facilitate global distance comparisons, while panoramic views provide broader context for reasoning about open space. To isolate the role of representation in spatial imagination, we introduce ImagiRep, a simulator-grounded benchmark of 922 questions spanning seven question types across two settings, Viewpoint Imagination and Cross-view Integration, providing oracle RGB and six alternative representations (e.g., depth, panoramic views, and top-down geometry) of the same unobserved state. This controlled comparison reveals that even accurate imagined RGB does not guarantee accurate spatial reasoning, and different questions benefit from different representations. Similar trends hold for representations derived from world-model-estimated states. Notably, estimated RGB alone can mislead spatial reasoning, while alternative representations retain complementary benefits. Building on these findings, we introduce GEARS, a lightweight test-time strategy for adaptive representation selection, outperforming existing spatial imagination methods and showing that reasoning about unseen states requires more than generating RGB views.

视觉世界模型通过生成未观察到的状态的 RGB 视图来越来越支持空间推理。然而，RGB 已成为默认接口，很大程度上是因为它非常适合视觉生成，而不一定是下游推理的最有效表示。不同的空间问题可能受益于同一想象状态的不同表示：自上而下的几何形状可以促进全局距离比较，而全景视图为开放空间的推理提供更广泛的背景。为了分离表征在空间想象中的作用，我们引入了 ImagiRep，这是一个基于模拟器的基准，包含 922 个问题，跨越两种设置（视点想象和跨视图集成）的 7 种问题类型，提供了 Oracle RGB 和相同未观察状态的六种替代表征（例如，深度、全景视图和自上而下的几何）。这种受控比较表明，即使是准确的想象 RGB 也不能保证准确的空间推理，并且不同的问题受益于不同的表示。类似的趋势也适用于来自世界模型估计状态的表示。值得注意的是，单独估计的 RGB 可能会误导空间推理，而替代表示保留了互补的优势。基于这些发现，我们引入了 GEARS，这是一种用于自适应表示选择的轻量级测试时策略，其性能优于现有的空间想象方法，并表明推理不可见状态需要的不仅仅是生成 RGB 视图。

## 5. JEPA-Inspired Latent Field Prediction for Sparse-Pilot MIMO-OFDM Channel Reconstruction / 受 JEPA 启发的稀疏导频 MIMO-OFDM 信道重建的潜在场预测

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37391v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37391v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37391v1)

**Categories**: eess.SP, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Accurate acquisition of full channel state information (CSI) is essential for multiple-input multiple-output orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing (MIMO-OFDM) wireless systems, while dense pilot-based estimation incurs substantial overhead. Directly minimizing the prediction error over raw CSI coefficients is fragile in realistic propagation environments, since small-scale fading, mobility, local scattering, and small spatial perturbations can induce large phase-sensitive variations. Nevertheless, wireless channels contain strong spatial, temporal, and frequency-domain correlations that can be more predictably represented in a latent propagation-field domain. Motivated by this observation, we propose a joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA)-inspired field world model, defined as a latent predictive model of the wireless propagation field, for sparse-pilot channel reconstruction. The model maps CSI observations into latent time-frequency tokens, predicts masked or future latent field states from historical multi-band context, and fuses the predicted latent prior with sparse noisy current pilots to reconstruct the full MIMO-OFDM channel. Ray-tracing experiments show that the proposed method improves normalized mean square error (NMSE) over pilot-only and direct reconstruction baselines, supporting latent field prediction as an effective prior for sparse-pilot channel acquisition.

准确获取完整的信道状态信息 (CSI) 对于多输入多输出正交频分复用 (MIMO-OFDM) 无线系统至关重要，而密集的基于导频的估计会产生大量开销。在现实传播环境中，直接最小化原始 CSI 系数的预测误差是脆弱的，因为小规模衰落、移动性、局部散射和小空间扰动可能会引起大的相敏变化。然而，无线信道包含强大的空间、时间和频域相关性，这些相关性可以在潜在传播场域中更可预测地表示。受这一观察的启发，我们提出了一种受联合嵌入预测架构（JEPA）启发的场世界模型，定义为无线传播场的潜在预测模型，用于稀疏导频信道重建。该模型将 CSI 观测结果映射到潜在时频标记，根据历史多频段上下文预测屏蔽或未来潜在场状态，并将预测的潜在先验与稀疏噪声当前导频融合以重建完整的 MIMO-OFDM 信道。射线追踪实验表明，所提出的方法相对于仅导频和直接重建基线改进了归一化均方误差（NMSE），支持潜在场预测作为稀疏导频信道采集的有效先验。

## 6. PDRVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model with Physical Dynamics Representations / PDRVLA：具有物理动力学表示的视觉-语言-动作模型

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37381v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37381v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37381v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.CV, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Vision--Language--Action (VLA) models generate robot actions from visual observations and language instructions, but their understanding of physical dynamics remains limited. Video world models can capture scene dynamics, yet generating future videos introduces high latency into the control loop. We introduce PDRVLA, whose lightweight Physical Dynamics Module extracts physical tokens from vision--language features to condition action generation without online video generation. The module learns through direct prediction of future RGB, depth, and optical-flow features, with auxiliary supervision through a frozen pretrained video world model. Training relies entirely on publicly available data, expanded through human-to-robot synthesis, subtask annotation, and embodied chain-of-thought (ECoT) annotation. Across four simulation benchmarks, PDRVLA achieves success rates of 99.3% on LIBERO, 89.9% on LIBERO-Plus, 93.6% on RoboTwin~2.0, and 81.5% on SimplerEnv. Ablations show improved future prediction with world-model supervision and evaluate how subtask conditioning, future object-pose supervision, and ECoT training affect action success.

视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型根据视觉观察和语言指令生成机器人动作，但它们对物理动力学的理解仍然有限。视频世界模型可以捕获场景动态，但生成未来视频会给控制循环带来高延迟。我们介绍 PDRVLA，其轻量级物理动力学模块从视觉语言特征中提取物理标记，以条件动作生成，而无需生成在线视频。该模块通过直接预测未来的 RGB、深度和光流特征进行学习，并通过冻结的预训练视频世界模型进行辅助监督。训练完全依赖于公开可用的数据，并通过人机合成、子任务注释和体现思想链（ECOT）注释进行扩展。在四个模拟基准中，PDRVLA 在 LIBERO 上实现了 99.3% 的成功率，在 LIBERO-Plus 上实现了 89.9%，在 RoboTwin~2.0 上实现了 93.6%，在 SimplerEnv 上实现了 81.5%。消融显示通过世界模型监督改进了未来预测，并评估子任务调节、未来物体姿势监督和 ECoT 训练如何影响行动成功。

## 7. How Much Can Best-of-N Help a World Action Model? Measuring Selectable Headroom and Realizing It in Closed Loop / Best-of-N 对世界行动模型有多大帮助？测量可选余量并在闭环中实现

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37196v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37196v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37196v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.CV, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World action models (WAMs) predict the visual consequences of the actions they generate, which makes test-time selection a natural way to improve a fixed policy: imagine the futures of candidate actions and execute the best one. The benefit of selection is bounded by the selectable headroom, how much better the best candidate truly is, but the standard estimate, which executes each candidate once and keeps the best outcome, conflates candidate quality with the randomness of everything that follows. We introduce a denoised counterfactual protocol, based on bitwise-reproducible branching, repeated continuations, held-out oracles, and common random numbers, that measures the headroom a selector can realize, and use its measurements to build a fail-gated selector. Across four WAMs, two benchmarks, over 100,000 branched rollouts, and pre-registered tests, we find that (i) the standard estimate overstates the selectable headroom of native samples: bounds from 17 continuations per candidate place its selectable share between 24% and 66% for three WAMs; (ii) structured action perturbations create up to 16 points of selectable headroom; and (iii) in both WAMs with action-conditioned imagination, imagined futures carry candidate-outcome information with no detectable loss relative to real futures, information that the value head of Cosmos Policy leaves unused. Deviating from the policy chunk only when a readout of the WAM predicts failure, the selector raises closed-loop task success from 76.9% to 81.6% on held-out episodes of training tasks, with the largest gain where the policy fails most often, and from 62.6% to 69.8% on unseen tasks.

世界动作模型 (WAM) 可以预测它们生成的动作的视觉后果，这使得测试时选择成为改进固定策略的自然方式：想象候选动作的未来并执行最佳动作。选择的好处受到可选择余量的限制，即最佳候选者真正好多少，但标准估计（执行每个候选者一次并保持最佳结果）将候选者质量与后续一切的随机性混为一谈。我们引入了一种去噪反事实协议，基于按位可再现分支、重复延续、保留预言和常见随机数，该协议测量选择器可以实现的净空，并使用其测量结果来构建故障门控选择器。在四个 WAM、两个基准、超过 100,000 个分支部署和预注册测试中，我们发现 (i) 标准估计夸大了本机样本的可选择余量：每个候选者 17 个延续的界限将三个 WAM 的可选择份额置于 24% 到 66% 之间； (ii) 结构化动作扰动可创建多达 16 个点的可选净空； (iii) 在两个具有行动条件想象的 WAM 中，想象的未来携带候选结果信息，相对于真实的未来没有可检测到的损失，而 Cosmos 政策的价值主管并未使用这些信息。仅当 WAM 的读数预测失败时，选择器才会偏离策略块，在保留的训练任务中，选择器将闭环任务成功率从 76.9% 提高到 81.6%，在策略最常失败的地方收益最大，在未见过的任务上，将闭环任务成功率从 62.6% 提高到 69.8%。

## 8. MLE-World: Predicting Machine Learning Experiment Outcomes with a Learned World Model / MLE-World：使用学习世界模型预测机器学习实验结果

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37175v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37175v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37175v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

AI research agents can propose, execute, and improve programs that solve machine learning tasks. However, executing each candidate program requires training and evaluating ML models, limiting how many candidates an agent can explore at each step. We introduce MLE-World, a world model finetuned from Qwen3.5-4B that predicts a program's terminal output (error traces, training logs, and validation scores) from its code and data description without executing it, together with a confidence score estimating the prediction's correctness. Because a prediction costs one inference call rather than a training run, MLE-World makes test time scaling (TTS) affordable for these agents. We build five TTS policies, guided by MLE-World, that compare several candidates per step by their predicted outputs: Sequential revision, Best-of-N, Pairwise judging, Fusion, and a UCB style rule that also adds an exploration bonus from this confidence score. Since predictions can be wrong, we run a candidate for real only when its confidence score is low. On 17 MLE-bench Lite tasks, MLE-World matches the real terminal output on 77.9% of held out programs, versus 41.1% for much larger prompted models. MLE-World guided TTS achieves up to 22.1% higher observed mean performance than the agent without a world model, with less real execution. Project webpage: https://mle-world.github.io/.

人工智能研究代理可以提出、执行和改进解决机器学习任务的程序。然而，执行每个候选程序需要训练和评估 ML 模型，从而限制了代理在每一步可以探索的候选数。我们引入了 MLE-World，这是一个从 Qwen3.5-4B 进行微调的世界模型，它可以根据程序的代码和数据描述来预测程序的终端输出（错误跟踪、训练日志和验证分数）而不执行它，以及估计预测正确性的置信度分数。由于预测需要一次推理调用而不是一次训练运行，因此 MLE-World 使这些代理能够负担得起测试时间缩放 (TTS)。我们在 MLE-World 的指导下构建了五种 TTS 策略，通过预测输出每一步对多个候选进行比较：顺序修订、Best-of-N、成对判断、融合和 UCB 风格规则，该规则还从该置信度得分中增加了探索奖励。由于预测可能是错误的，因此只有当候选者的置信度分数较低时，我们才会对其进行真实测试。在 17 个 MLE-bench Lite 任务中，MLE-World 与 77.9% 的保留程序的实际终端输出相匹配，而对于更大的提示模型，这一比例为 41.1%。与没有世界模型的代理相比，MLE-World 引导的 TTS 的观察到的平均性能提高了 22.1%，但实际执行较少。项目网页：https://mle-world.github.io/。

## 9. GAWM: A Compact Latent World-Action Model for Multi-Task Robot Manipulation / GAWM：用于多任务机器人操作的紧凑潜在世界动作模型

**Date**: 2026-10-27 | **arXiv**: [2610.37091v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37091v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37091v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

**Code**: https://github.com/gaoxiang12/ga-wm.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World-action models (WAMs) incorporate the prediction of future observations into robot policy learning. However, most WAMs build on billion-parameter vision-language or video-generation backbones, which incur high training cost and inference latency. In this letter, we present GAWM (Geometry-Aware World Model), a compact multi-task WAM that predicts future observations in the latent space of a frozen DINOv3 encoder. GAWM compresses the features of each camera view into a small set of tokens. A lightweight predictor, conditioned on the task rather than on actions, forecasts these tokens at two future horizons. The forecast is supervised with the dense patch features of future frames, which requires it to encode the spatial layout of the future scene. An action decoder directly regresses a chunk of future actions from the current and predicted tokens. With fewer than 100M parameters and no robot or video pretraining, GAWM outperforms billion-parameter WAMs on RoboTwin 2.0 and performs comparably to billion-parameter VLAs on LIBERO. ## 17. RoKON: Efficient and Adaptive Data-Enabled Predictive Control for Robotic Manipulation / RoKON：用于机器人操作的高效、自适应数据支持预测控制

**Date**: 2026-10-26 | **arXiv**: [2610.35458v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35458v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35458v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Data-enabled predictive control has recently emerged as a promising control paradigm, enabling a transparent optimal-control formulation without the need to learn an explicit dynamics model. This paper presents a number of extensions paving the path towards its use in real-time robotics tasks. Following recent work, we utilize a nonlinear predictor based on Gaussian kernels to represent the relationship between input and output data. We propose a damped Gauss-Newton method that enables real-time control by efficiently exploiting the least-squares structure of the control problem. In addition, we introduce a hybrid buffer management strategy that incorporates informative online data while retaining relevant offline data, allowing the predictor to account for changes in the dynamics. We demonstrate the tracking performance and computational suitability of the proposed method in MuJoCo using a Franka Research 3 arm pushing a block across surface, without prior knowledge of its changing friction values.

数据支持的预测控制最近已成为一种有前景的控制范式，无需学习显式动力学模型即可实现透明的最优控制公式。本文提出了许多扩展，为其在实时机器人任务中的使用铺平了道路。根据最近的工作，我们利用基于高斯核的非线性预测器来表示输入和输出数据之间的关系。我们提出了一种阻尼高斯-牛顿方法，该方法通过有效利用控制问题的最小二乘结构来实现实时控制。此外，我们引入了一种混合缓冲区管理策略，该策略包含信息丰富的在线数据，同时保留相关的离线数据，使预测器能够考虑动态变化。我们使用 Franka Research 3 手臂在表面上推动块，在 MuJoCo 中演示了所提出方法的跟踪性能和计算适用性，而无需事先了解其变化的摩擦值。
