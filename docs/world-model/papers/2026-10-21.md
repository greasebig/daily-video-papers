## 1. Physical Canvas: A Vision Tactile World Model For Robot Policy Evaluation And Learning / 物理画布：用于机器人政策评估和学习的视觉触觉世界模型

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.28613v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28613v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28613v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

**Project**: https://zebinx.github.io/PhysCanvas_HomePage/.  <details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Evaluating and post-training robot policies through physical interaction is costly and difficult to scale, motivating action-conditioned world models as interactive simulators. However, visually plausible predictions can misrepresent contact dynamics, providing unreliable feedback for policy evaluation and learning. We introduce PhysCanvas, a state-consistent visuo-tactile world model for contact-rich robot manipulation. PhysCanvas unifies multi-view RGB, tactile images, marker displacement, task state, and actions within a unified vision space. All modalities share a video VAE and pretrained generative backbone, allowing optical touch to reuse visual and temporal priors. Motion images derived through forward kinematics and camera projection provide spatially aligned action conditioning, while an initial RGB anchor and updated reference observations guide sequential generation. Our consistency-aware post-training uses the frozen Scene Reward Model, Modality Reward Model, and State Reward Model to supervise scene preservation, visual-tactile compatibility, and agreement with real task outcomes. The resulting world model supports closed-loop policy rollouts for evaluation and provides imagined interactions and rewards for GRPO policy updates. Across four real-world manipulation tasks, when policies are rolled out in the world model, State Reward Model supervision increases the arithmetic mean of task-wise Pearson correlations between world-model and real-world success rates from 0.602 to 0.885. Policy post-training in the reward-guided world model further increases $π_{0.5}$'s mean real-world success rate from a 59.1% SFT baseline to 70.8%. These results demonstrate the value of unified multimodal modeling and consistency-aware post-training for robot policy evaluation and learning. Project page: https://zebinx.github.io/PhysCanvas_HomePage/.

通过物理交互评估和后训练机器人策略成本高昂且难以扩展，从而激发了将动作条件世界模型作为交互式模拟器的兴趣。然而，视觉上合理的预测可能会歪曲接触动态，为政策评估和学习提供不可靠的反馈。我们介绍 PhysCanvas，这是一种状态一致的视觉触觉世界模型，用于接触丰富的机器人操作。 PhysCanvas 在统一的视觉空间内统一了多视图 RGB、触觉图像、标记位移、任务状态和动作。所有模式共享视频 VAE 和预训练的生成主干，允许光学触摸重用视觉和时间先验。通过正向运动学和相机投影导出的运动图像提供了空间对齐的动作调节，而初始 RGB 锚点和更新的参考观察指导顺序生成。我们的一致性意识后训练使用冻结的场景奖励模型、模态奖励模型和状态奖励模型来监督场景保存、视觉触觉兼容性以及与真实任务结果的一致性。由此产生的世界模型支持闭环政策推出进行评估，并为 GRPO 政策更新提供想象的交互和奖励。在四个现实世界的操纵任务中，当在世界模型中推出政策时，状态奖励模型监督将世界模型和现实世界成功率之间的任务皮尔逊相关性的算术平均值从 0.602 增加到 0.885。奖励引导世界模型中的政策后训练进一步将 $π_{0.5}$ 的平均现实世界成功率从 59.1% SFT 基线提高到 70.8%。这些结果证明了统一多模态建模和一致性感知后训练对于机器人策略评估和学习的价值。项目页面：https://zebinx.github.io/PhysCanvas_HomePage/。

## 2. Learning Computational Structure for Sample-Efficient Prediction / 学习计算结构以实现样本高效预测

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.28555v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28555v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28555v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Statistical learning in high dimensions suffers from the curse of dimensionality. Fortunately, many functions of practical interest are compositionally sparse, meaning they admit a composition of local functions, each depending on only a few variables, allowing sample complexity to depend on local arity rather than ambient dimension. Yet realizing this benefit requires exploiting hidden compositional structure, which theory does not establish for practical gradient-based training. We study this gap with CSFRBench, a controlled benchmark that hides the target's compositional computation during training but retains it for diagnosis, and find that dense networks and sparsity-based baselines leave much of that benefit unrealized. Inspired by the spatial organization of biological neural circuits, we introduce Learned Wiring (LeWi): each neuron has a learned low-dimensional embedding, only nearby neurons connect, and embeddings and weights are trained jointly. LeWi improves sample efficiency on targets with hierarchical structure, intermediate reuse, or independent modules. Across 32 additional targets generated by independently sampled computation graphs, LeWi is the most sample-efficient method not given the target graph, with 27% lower mean learning-curve area than dense networks. Dense networks perform better on targets with a single high-arity function. LeWi's representations and connectivity align with the target computation. On 17 world-model prediction tasks spanning control, navigation, and manipulation, LeWi beats the dense baseline on 16 and wins 99/119 baseline comparisons. These results show that jointly learning connectivity and weights can exploit computational structure to improve sample efficiency.

高维度的统计学习遭受维度灾难。幸运的是，许多实际感兴趣的函数在组合上是稀疏的，这意味着它们允许局部函数的组合，每个函数仅依赖于几个变量，从而允许样本复杂性取决于局部数量而不是环境维度。然而，实现这一好处需要利用隐藏的组合结构，而该理论并未为实际的基于梯度的训练建立起来。我们使用 CSFRBench 来研究这一差距，CSFRBench 是一个受控基准，它在训练期间隐藏目标的组成计算，但保留它用于诊断，并发现密集网络和基于稀疏性的基线使大部分好处无法实现。受生物神经回路空间组织的启发，我们引入了学习连线（LeWi）：每个神经元都有一个学习的低维嵌入，只有附近的神经元连接，并且嵌入和权重联合训练。 LeWi 通过分层结构、中间重用或独立模块提高了目标的采样效率。在由独立采样计算图生成的 32 个附加目标中，LeWi 是在未给定目标图的情况下样本效率最高的方法，其平均学习曲线面积比密集网络低 27%。密集网络在具有单个高数量函数的目标上表现更好。 LeWi 的表示和连接性与目标计算一致。在涵盖控制、导航和操作的 17 个世界模型预测任务中，LeWi 在 16 个任务中击败了密集基线，并赢得了 99/119 的基线比较。这些结果表明，联合学习连通性和权重可以利用计算结构来提高样本效率。

## 3. SuperMimic: Scaling Humanoid Whole-Body Control with One Differentiable World Model / SuperMimic：用一个可微的世界模型扩展人形全身控制

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.28480v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28480v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28480v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Tracking large human-motion datasets has become the dominant route to general, expressive humanoid whole-body control, but scaling across many motions is expensive. Current pipelines use model-free reinforcement learning, either merging per-motion specialists through lossy distillation, or brute-forcing a single policy with cluster-scale compute. SuperMimic amortizes learning across all motions with one differentiable world model of their shared dynamics, fit from logged transitions of a standard rigid-body simulator. The world model and the tracking policy train together in one loop, and backpropagation-through-time over short imagined rollouts gives the policy analytic gradients. Fast imagined rollouts and analytic gradients make policy learning efficient. In 3 hours on a single GPU, SuperMimic surpasses the return of a state-of-the-art motion tracker baseline trained for 7 days on 128 GPUs. It reduces GPU-hours by more than $1000\times$ and fails on only 25 of 129,514 benchmark motions, versus 179 failures for the baseline. After 9.8 hours on two GPUs, SuperMimic achieves zero failures. From 1 to 264 hours of motion, the world model's prediction error stays stable, and GPU-hours to a target return grow slowly. Our policy transfers to a physical Unitree G1 across diverse and acrobatic motions. SuperMimic removes compute as the bottleneck to scaling humanoid whole-body control, making it practical to measure motion-tracking scaling laws.

跟踪大型人体运动数据集已成为实现通用、富有表现力的人形全身控制的主要途径，但跨多个运动进行缩放的成本很高。当前的管道使用无模型强化学习，要么通过有损蒸馏合并每运动专家，要么通过集群规模计算强制执行单个策略。 SuperMimic 通过共享动态的一个可微世界模型来分摊所有运动的学习，该模型来自标准刚体模拟器的记录转换。世界模型和跟踪策略在一个循环中一起训练，并且在短时间内想象的推出中的反向传播给出了策略分析梯度。快速想象的推出和分析梯度使策略学习变得高效。 SuperMimic 在单个 GPU 上运行 3 小时，超越了在 128 个 GPU 上训练 7 天的最先进的运动跟踪器基线的返回结果。它减少了超过 1000 美元\times$ 的 GPU 时间，并且在 129,514 个基准测试运动中仅失败了 25 个，而基准运动中有 179 个失败。在两个 GPU 上运行 9.8 小时后，SuperMimic 实现了零故障。从 1 到 264 小时的运动，世界模型的预测误差保持稳定，达到目标返回的 GPU 小时增长缓慢。我们的策略转移到物理 Unitree G1 上，跨越各种复杂的动作。 SuperMimic 消除了计算作为扩展人形全身控制的瓶颈，使得测量运动跟踪缩放定律变得实用。

</details>

## 4. A Bochner Weitzenböck Decomposition for Cellular Sheaf Laplacians, Curvature and Neural Sheaf Diffusion / 细胞束拉普拉斯、曲率和神经束扩散的 Bochner Weitzenböck 分解

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.28455v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28455v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28455v1)

**Categories**: cs.CG, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We extend the Bochner-Weitzenböck decomposition to cellular sheaf Laplacians, splitting it into a positive semidefinite term, the Bochner sheaf Laplacian, and a block diagonal term, the Forman curvature. We show that the Bochner Laplacians form a class of cellular sheaves that extends both connection and matrix-weighted Laplacians, and generalizes signed graphs to $d$ dimensions. We give a holonomy characterization of their kernel, prove the related Cheeger inequalities, and attach to them a random walk that generalizes the Gremban expansion. For the Forman term, we prove a discrete Bochner theorem, which bounds the first cohomology of a cellular sheaf with nonnegative curvature, and we read the whole decomposition as an opinion-dynamics model. We then apply this machinery to Sheaf Neural Networks, whose learnt Laplacian is otherwise unobserved. The Bochner interpretation yields a node-level conviction score that acts as an operational importance measure under frozen-model interventions, while the Forman profile provides a local descriptor of the learnt operator.

我们将 Bochner-Weitzenböck 分解扩展到细胞束拉普拉斯，将其分解为正半定项（Bochner 束拉普拉斯）和块对角项（福尔曼曲率）。我们证明博赫纳拉普拉斯算子形成了一类细胞滑轮，它扩展了连接和矩阵加权拉普拉斯算子，并将符号图推广到 $d$ 维度。我们给出了它们的核的完整特征，证明了相关的 Cheeger 不等式，并附加了一个概括 Gremban 展开式的随机游走。对于福尔曼项，我们证明了一个离散博赫纳定理，该定理限制了具有非负曲率的细胞束的第一上同调，并且我们将整个分解视为观点动力学模型。然后，我们将这种机制应用于层神经网络，其学习的拉普拉斯算子是无法观察到的。 Bochner 解释产生节点级信念分数，充当冻结模型干预下的操作重要性度量，而 Forman 配置文件提供学习算子的局部描述符。

</details>

## 5. Knowing When to Stop Thinking: A Dissection of Expected Free Energy as a Test-Time Compute Controller for Latent World-Model Planning / 知道何时停止思考：对预期自由能的剖析作为潜在世界模型规划的测试时计算控制器

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.28011v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28011v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28011v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

A planner using a learned world model can improve its predicted outcome while choosing a worse action. We study when expected free energy (EFE) can help decide how much computation to spend on a plan. An EFE-inspired objective combines goal distance with disagreement among stochastic predictions. We test candidate scoring, stopping, and online predictor updates separately. Stopping when elite cost stalls reduces refinement on tasks whose success plateaus with depth, with mean success close to fixed-depth references. At the larger tested weights, rewarding disagreement lowers success with a frozen model. Penalizing it helps at some task and weight settings but harms others. When training data omit a region needed to reach the goal, online updates recover part of the resulting loss. The tested cost-stall rule can fail when further optimization exploits model errors, because predicted cost keeps falling after task success peaks. Cost progress can guide computation on a plateau, but protecting performance after a peak requires information about the consequences of the chosen actions.

使用学习世界模型的规划者可以改善其预测结果，同时选择更糟糕的行动。我们研究预期自由能 (EFE) 何时可以帮助决定在计划上花费多少计算量。受 EFE 启发的目标将目标距离与随机预测之间的分歧结合起来。我们分别测试候选人评分、停止和在线预测器更新。当精英成本停滞时停止会减少对成功随着深度而趋于稳定的任务的精细化，平均成功接近固定深度参考。在较大的测试权重下，奖励分歧会降低冻结模型的成功率。惩罚它有助于某些任务和体重设置，但会伤害其他任务和体重设置。当训练数据遗漏达到目标所需的区域时，在线更新会恢复部分损失。当进一步优化利用模型错误时，测试的成本停滞规则可能会失败，因为预测成本在任务成功达到峰值后不断下降。成本进展可以指导平台期的计算，但在峰值后保护性能需要有关所选操作的后果的信息。

</details>

## 6. Quantum-JEPA: Quantum Born Priors for Latent-Action JEPA World Models / Quantum-JEPA：潜在作用 JEPA 世界模型的量子先验

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.27921v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27921v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.27921v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

A single observation can admit several plausible futures, making uncertainty an essential part of world modelling. However, quantum generative models remain underexplored for this task. We introduce Quantum-JEPA, a latent-action JEPA framework that learns distributions of future representations using a conditional quantum Born prior. Circuit measurements generate binary latent actions, which a shared predictor maps to alternative future representations. We train the quantum prior through the quality and diversity of these predictions using a sampled energy objective. Our model learns distinct continuations on constructed multimodal tasks and captures latent-action correlations that support coordinated multi-agent futures. On D3IL, a benchmark of diverse human demonstrations, our trained models generate multiple obstacle-crossing routes from a common starting point without observed motion. On the controlled tasks, our classical and quantum prior implementations improve prediction validity and distributional fidelity over the evaluated variational baselines. Our quantum priors combine competitive predictive quality with a parameter-efficient sampling structure: they avoid an exponentially sized categorical output layer while supporting correlated latent actions. We further validate finite-shot parameter-shift training, including training with readout noise. Unlike most prior work, which focuses on simulation, we also verify the feasibility of inference with trained priors on a real QPU. These results establish a trainable quantum component for JEPA future prediction and a basis for exploring quantum-enhanced world models at larger scales.

一次观察就可以承认几个可能的未来，从而使不确定性成为世界建模的重要组成部分。然而，针对这项任务的量子生成模型仍未得到充分探索。我们引入了 Quantum-JEPA，这是一个潜在动作 JEPA 框架，它使用条件量子 Born 先验来学习未来表示的分布。电路测量生成二进制潜在动作，共享预测器将其映射到替代的未来表示。我们使用采样的能量目标通过这些预测的质量和多样性来训练量子先验。我们的模型学习构建的多模式任务的不同延续，并捕获支持协调的多智能体未来的潜在动作相关性。在 D3IL（多样化人类演示的基准）上，我们经过训练的模型从一个共同的起点生成多条越障路线，而无需观察到运动。在受控任务上，我们的经典和量子先验实现提高了评估的变分基线的预测有效性和分布保真度。我们的量子先验将有竞争力的预测质量与参数高效的采样结构结合起来：它们避免了指数大小的分类输出层，同时支持相关的潜在动作。我们进一步验证有限样本参数转移训练，包括使用读出噪声进行训练。与大多数专注于模拟的先前工作不同，我们还验证了在真实 QPU 上使用经过训练的先验进行推理的可行性。这些结果为 JEPA 未来预测建立了可训练的量子组件，并为探索更大规模的量子增强世界模型奠定了基础。

## 7. LaLa-World: Scaling Latent World Models with Natural Language Actions / LaLa-World：用自然语言动作扩展潜在世界模型

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.27851v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27851v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.27851v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

This paper shows that JEPA-style latent world models (LWMs) can learn across diverse domains and data sources by adopting the language-conditioned interface of video generation models. We establish this approach as an effective and efficient basis for planning with imagination and downstream policy fine-tuning. To this end, we introduce LaLa-World, a Language-conditioned Latent World model that learns from simulation and robot trajectories together with broader image and video data. Two designs make this work. First, we describe both numerical actions and visual changes in language, enabling joint training of a single future predictor across heterogeneous datasets. Second, we build the predictor on a language model, allowing it to interpret language conditions while predicting future latent states. The resulting predictor achieves higher average MPC planning performance than existing LWMs trained separately for each domain or task. Adding image and video data further improves average planning performance and robustness to action phrasing. In a matched comparison with an RAEv2-based video generation model baseline, LaLa-World reaches comparable planning performance with $6\times$ less training cost and $13.9\times$ faster planning. Beyond planning, the pretrained LaLa-World serves as an initialization for LaLa-Policy, which jointly predicts actions and future visual representations. LaLa-Policy performs competitively with Cosmos Policy on LIBERO and RoboCasa, with up to $5.5\times$ faster inference.

本文表明，JEPA 式的潜在世界模型 (LWM) 可以通过采用视频生成模型的语言条件接口来跨不同领域和数据源进行学习。我们将这种方法作为富有想象力的规划和下游政策微调的有效和高效的基础。为此，我们引入了 LaLa-World，这是一种语言条件的潜在世界模型，可以从模拟和机器人轨迹以及更广泛的图像和视频数据中学习。有两种设计可以实现这一目的。首先，我们描述语言中的数字动作和视觉变化，从而能够跨异构数据集联合训练单个未来预测器。其次，我们在语言模型上构建预测器，使其能够解释语言条件，同时预测未来的潜在状态。与针对每个域或任务单独训练的现有 LWM 相比，生成的预测器实现了更高的平均 MPC 规划性能。添加图像和视频数据进一步提高了平均规划性能和行动措辞的稳健性。在与基于 RAEv2 的视频生成模型基线的匹配比较中，LaLa-World 达到了可比的规划性能，培训成本减少了 6 倍，规划速度加快了 13.9 美元。除了规划之外，预训练的 LaLa-World 还可作为 LaLa-Policy 的初始化，共同预测动作和未来的视觉表示。 LaLa-Policy 在 LIBERO 和 RoboCasa 上的表现与 Cosmos Policy 具有竞争力，推理速度提高了 5.5 美元\倍。

## 8. RoboJudge: Multimodal Language Models as Judges for Embodied Video Generation / RoboJudge：多模态语言模型作为具体视频生成的法官

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.27782v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27782v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.27782v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Video generation models are promising foundations for embodied world models, but only if their generated rollouts are physically valid and faithful to task instructions. Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) are increasingly used to judge these properties, yet their agreement with human judgments on embodied videos has not been systematically evaluated. To address this gap, we introduce RoboJudge-Bench, a benchmark for evaluating MLLMs as judges of embodied video generation. The benchmark measures two fundamental dimensions, Physical Adherence and Instruction Alignment, across 800 human-annotated videos from 8 source corpora and 10 video generators, spanning grippers, human hands, and dexterous hands. Experiments reveal uneven alignment between existing judges and human ratings: general-purpose MLLMs show lower agreement on physical adherence, while specialized video judges designed for general-domain physical reasoning do not consistently transfer to embodied scenarios. Motivated by these findings, we construct RoboJudge-Train, comprising 12K videos annotated with 25K human-verified scores and model-assisted rationales for Physical Adherence and Instruction Alignment. We fine-tune a 9B MLLM on this dataset with supervised learning followed by reinforcement learning, yielding RoboJudge-9B. Among the model judges evaluated on RoboJudge-Bench, RoboJudge-9B achieves the highest overall agreement, reaching a Pearson correlation of 0.719 with a statistically significant 0.089 gain over Gemini-3.7-Flash, the best-performing proprietary baseline in our evaluation. ## 13. Latent Projection for Latent Planning / 潜在规划的潜在预测

**Date**: 2026-10-19 | **arXiv**: [2610.26979v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26979v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.26979v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.RO

**Code**: https://github.com/yjang43/lp2.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Latent world models let an agent plan in latent space. The planner searches for an action sequence that minimizes a cost, and that cost is often the $\ell_2$ distance between the predicted terminal latent and the goal latent. The latent, however, is partly noise: the regularizer that prevents collapse during world model training promotes variance in the directions carrying no signal. We show that the same regularizer also supplies the structure to prune that noise in the latent. SIGReg drives the latent marginal to a standard Gaussian at every step, and assuming further that the latent sequence is a Gauss-Markov process, the transition is linear with its covariance pinned by the map itself, $z' \mid z \sim \mathcal{N}(Az,\, I - AA^\top)$. Fitting the state dynamics $A$ therefore yields the covariance at no additional cost, and in its eigenbasis each latent direction carries its own uncertainty. Furthermore, constraining $A$ to be a normal matrix fixes that basis across timesteps, so one eigenbasis measures the uncertainty over an arbitrary number of steps and serves as a stable basis to compute the planning cost on. We then restrict the $\ell_2$ cost to the $k$-dimensional subspace the state dynamics carry forward with the least uncertainty. The correction is post hoc: the encoder, the predictor, the rollout, and the planner are all left untouched, and only a single constrained matrix, $A$, is fit. We evaluate across six environments and show large gains on the 2D navigation tasks, TwoRoom and PointMaze, raising success rate over the plain $\ell_2$ cost by up to $+54.7$ points under a harder planning setting. Code is available at https://github.com/yjang43/lp2.

## 16. AnyGoal: On World Model Generalization Beyond Expert Rollouts / AnyGoal：超越专家推广的世界模型泛化

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

The goal of a world model is to capture the dynamics of an environment. This has the great appeal that it allows agents to plan and simulate a course of actions with the help of a world model. For these uses to be reliable, however, the learned dynamics must behave well beyond the specific state-action combinations observed in expert rollouts. Despite this, the relationship between data coverage, dynamics generalization, and downstream planning performance remains underexplored. We study this relationship through the lens of compositional generalization: can a world model recombine familiar state and action factors into valid dynamics? To answer this, we introduce AnyGoal, a benchmark of goal-shifted task variants built from widely used world-model environments, together with an open-source library. Each task is designed to be within reach of a world model that has learned the underlying dynamics: individual state factors and actions remain within their training support, but their combinations are underrepresented in expert trajectories. Across multiple environments and world model families, we find that current models consistently exhibit failures under these recombinations. By examining dynamics and representation failures across model families, interaction regimes, and evaluation metrics, we study where current world models generalize and where challenges remain for goal-shifted prediction and planning. Together, this framework, benchmark, and analysis provide a basis for evaluating progress toward world models that generalize to new planning tasks.

## 17. Agent World Model in the Wild: Computer-Use Agent Environment Synthesis / 真实的代理世界模型：计算机使用代理环境综合

**Date**: 2026-10-19 | **arXiv**: [2610.26828v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26828v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.26828v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.LG

**Code**: https://github.com/MSR-Orchard/WildAWM.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Agent learning is shifting from learning from human data toward learning through interaction with the world. However, scaling such learning directly in real-world computer systems is challenging and can introduce substantial security risks. We therefore propose Agent World Model in the Wild (WildAWM), a scalable pipeline for synthesizing controllable environments and tasks for training general computer-use agents (CUAs). Whereas existing approaches typically specialize in a single agent interaction interface, we construct executable environments that jointly support graphical user interfaces, model context protocol services, and command-line interfaces, allowing agents to flexibly combine visual interaction, tool use, and code execution. WildAWM is entirely powered by open-source models, demonstrating that high-quality computer-use environments can be generated without proprietary frontier models. Beyond environments, we address three recurring challenges in task synthesis: ensuring solvability, maintaining sufficient difficulty, and producing reliable verifiers, by introducing execution-based quality gates that combine heterogeneous agent rollouts, cross-judge consistency checks, and counterfactual probes of judgment robustness. The resulting pipeline synthesizes 341 long-horizon environment-task pairs that can require over 100 agent steps to solve and remain challenging even for frontier models, with GPT-6-Astra solving only 60% of them. We train two Qwen models of different sizes exclusively in these synthetic worlds. Both models achieve substantial gains across 8 out-of-distribution benchmarks spanning web, claw, and computer-use agents, demonstrating that learning in WildAWM's synthesized worlds generalizes broadly to unseen computer-use tasks. ## 18. Simulation Priors for Rapid Adaptation in Model-Based RL for Autonomous Racing / 自主赛车基于模型的强化学习中快速适应的仿真先验

**Date**: 2026-10-19 | **arXiv**: [2610.26757v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26757v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.26757v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.RO, eess.SY

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Reinforcement learning enables learning high-performance control policies, but its reliance on extensive interaction limits deployment on physical systems where data collection is costly, and failures are unsafe. We introduce Prior-Informed Adaptation (PIA), an offline-to-online model-based RL framework that uses structured simulation priors for rapid adaptation. PIA pretrains a world model on heterogeneous low- and high-fidelity simulated trajectories, then adapts it with limited target-domain interaction using uncertainty-regularized planning. Rather than requiring an accurate simulator for policy transfer, PIA treats simulated trajectories as biased priors whose residual mismatch is corrected during online adaptation. We evaluate PIA in autonomous racing using high-fidelity simulation and on a real full-size autonomous race car. In simulation, PIA adapts faster across track and surface shifts than strong baselines, achieving lower lap times and fewer failures. On hardware, PIA achieves reliable few-shot adaptation with less than one hour of on-vehicle interaction.
