## 1. ReactWorld: Structuring Interaction Responses for Embodied Video Generation / ReactWorld：为具体视频生成构建交互响应

**Date**: 2026-11-04 | **arXiv**: [2611.06120v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06120v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.06120v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Embodied video world models predict scene changes caused by robot actions, yet generated object responses may remain inconsistent with task intent and contact dynamics. Critical responses in embodied scenes often concentrate in local regions and specific time intervals. Inspired by chain-of-thought reasoning, we introduce ReactWorld, an embodied video generation model guided by structured interaction anticipation. In the generation backbone's feature space, an interaction response field predictor anticipates how the interaction will unfold, explicitly describing participant roles, future motion response intensity, and local motion changes. A response feedback adapter forms increments to object features from the difference between the object branch's outputs with predicted and zeroed local dynamics inputs. It also converts predicted displacements into positional biases for temporal attention. These two mechanisms guide object feature updates and information retrieval across frames, respectively. Through interaction anticipation and generative feedback, ReactWorld achieves an average score of 77.51 on RBench, outperforming PhysisForcing by 3.29 points with improvements in all nine categories. On EZS-Bench, it achieves a Domain score of 86.98 and an average score of 81.87.

体现的视频世界模型预测由机器人动作引起的场景变化，但生成的对象响应可能与任务意图和接触动态不一致。具身场景中的批判性反应通常集中在局部区域和特定时间间隔。受思想链推理的启发，我们引入了 ReactWorld，一种由结构化交互预期引导的具体视频生成模型。在生成主干的特征空间中，交互响应场预测器预测交互将如何展开，明确描述参与者角色、未来运动响应强度和局部运动变化。响应反馈适配器根据对象分支的输出与预测的和归零的局部动态输入之间的差异形成对象特征的增量。它还将预测的位移转换为位置偏差以进行时间注意力。这两种机制分别指导跨帧的对象特征更新和信息检索。通过交互预期和生成反馈，ReactWorld 在 RBench 上的平均得分为 77.51，比 PhysisForcing 高出 3.29 分，在所有九个类别上都有所改进。在 EZS-Bench 上，它的领域得分为 86.98，平均得分为 81.87。

</details>

## 2. Online Adaptation to Latent Physical Properties for Learning from Demonstration / 在线适应潜在物理特性以从演示中学习

**Date**: 2026-11-04 | **arXiv**: [2611.06118v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06118v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.06118v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

**Project**: https://isri-aist.github.io/latent-dynamics-project-page/  <details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Manipulating visually identical objects with different physical properties requires adapting actions based on information acquired through interaction. We address the problem of learning from demonstrations how to adapt manipulation actions, such as regrasping and changing contact locations, based on physical-property information acquired during interaction. To this end, we propose a learning-from-demonstration method that learns latent representations of physical properties needed to predict object responses and conditions a policy on online estimates of these representations. A dynamics model and object-specific latent representations are jointly learned from demonstrations that include behaviors adapting to the objects' physical properties, and the learned representations are then used to condition an imitation policy. During execution, the dynamics model and policy weights remain fixed, while the latent representation is updated based on the discrepancy between predicted and actual observations, allowing interaction with the object to inform action generation. Real-world and simulation experiments with objects whose centers of mass differ suggest that online adaptation through latent representations of physical properties can improve task success rates. However, in simulation, success rates on center-of-mass locations absent from the demonstrations remained around 30%, indicating limited adaptation to unseen physical properties. Project page: https://isri-aist.github.io/latent-dynamics-project-page/

操纵具有不同物理属性的视觉上相同的对象需要根据通过交互获取的信息来调整动作。我们解决了从演示中学习如何根据交互过程中获取的物理属性信息调整操纵动作的问题，例如重新抓取和改变接触位置。为此，我们提出了一种从演示中学习的方法，该方法可以学习预测对象响应所需的物理属性的潜在表示，并为这些表示的在线估计制定策略。动态模型和特定于对象的潜在表示是从包括适应对象物理属性的行为的演示中共同学习的，然后使用学习到的表示来调节模仿策略。在执行过程中，动态模型和策略权重保持固定，而潜在表示根据预测和实际观察之间的差异进行更新，从而允许与对象交互以通知动作生成。对质心不同的物体进行的现实世界和模拟实验表明，通过物理属性的潜在表示进行在线适应可以提高任务的成功率。然而，在模拟中，演示中未出现的质心位置的成功率仍保持在 30% 左右，这表明对看不见的物理特性的适应有限。项目页面：https://isri-aist.github.io/latent-dynamics-project-page/

## 3. TouchéWorld tech report : Towards Online Unified Camera--Hand Exploration in Generative Worlds / TouchéWorld 技术报告：迈向在线统一相机——生成世界中的手部探索

**Date**: 2026-11-04 | **arXiv**: [2611.06061v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06061v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.06061v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent video world models allow users to explore generated environments through camera navigation. Continuous hand motion provides a fine-grained action channel for interacting with the people and objects encountered during exploration. We present TouchéWorld, a framework that integrates gesture-driven interaction into a camera-controlled video world model. Given an initial image and a scene-description prompt, the model generates video conditioned on temporally aligned camera and hand controls. We construct joint camera--hand training data from heterogeneous egocentric videos through annotation completion, consistency-based filtering, and temporal alignment. Building on a pretrained camera-controlled world model, we combine spatial hand-skeleton conditioning with numerical hand-motion features through parameter-efficient adaptation. Causal adaptation and few-step distillation support chunk-level online generation, allowing users to update their controls based on generated visual feedback. We further introduce TouchéBench to evaluate hand-motion following, camera--hand coordination, online interaction, and object response. Together, these components establish a framework for studying continuous exploration and hand-driven interaction within generative worlds.

最近的视频世界模型允许用户通过相机导航探索生成的环境。连续的手部动作为与探索过程中遇到的人和物体进行交互提供了细粒度的动作通道。我们提出了 TouchéWorld，这是一个将手势驱动的交互集成到摄像机控制的视频世界模型中的框架。给定初始图像和场景描述提示，该模型会生成以时间对齐的摄像机和手动控制为条件的视频。我们通过注释完成、基于一致性的过滤和时间对齐从异构自我中心视频构建联合相机-手训练数据。基于预先训练的相机控制的世界模型，我们通过参数有效的适应将空间手部骨骼调节与数字手部运动特征结合起来。因果适应和几步蒸馏支持块级在线生成，允许用户根据生成的视觉反馈更新其控件。我们进一步引入 TouchéBench 来评估手部动作跟随、相机-手部协调、在线交互和物体响应。这些组件共同建立了一个框架，用于研究生成世界中的持续探索和手动驱动交互。

## 4. Does Your Trial Simulator Know What the Drug Does? An Intervention-Blind Test of Interventional Validity on 7,207 Registered Trials / 您的试验模拟器知道该药物的作用吗？对 7,207 项注册试验的干预有效性进行干预盲测

**Date**: 2026-11-04 | **arXiv**: [2611.05907v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05907v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05907v1)

**Categories**: stat.AP, cs.AI, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Patient world models are increasingly proposed as simulators of clinical trials, and recent surveys rank their capabilities on a ladder from temporal prediction to counterfactual rollouts. Position on that ladder is currently assigned by reading papers rather than by measurement. We turn the counterfactual rung into a measured quantity. From 30,119 completed phase 2/3 trials with results posted to ClinicalTrials.gov we construct 9,996 audited within-trial contrasts from 7,207 trials, and we score a simulator on how much of its accuracy depends on knowing which intervention was tested. The instrument is an intervention-blind reference: the same model, trained on the same trials, with every intervention-identifying field withheld. Interventional skill is the error the full model removes relative to that reference. On a temporal split (878 test contrasts from 655 trials) a registry-trained gradient-boosting simulator removes a fifth of the error of a trivial baseline, but its interventional skill is -0.12 (95% CI -0.21 to -0.05): withholding the intervention makes it better, and no alternative featurisation, stratum, temporal cut or same-molecule retrieval yields an interval above zero. A frontier language model reading only the registered protocol has interventional skill 0.20 [0.08, 0.31]; smaller and open-weight models sit at the limit of detection. Perturbation tests show the large model uses the intervention as such: swapping the arms reverses the sign of its prediction in 86% of contrasts. The blind reference is itself not drug-blind (a language model recovers the withheld drug class 45% of the time from the blind prompt), so interventional skill is a conservative lower bound. A reliability layer shows that ranking by predicted error lowers it but that calibrated error control does not transfer under temporal shift. Benchmark, code, prompts and per-call model outputs are released.

患者世界模型越来越多地被提议作为临床试验的模拟器，最近的调查将其能力按照从时间预测到反事实推出的阶梯进行排名。目前，该阶梯上的位置是通过阅读论文而不是通过测量来分配的。我们将反事实的梯级转化为可测量的数量。从 30,119 项已完成的 2/3 期试验并将结果发布到 ClinicalTrials.gov 中，我们从 7,207 项试验中构建了 9,996 项经审计的试验内对比，并根据其准确性在多大程度上取决于测试的干预措施来对模拟器进行评分。该工具是一个干预盲参考：相同的模型，在相同的试验中进行训练，并保留每个干预识别字段。干预技能是完整模型相对于该参考消除的误差。在时间分割（来自 655 项试验的 878 项测试对比）中，注册中心训练的梯度增强模拟器消除了微不足道基线的五分之一的误差，但其干预技能为 -0.12（95% CI -0.21 至 -0.05）：保留干预使其更好，并且没有其他特征化、分层、时间切割或同分子检索产生高于零的间隔。仅读取注册协议的前沿语言模型的干预技能为 0.20 [0.08, 0.31]；较小且开放重量的模型处于检测极限。扰动测试表明，大型模型使用了这样的干预措施：在 86% 的对比中，交换手臂会反转其预测的符号。盲参考本身并不是药物盲（语言模型从盲提示中恢复了 45% 的时间所保留的药物类别），因此介入技能是一个保守的下限。可靠性层显示，按预测误差排序会降低可靠性，但校准误差控制在时间偏移下不会转移。发布基准、代码、提示和每次调用模型输出。

## 5. CellCanon: An Agentic Cellular World Model for Prediction, Explanation, and Observation-Driven Updating / CellCanon：用于预测、解释和观察驱动更新的代理细胞世界模型

**Date**: 2026-11-04 | **arXiv**: [2611.05890v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05890v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05890v1)

**Categories**: q-bio.QM, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Biological discovery requires using molecular knowledge and new measurements to infer responses beyond the conditions already observed. We introduce CellCanon, the first agentic cellular world model to unify single-cell expression prediction, evidence-grounded explanation, and observation-driven updating. Its predictive core reuses condition-level biological evidence across gene-specific numerical readouts to guide single-cell expression generation. A shared world state links predictions, acquired measurements, and source-linked claims, supporting explanation and revision. On 339 held-out SciPlex3 conditions across three cell lines, CellCanon improves differential-expression AUPRC and Macro-F1 over the best baseline for each metric by 63.3% and 17.3%, respectively, and direction Macro-F1 by 7.2%. Relative to gene-wise reasoning on matched drug-cell cohorts, measured language-model token use falls by 99.77% for Predict and 98.41% for Explain. On the source-grounded benchmark, Explain correctly answers all answerable questions with supporting proofs and identifies all insufficient-evidence cases. In simulated experimental feedback, Update uses one measured source context per drug to exceed the best fine-tuned baselines in differential-expression AUPRC and Macro-F1 without changing model weights. Together, these capabilities support a cumulative account of cellular responses, with predictions and biological judgments that can be revised as evidence accumulates.

生物学发现需要使用分子知识和新的测量来推断超出已观察到的条件的反应。我们推出 CellCanon，这是第一个统一单细胞表达预测、基于证据的解释和观察驱动更新的代理细胞世界模型。其预测核心在基因特异性数值读数中重复使用条件级生物证据来指导单细胞表达生成。共享的世界状态将预测、获得的测量结果和源链接的声明联系起来，支持解释和修订。在三个细胞系的 339 个保留的 SciPlex3 条件下，CellCanon 将差异表达 AUPRC 和 Macro-F1 相对于每个指标的最佳基线分别提高了 63.3% 和 17.3%，将 Macro-F1 方向提高了 7.2%。相对于匹配药物细胞队列的基因推理，测得的预测语言模型标记使用率下降了 99.77%，解释语言模型标记使用率下降了 98.41%。在基于源的基准上，Explain 通过支持证据正确回答了所有可回答的问题，并识别了所有证据不足的案例。在模拟实验反馈中，Update 使用每种药物的一个测量源上下文来超越差异表达 AUPRC 和 Macro-F1 中的最佳微调基线，而无需更改模型权重。这些功能共同支持细胞反应的累积记录，并可以随着证据的积累而修改预测和生物学判断。

## 6. Time-to-Goal: Learned Temporal Metrics for Long-Horizon Planning with JEPA World Models / 实现目标的时间：使用 JEPA 世界模型学习长期规划的时间指标

**Date**: 2026-11-04 | **arXiv**: [2611.05847v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05847v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05847v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) support planning by comparing predicted latent states with an encoded goal. For this comparison to guide temporal progress, states closer in time to the goal should receive lower costs. However, training representations for prediction and distribution regularization does not directly enforce this ordering. We examine this mismatch in flat LeWorldModel (LeWM) by analyzing how its Euclidean goal cost changes with temporal separation. Our analysis identifies conditions under which states at different long offsets receive similar average costs, weakening their temporal distinction. To learn the missing ordering explicitly, we introduce Time-to-Goal (T2G), a lightweight metric trained on latent triples from offline trajectories. A margin ranking loss encourages smaller distances for temporally nearer states, requiring no rewards or policy labels. The learned metric then replaces the Euclidean planning cost while the JEPA encoder and predictor remain frozen. Diagnostics on AntMaze and FourRoom show that T2G preserves stronger temporal contrast and improves near--far ranking at long offsets. Across four navigation and manipulation benchmarks, T2G improves the average planning success rate from 32.7\% (flat LeWM) to 63.0\% and approaches or even surpasses the performance of the hierarchical H-JEPA baselines. These results show that learning temporal ordering for the goal cost can substantially improve latent planning even with a flat world model.

联合嵌入预测架构 (JEPA) 通过将预测的潜在状态与编码目标进行比较来支持规划。为了通过这种比较来指导时间进展，距离目标较近的国家应该获得较低的成本。然而，预测和分布正则化的训练表示并不直接强制执行这种排序。我们通过分析其欧几里得目标成本如何随时间分离变化来检查扁平 LeWorldModel (LeWM) 中的这种不匹配。我们的分析确定了不同长偏移量的国家获得相似平均成本的条件，从而削弱了它们的时间区别。为了明确地学习缺失的顺序，我们引入了目标时间（T2G），这是一种针对离线轨迹中的潜在三元组进行训练的轻量级度量。边际排名损失鼓励暂时较近的州缩小距离，不需要奖励或政策标签。然后，学习的指标将取代欧几里得规划成本，而 JEPA 编码器和预测器保持冻结状态。 AntMaze 和 FourRoom 上的诊断表明，T2G 保留了更强的时间对比度，并提高了长偏移时的近-远排名。在四个导航和操纵基准中，T2G 将平均规划成功率从 32.7%（平坦 LeWM）提高到 63.0%，接近甚至超过分层 H-JEPA 基准的性能。这些结果表明，即使使用平坦的世界模型，学习目标成本的时间排序也可以显着改善潜在规划。

</details>

## 7. Readable Is Not Steerable: Pre-Registered Tests of Physical Structure in Learned Fluid Representations / 可读不可操纵：学习流体表示中物理结构的预先注册测试

**Date**: 2026-11-03 | **arXiv**: [2611.05577v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05577v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05577v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI, physics.flu-dyn

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Self-supervised world models are often said to organize their latents around physical quantities. We test this on 2D decaying Navier-Stokes flow in three pre-registered studies, each tested once on held-out data. In Study 1, a joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA) trained to predict the flow fails the pre-registered criteria for organizing its latent around fluid quantities better than an untrained network of the same architecture (not supported); a JEPA trained on non-physical linear dynamics and a static invariance model that never sees time do as well or better. In Study 2, plain autoencoders that render the field make enstrophy prominent in their slowest unsupervised latent directions (best r 0.80-0.86 vs 0.38 untrained; fresh seeds, sealed data). Adding VICReg lowers this prominence in every comparison, though not by the pre-registered margin for vorticity, while raising the autoencoders' linear decodability (partially supported). In Study 3, we build steerable directions ("dials") by routing: the decoder gets overall scale only through a small conditioning code, while its other pathway carries a degraded copy of spectral shape. Label-free rules find a loudness dial in all 8 trained models and a shape dial in 3 of 5 fresh seeds on sealed data, against a pre-registered bar of 4; neither control shows a shape dial (partially supported). Across the studies, what a latent contains and what can be controlled through it come apart. In exploration, a supervised direction reading spectral shape almost perfectly moved nothing when steered. During the build, the loudness dial had to be found by its effect on the output (closest to a pure rescaling), not by data variance; for shape, a variance rule and an effect-constrained rule each worked in 3 of 5 seeds. The program used about 70 logged hours on one consumer GPU; all pre-registrations, deviations, code and outputs are public.

人们常说，自我监督的世界模型是围绕物理量来组织其潜力的。我们在三项预先注册的研究中对二维衰减纳维-斯托克斯流进行了测试，每项研究都对保留的数据进行了一次测试。在研究 1 中，经过训练来预测流量的联合嵌入预测架构 (JEPA) 未达到预先注册的标准，无法比相同架构的未经训练的网络更好地围绕流体量组织其潜在网络（不支持）；一个接受非物理线性动力学训练的 JEPA 和一个静态不变性模型，从来没有看到时间表现得那么好或更好。在研究 2 中，渲染该场的普通自动编码器使熵在最慢的无监督潜在方向上突出（最佳 r 0.80-0.86 vs 未经训练的 0.38；新鲜种子，密封数据）。添加 VICReg 会降低每次比较中的这种显着性，尽管不是通过预先注册的涡度余量，同时提高自动编码器的线性可解码性（部分支持）。在研究 3 中，我们通过路由构建可操纵的方向（“刻度盘”）：解码器仅通过小的调节代码获得整体尺度，而其其他路径则承载频谱形状的降级副本。无标签规则在所有 8 个经过训练的模型中找到响度刻度盘，并在密封数据上的 5 个新鲜种子中的 3 个中找到形状刻度盘，而预先注册的条形为 4；两个控件都不显示形状表盘（部分支持）。在所有研究中，潜伏包含什么以及可以通过它控制什么是分开的。在探索过程中，受监督的方向读取光谱形状在转向时几乎完美地没有移动任何东西。在构建过程中，必须通过其对输出的影响（最接近纯粹的重新缩放）而不是数据方差来找到响度刻度盘；对于形状，方差规则和效果约束规则分别适用于 5 个种子中的 3 个。该程序在一个消费级 GPU 上使用了大约 70 个记录小时；所有预注册、偏差、代码和输出都是公开的。

## 8. Jev with Foresight: Safe Language-Guided Autonomous Driving via a Language World Model / 有远见的 Jev：通过语言世界模型实现安全的语言引导自动驾驶

**Date**: 2026-11-03 | **arXiv**: [2611.05448v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05448v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05448v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI

**Project**: https://ys-qu.github.io/jev-ad-website.  <details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large language models understand passengers' driving commands, but as driving agents they reason in generated text for seconds and must infer each action's consequences implicitly from the scene. We present Jev-AD, which pairs Jev, a typed decision model that answers predefined questions with probabilities, not generated text, with a language world model that predicts for every candidate action how nearby vehicles move over the next two seconds. Code turns these predictions into foreseen consequences, which Jev compares instead of inferring them from coordinates. In simulated highway merging (500 car-episodes), this foresight lowers the same decision model's collision rate from 26.0% to 2.4% at 0.25 seconds per decision, and Jev-AD achieves the passenger's intent in 93% of free-form command trials.
