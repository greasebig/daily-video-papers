## 1. GuidedWAM: Training-Free Energy Guidance for World Action Models in Autonomous Driving / GuidedWAM：自动驾驶世界行动模型的免培训能源指南

**Date**: 2026-11-05 | **arXiv**: [2611.07912v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07912v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07912v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World action models (WAMs) for autonomous driving couple the driving policy and the visual world model in a single generative network whose objectives are fixed post-training. Conditioning on a new driving objective requires retraining. We study energy guidance as a general mechanism and present GuidedWAM, a model-agnostic framework for injecting driving objectives into frozen WAMs at sampling time. Our framework constructs each objective as differentiable energies on the sampler's intermediate clean-action estimate, which needs only forward access to the frozen model, and is stabilized by sampling-time guidance scheduling, per-energy normalization, and velocity-bounded correction. On the NAVSIM v2 hard-split, guidance improves all three frozen, publicly released WAMs by 1.8 to 3.6 EPDMS, averaged over three sampler seeds, and adds less than 10% to the sampling time per step. These results position energy guidance as a practical post-training control channel for WAMs.

自动驾驶的世界行动模型（WAM）将驾驶策略和视觉世界模型结合在一个生成网络中，其目标在训练后是固定的。适应新的驾驶目标需要重新培训。我们将能量引导作为一种通用机制进行研究，并提出了 GuidedWAM，这是一种与模型无关的框架，用于在采样时将驱动目标注入冻结的 WAM 中。我们的框架将每个目标构建为采样器中间清洁动作估计上的可微分能量，仅需要前向访问冻结模型，并通过采样时间引导调度、每能量归一化和速度有界校正来稳定。在 NAVSIM v2 硬分割上，指南将所有三个冻结的、公开发布的 WAM 改进了 1.8 至 3.6 EPDMS（三个采样器种子的平均值），并且每步采样时间增加了不到 10%。这些结果将能量引导定位为 WAM 的实用训练后控制通道。

## 2. PlayGen2: Learning Real-Time Playable Worlds with Explicit 3D State / PlayGen2：通过显式 3D 状态学习实时可玩世界

**Date**: 2026-11-05 | **arXiv**: [2611.07867v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07867v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07867v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Building a playable world requires more than generating plausible video: it demands responsive control, persistent spatial structure, and coherent interactions with dynamic entities. Using generated frames as the sole recurrent world representation ties these requirements to view-dependent appearance synthesis, making it difficult to maintain long-term spatial and temporal consistency in generation and interaction. We present PlayGen2, a real-time interactive world model that separates learned 3D state evolution from neural rendering. PlayGen2 maintains a persistent world memory comprising static geometry represented by a volumetric occupancy grid and identity-indexed 3D states for the player and other entities. Within a fixed-size local region, its world-state model jointly learns action-conditioned entity dynamics, geometry completion, and entity discovery, committing updates to a persistent world memory. A separate neural renderer synthesizes first-person observations from depth and semantic projections of the world state, without feeding generated images back into state prediction. Using Doom as a testbed, we demonstrate that the model successfully learns world dynamics including geometry-aware player motion and player--NPC interactions. The framework is computationally efficient, enabling the complete simulation and rendering pipeline to achieve approximately 160ms backend latency on a MacBook Pro with an M4 Pro chip. Beyond single-player interaction, we demonstrate zero-shot deployment on unseen Doom maps, multiplayer interaction through the composition of single-player models, and programmable gameplay through rule-based state interventions. These capabilities demonstrate the transferability and extensibility of the learned game-world model. Code and pretrained model weights are publicly available.

构建一个可玩的世界需要的不仅仅是生成合理的视频：它需要响应控制、持久的空间结构以及与动态实体的连贯交互。使用生成的帧作为唯一的循环世界表示将这些要求与依赖于视图的外观合成联系起来，使得很难在生成和交互中保持长期的空间和时间一致性。我们提出了 PlayGen2，一种实时交互式世界模型，它将学习的 3D 状态演化与神经渲染分开。 PlayGen2 维护持久的世界记忆，其中包括由体积占用网格表示的静态几何图形以及玩家和其他实体的身份索引 3D 状态。在固定大小的局部区域内，其世界状态模型共同学习动作条件实体动力学、几何补全和实体发现，并将更新提交到持久的世界记忆。一个单独的神经渲染器从世界状态的深度和语义投影中合成第一人称观察，而不将生成的图像反馈回状态预测。使用《Doom》作为测试平台，我们证明该模型成功学习了世界动态，包括几何感知的玩家运动和玩家与 NPC 交互。该框架计算效率高，使完整的模拟和渲染管道能够在配备 M4 Pro 芯片的 MacBook Pro 上实现大约 160 毫秒的后端延迟。除了单人交互之外，我们还演示了在未见过的 Doom 地图上的零镜头部署、通过单人模型的组合进行多人交互，以及通过基于规则的状态干预进行可编程游戏玩法。这些功能展示了所学习的游戏世界模型的可迁移性和可扩展性。代码和预训练模型权重是公开的。

## 3. Learning Robotic World Models with Semantic and Visual Feedback / 通过语义和视觉反馈学习机器人世界模型

**Date**: 2026-11-05 | **arXiv**: [2611.07850v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07850v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07850v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Robot world models predict action-conditioned future visual states to support robotic planning and decision making. However, visual reconstruction does not fully capture task progress, semantic evaluation alone provides limited constraints on fine-grained visual errors, and homogeneous predictions limit comparative feedback. We present RoboImagine, a framework for post-training autoregressive robotic world models through candidate-future comparisons with semantic and visual feedback. Diverse search promotes diversity among candidate trajectories under shared visual observations, action sequences, and task instructions. A frozen multimodal evaluator provides task-relevant semantic preferences through pairwise video comparisons in both presentation orders, complemented by pixel- and perceptual-level visual consistency feedback that constrains local structural and appearance errors. These signals drive policy optimization through group-relative advantages. Fixed initial context and branch-specific rolling histories support long-sequence generation with bounded context and consistent conditioning between candidate generation and policy optimization. Experiments with separately trained models on RT-1, BridgeV2, and RoboCasa cover real-world and simulated manipulation. On RT-1, under matched initialization, update counts, and evaluation protocols, RoboImagine improves PSNR by 1.26 dB and reduces LPIPS by 11.93% relative to continued supervised fine-tuning. Ablations show that diverse search improves prediction quality and that combined semantic and visual feedback yields higher external multimodal evaluation scores than visual feedback alone while maintaining comparable visual fidelity. Rolling histories further improve long-horizon prediction. These results support improving visual fidelity, task relevance, and long-horizon prediction through candidate comparison and complementary feedback.

机器人世界模型预测以动作为条件的未来视觉状态，以支持机器人规划和决策。然而，视觉重建并不能完全捕获任务进度，语义评估本身对细粒度视觉错误的限制有限，同质预测限制了比较反馈。我们提出了 RoboImagine，这是一个通过语义和视觉反馈与候选未来进行比较来训练后自回归机器人世界模型的框架。多样化搜索促进了共享视觉观察、动作序列和任务指令下候选轨迹的多样性。冻结的多模态评估器通过两种呈现顺序的成对视频比较来提供与任务相关的语义偏好，并辅以限制局部结构和外观错误的像素和感知级视觉一致性反馈。这些信号通过集团相对优势推动政策优化。固定的初始上下文和特定于分支的滚动历史支持具有有限上下文的长序列生成以及候选生成和策略优化之间的一致条件。在 RT-1、BridgeV2 和 RoboCasa 上单独训练的模型的实验涵盖了真实世界和模拟操作。在 RT-1 上，在匹配的初始化、更新计数和评估协议下，相对于持续监督微调，RoboImagine 将 PSNR 提高了 1.26 dB，并将 LPIPS 降低了 11.93%。消融表明，多样化的搜索提高了预测质量，并且语义和视觉反馈的结合产生了比单独的视觉反馈更高的外部多模态评估分数，同时保持了相当的视觉保真度。滚动历史进一步改善了长期预测。这些结果支持通过候选比较和补充反馈来提高视觉保真度、任务相关性和长期预测。

## 4. Seeing Beyond the Next Step: World-Model-Guided Human-Like Navigation in Multi-Agent Scenes / 展望下一步：多智能体场景中世界模型引导的类人导航

**Date**: 2026-11-05 | **arXiv**: [2611.07730v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07730v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07730v1)

**Categories**: cs.GR, cs.AI, cs.CV, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Selecting a next motion in a populated scene is not just a matter of avoiding what is currently in the way: an action can be locally feasible yet drift onto the road instead of the crosswalk, cut into a stream of crossing pedestrians, or bottleneck a narrow passage. These failure modes are invisible from the current state alone, and acting on them requires reasoning about what each candidate action will cause. World models offer exactly this, namely action-conditioned predictions of the future, but rolling them out at every decision step is a cost deployable navigation systems cannot pay, and the field has largely traded foresight for speed. We argue this is a false dichotomy. The natural place for a world model in human-aware navigation is at training time, as a preference oracle that distills foresight into a fast policy, rather than at inference time, as an online planner. We pair a discrete visual action prior with two task-aligned world models, one for nearby social dynamics and one for future scene semantics, that score candidate actions by their predicted consequences and refine the policy through group-relative preference distillation. At deployment, both world models are removed and the policy acts in a single forward pass. Across five held-out evaluation scenarios, this yields navigation that is simultaneously safer, more socially and group-coherent, and more goal-directed than strong reactive and learned baselines, with naive observers rating the resulting motion as more human-like. Taken together, our findings point to a different approach for bringing world-model foresight into deployable agents: not accelerating rollouts, but amortizing them into the policy so that the cost of looking ahead is confined to training. Project page: https://seoneun.github.io/-NeurIPS-2026-Seeing-Beyond-the-Next-Step/

在人口稠密的场景中选择下一个动作不仅仅是避免当前障碍物的问题：动作可以是局部可行的，但会漂移到道路上而不是人行横道上，切入过路的行人流中，或者阻碍狭窄的通道。这些故障模式仅从当前状态来看是不可见的，对它们采取行动需要推理每个候选操作将导致什么。世界模型正是提供了这一点，即对未来的行动条件预测，但在每个决策步骤中推出它们是可部署导航系统无法支付的成本，并且该领域在很大程度上用远见换取了速度。我们认为这是错误的二分法。在人类感知导航中，世界模型的自然位置是在训练时，作为将远见提炼为快速策略的偏好预言机，而不是在推理时作为在线规划器。我们将离散视觉动作先验与两个与任务相关的世界模型配对，一个用于附近的社会动态，一个用于未来场景语义，根据预测的结果对候选动作进行评分，并通过群体相对偏好蒸馏来完善策略。在部署时，两个世界模型都被删除，并且策略在单个前向传递中起作用。在五个保留的评估场景中，这产生的导航比强反应性和学习基线更安全、更具有社交性和群体一致性，并且更有目标导向，天真的观察者认为最终的运动更类似于人类。总而言之，我们的研究结果指出了将世界模型的远见引入可部署代理的不同方法：不是加速部署，而是将其分摊到政策中，以便将前瞻性的成本限制在培训上。项目页面：https://seoneun.github.io/-NeurIPS-2026-Seeing-Beyond-the-Next-Step/

## 5. MAC-v2: Long-horizon model-based value expansion from pixels / MAC-v2：基于长视野模型的像素值扩展

**Date**: 2026-11-04 | **arXiv**: [2611.07245v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07245v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07245v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We study whether \emph{long-horizon model-based value expansion} can serve as an effective framework in pixel-based offline reinforcement learning (RL). Long-horizon model-based value expansion from pixels is substantially harder than state-based environments, as the agent must learn both the representation and its dynamics, and errors from either can accumulate recursively over imagined trajectories. We introduce MAC-v2, which enables long-horizon model-based expansion from pixels by training a reconstruction-free latent world model with action chunking. By learning representations from prediction and control objectives, MAC-v2 does not need to encode control-irrelevant visual details that can hinder the dynamics prediction. Action chunking further mitigates the prediction error by reducing the number of recursive model predictions required for value expansion. Across diverse visual benchmarks including visual OGBench, MMBench2, Robomimic, and LIBERO-10, MAC-v2 achieves strong performance against model-free and model-based offline RL baselines. Our analyses show that long-horizon model-based value expansion is critical for the performance of MAC-v2, and that both action chunking and latent learning objectives are essential for reliable long-horizon latent imagination.

我们研究\emph{基于长视野模型的价值扩展}是否可以作为基于像素的离线强化学习（RL）的有效框架。从像素进行基于长视野模型的值扩展比基于状态的环境要困难得多，因为代理必须学习表示及其动态，并且两者的错误都可以在想象的轨迹上递归累积。我们引入了 MAC-v2，它通过训练具有动作分块的免重建潜在世界模型，实现从像素进行基于长视野模型的扩展。通过学习预测和控制目标的表示，MAC-v2 不需要对可能阻碍动态预测的与控制无关的视觉细节进行编码。动作分块通过减少价值扩展所需的递归模型预测的数量来进一步减轻预测误差。在包括视觉 OGBench、MMBench2、Robomimic 和 LIBERO-10 在内的各种视觉基准测试中，MAC-v2 相对于无模型和基于模型的离线 RL 基线实现了强大的性能。我们的分析表明，基于长期模型的价值扩展对于 MAC-v2 的性能至关重要，并且动作分块和潜在学习目标对于可靠的长期潜在想象力至关重要。

## 6. Detecting Silent Action-Interface Mismatch with Calibrated Relational World Models / 使用校准的关系世界模型检测无声动作界面不匹配

**Date**: 2026-11-04 | **arXiv**: [2611.06653v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06653v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.06653v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

A robot action can satisfy shape and range constraints yet carry the wrong semantics: axes may be permuted, signs inverted, rotations rescaled, or gripper polarity reversed. We study learned state-action-effect relations as offline contracts for detecting these mismatches and ranking their likely fields. Clean held-out transitions calibrate alarms; a fixed inverse vocabulary supports closed-set localization. On ten LIBERO Spatial tasks comprising 432 source episodes, a capacity-controlled comparison at 2.5% empirical clean false-positive rate gives 46.1% recall for a ridge forward residual, 61.0% for a four-component joint Gaussian mixture, and 65.7% for the exact conditional of that same mixture. The joint score retains a 14.9 percentage-point advantage over ridge, while conditioning improves recall by a further 4.7 points. Joint scoring instead has higher localization: 95.8% versus 94.6%. These matched-rate comparisons are retrospective diagnostics, not deployment calibration guarantees. Earlier Object/Goal results broaden tasks within the LIBERO convention; DROID, all three HYDRA tasks, short temporal faults, small offsets, exact repair, and simulated mitigation fail their respective gates, while xArm contributes one task only. The evidence supports a bounded offline diagnostic and a detection-localization tradeoff, rather than joint-density superiority or cross-robot safety.

机器人动作可以满足形状和范围约束，但带有错误的语义：轴可能被排列、符号反转、旋转重新缩放或夹具极性反转。我们将学习到的状态-行动-效果关系作为离线合约来研究，以检测这些不匹配并对它们可能的领域进行排名。干净的保留转换校准警报；固定的逆词汇表支持闭集定位。在包含 432 个源集的 10 个 LIBERO Spatial 任务中，以 2.5% 的经验干净假阳性率进行容量控制比较，对脊前向残差给出 46.1% 的召回率，对四分量联合高斯混合给出 61.0% 的召回率，对同一混合物的精确条件给出 65.7% 的召回率。联合得分比 ridge 保持 14.9 个百分点的优势，而条件反射则进一步提高了 4.7 个百分点。相反，联合评分具有更高的本地化：95.8% 与 94.6%。这些匹配率比较是回顾性诊断，而不是部署校准保证。早期的对象/目标结果拓宽了 LIBERO 公约内的任务； DROID、所有三个 HYDRA 任务、短时故障、小偏移、精确修复和模拟缓解都失败了各自的门，而 xArm 仅贡献了一项任务。证据支持有界离线诊断和检测定位权衡，而不是联合密度优势或跨机器人安全性。

## 7. Synthesizing Dexterous Robot Demonstrations from Human Videos via Virtual Try-On / 通过虚拟试穿从人类视频中合成灵巧机器人演示

**Date**: 2026-11-03 | **arXiv**: [2611.05163v3](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05163v3) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05163v3)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Robot foundation models have shown remarkable performance in robot control, yet adapting them to a new task still relies heavily on teleoperated demonstrations. Egocentric human videos offer a scalable substitute, but overlaying rendered robots on human videos or retargeting human poses into robot actions leaves significant visual or action gaps for high-DoF robot embodiments. To address this limitation, we propose RoboTryOn, which treats human-to-robot translation as virtual try-on: try-on keeps the person and swaps the garment, RoboTryOn keeps the demonstrated motion and swaps the human for the robot. Specifically, RoboTryOn converts a human video into a video of the target robot specified by a reference image, while preserving the original task, objects, and background. To maintain visual consistency, we translate selected keyframes autoregressively and use them to guide video synthesis with motion conditioned on the human hand skeleton. Additionally, we use an inverse dynamics model (IDM) that incorporates depth features to infer action labels from synthesized videos for policy learning. Our experiments across multiple humanoid embodiments show that policies trained with our synthetic data improve the average success rate on novel tasks, including challenging bimanual manipulation, from 2% to 60%, without any real robot demonstration of those tasks. Moreover, training with our synthetic data matches the performance of policies trained solely on teleoperated demonstrations at approximately a third of the collection cost, demonstrating the cost efficiency of our approach.

机器人基础模型在机器人控制方面表现出了卓越的性能，但使其适应新任务仍然在很大程度上依赖于远程操作演示。以自我为中心的人类视频提供了一种可扩展的替代品，但将渲染的机器人叠加在人类视频上或将人类姿势重新定位为机器人动作，为高自由度机器人实施例留下了显着的视觉或动作间隙。为了解决这个限制，我们提出了 RoboTryOn，它将人到机器人的翻译视为虚拟试穿：试穿保留人并交换服装，RoboTryOn 保留演示的动作并将人类交换为机器人。具体来说，RoboTryOn 将人类视频转换为参考图像指定的目标机器人的视频，同时保留原始任务、对象和背景。为了保持视觉一致性，我们以自回归方式翻译选定的关键帧，并使用它们来指导以人手骨骼为条件的运动的视频合成。此外，我们使用逆动态模型（IDM），该模型结合了深度特征，从合成视频中推断动作标签以进行策略学习。我们在多个人形实施例中进行的实验表明，使用我们的合成数据训练的策略将新任务（包括具有挑战性的双手操作）的平均成功率从 2% 提高到 60%，而无需任何真实的机器人演示这些任务。此外，使用我们的合成数据进行的训练与仅在远程操作演示上训练的策略的性能相匹配，其收集成本约为收集成本的三分之一，这证明了我们方法的成本效率。
