## 1. The (Butter)fly Dream: Linear Separability of World-Model Rollouts in a Connectome-Constrained Visual Model / （蝴蝶）苍蝇梦想：连接组约束视觉模型中世界模型推出的线性可分离性

**Date**: 2026-11-01 | **arXiv**: [2611.01866v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01866v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01866v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, q-bio.NC

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Zhuangzi dreamt he was a butterfly and, on waking, could not tell which was the dream. We ask a version of his question of a model of the fly brain: placed inside a world generated by a world model, can a fixed brain tell that the world is a dream? We treat it as a question about what a connectome-constrained model trained on one task makes available beyond that task: do its responses make the defects of a dream linearly decodable? The observer is a published model of the fly visual system trained only for optic-flow estimation; reality is the MPI-Sintel movie on the model's 721-column retina; dreams are continuations of five real frames by three small world models (a persistence random walk, an MDN-RNN and an RSSM) at three temperatures. The judge is a classifier two-sample test on temporal statistics of cell-type responses, with sequence-level cross-validation. In the trained observer's responses all nine dreams are separable from reality (AUC 0.85-0.99, mean over 50 trained models), in those of the same network before training much less so (0.58-0.75 for the learned world models; paired difference 0.20-0.37), equally on sequences the observer never saw, and in an order unrelated to pixel error. Because these AUCs are near ceiling, we add graded probes made of each window's own real frames with only their order broken. Swapping one adjacent pair of the 14 generated frames gives 0.73 on the trained responses against 0.54 before training and 0.56 for pixel statistics; the trained curve reaches 0.75 at 1.1 swapped pairs of seven, the pixel statistics at 4.7, and the untrained network not at all. A detection horizon, the test on the first k generated frames, orders the world models where the full-length AUC saturates.

庄子梦见自己是一只蝴蝶，醒来后分不清是梦还是梦。我们问他的苍蝇大脑模型问题的一个版本：放置在由世界模型生成的世界中，固定的大脑能否判断出这个世界是一个梦？我们将其视为一个问题，即在一项任务上训练的连接组约束模型可以在该任务之外提供什么：它的反应是否使梦的缺陷可以线性解码？观察者是已发布的飞行视觉系统模型，仅针对光流估计进行训练；现实是模型 721 列视网膜上的 MPI-Sintel 电影；梦是三个小世界模型（持久性随机游走、MDN-RNN 和 RSSM）在三种温度下对五个真实框架的延续。判断是对细胞类型响应的时间统计进行分类器双样本测试，并进行序列级交叉验证。在受过训练的观察者的反应中，所有九个梦都与现实分离（AUC 0.85-0.99，超过 50 个经过训练的模型的平均值），在训练之前的同一网络中，情况要小得多（对于学习的世界模型为 0.58-0.75；配对差异 0.20-0.37），在观察者从未见过的序列上同样如此，并且顺序与像素误差无关。因为这些 AUC 接近上限，所以我们添加了由每个窗口自己的真实框架组成的分级探针，仅破坏了它们的顺序。交换 14 个生成帧中的一对相邻帧，训练后的响应为 0.73，训练前为 0.54，像素统计为 0.56；训练曲线在 1.1 交换七对时达到 0.75，像素统计数据为 4.7，而未经训练的网络则根本没有。检测范围（对前 k 个生成帧的测试）对全长 AUC 饱和的世界模型进行排序。

## 2. When Does Latent Prediction Error Support Physical Surprise Claims in Video World Models? / 潜在预测误差何时支持视频世界模型中的物理惊喜声明？

**Date**: 2026-11-01 | **arXiv**: [2611.01815v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01815v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01815v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Prediction errors from video world models are often treated as physical surprise, but they can also reflect appearance, static content, motion, or ordinary prediction difficulty. We present an evaluation framework that separates what frozen representations contain, how they organize appearance and physical differences, and what changes a model's own prediction error. On IntPhys2, a benchmark of physically possible and impossible events, we compare representations from latent prediction, masked pixel reconstruction, and simple visual statistics. Appearance remains easy to decode, while V-JEPA2 exposes modest information about whether events are possible or impossible. Looking at the latent geometry, appearance changes become smaller compared with differences between videos, their directions line up more consistently across scenes, and their overlap with physical changes decreases in deeper layers. Across action-conditioned models, incorrect actions raise prediction error, while appearance changes also produce substantial responses. In Physion++ video clips, repeating a single frame can produce higher error than the original clip, while changing color throughout the clip can lower it. Using Kubric, a simulation and rendering framework, we find that impossible events can produce lower error than possible events. A fixed error threshold also incorrectly flags many possible events as impossible. A model can therefore represent physical information and respond to incorrect actions without its native prediction error reliably measuring physical surprise.

视频世界模型的预测错误通常被视为物理意外，但它们也可以反映外观、静态内容、运动或普通预测难度。我们提出了一个评估框架，该框架区分了冻结表示所包含的内容、它们如何组织外观和物理差异，以及模型自身预测误差的变化。在 IntPhys2（物理上可能和不可能事件的基准）上，我们比较了潜在预测、掩模像素重建和简单视觉统计的表示。外观仍然易于解码，而 V-JEPA2 公开了有关事件是否可能或不可能的适度信息。观察潜在的几何形状，与视频之间的差异相比，外观变化变得更小，它们的方向在场景中排列得更加一致，并且它们与物理变化的重叠在更深的层中减少。在动作条件模型中，不正确的动作会增加预测误差，而外观变化也会产生实质性的反应。在 Physion++ 视频剪辑中，重复单个帧可能会产生比原始剪辑更高的错误，而更改整个剪辑的颜色可以降低错误。使用模拟和渲染框架 Kubric，我们发现不可能的事件比可能的事件产生的错误要低。固定的错误阈值还会错误地将许多可能的事件标记为不可能的事件。因此，模型可以表示物理信息并对不正确的动作做出响应，而无需其固有的预测误差可靠地测量物理惊喜。

</details>

## 3. Robust EEG-based Emotion Recognition from Coarse Trial-level Labels under Intra-trial Affective Variability / 试验内情感变异下粗略试验水平标签的基于脑电图的鲁棒情绪识别

**Date**: 2026-11-01 | **arXiv**: [2611.01731v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01731v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01731v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

EEG-based emotion recognition has become an active research area in brain-computer interfaces. However, fine-grained affective annotation is costly, so most datasets provide only a global label for each long EEG trial. For practical emotion recognition, trials are commonly divided into short temporal windows, with the global label propagated to all windows for training. This practice treats trial-level emotion as temporally uniform, although affective responses may fluctuate within a trial. Consequently, windows that weakly reflect or deviate from the global emotional state may receive unreliable labels, posing a key challenge to robust EEG-based emotion recognition. To address this challenge, we propose VARY, a variability-aware robust learning framework for EEG-based emotion recognition from coarse trial-level labels. VARY incorporates a Two-stage Sample Selection (TSS) strategy to identify EEG windows with reliable emotional labels, and introduces MixAug, a data augmentation method tailored for EEG to improve model robustness under limited and unreliable supervision. We comprehensively evaluate VARY under the setting of label-noise learning on two public datasets, using three network architectures and multiple label-noise settings. Our method demonstrates superior performance, achieving a maximum accuracy gain of 11.54% on the DREAMER dataset and 5.39% on the SEED dataset compared with the best-performing baseline, highlighting the effectiveness of VARY in learning from unreliable trial-level labels under intra-trial affective variability.

基于脑电图的情绪识别已成为脑机接口领域的一个活跃研究领域。然而，细粒度的情感注释成本高昂，因此大多数数据集仅为每个长期脑电图试验提供全局标签。对于实际的情绪识别，试验通常分为短时间窗口，将全局标签传播到所有窗口进行训练。这种做法将试验水平的情绪视为暂时一致的，尽管情感反应可能在试验内波动。因此，弱反映或偏离全局情绪状态的窗口可能会收到不可靠的标签，这对基于脑电图的强大情绪识别提出了关键挑战。为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了 VARY，一种可变性感知的鲁棒学习框架，用于从粗略的试验级标签中进行基于脑电图的情感识别。 VARY 采用两阶段样本选择 (TSS) 策略来识别具有可靠情绪标签的脑电图窗口，并引入了 MixAug，这是一种为脑电图量身定制的数据增强方法，可在有限且不可靠的监督下提高模型的稳健性。我们使用三种网络架构和多个标签噪声设置，在两个公共数据集上的标签噪声学习设置下全面评估 VARY。我们的方法表现出卓越的性能，与表现最佳的基线相比，在 DREAMER 数据集上实现了 11.54% 的最大准确度增益，在 SEED 数据集上实现了 5.39% 的最大准确度增益，突显了 VARY 在试验内情感变异下从不可靠的试验级标签中学习的有效性。

</details>

## 4. Xiaomi Auto World Model 2.0: Causal World Models for Real-World Driving and Embodied Intelligence / 小米汽车世界模型2.0：现实世界驾驶和具身智能的因果世界模型

**Date**: 2026-11-01 | **arXiv**: [2611.01677v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01677v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01677v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We present Xiaomi Auto World Model 2.0, a unified streaming causal world model for Physical AI. It models and generates causal physical evolution in the real world, and unifies heterogeneous conditioning inputs for view-consistent generation. The architecture integrates View-Temporal Attention (VTA) to capture cross-view spatial dependencies and temporal dynamics, together with Multi-view Rotary Position Encoding (RoPE) to encode positional relationships across views and time. WorldRec 2.0 reconstructs static and dynamic 3D Gaussians from multi-view, multi-temporal observations in a single forward pass and uses their renderings as explicit scene context to preserve geometry and appearance during long-horizon generation. Conditioning inputs are fused through VAE latent concatenation in a shared representation space. We incorporate Gated Linear Attention (GLA) into the temporal layers of VTA for efficient streaming inference, reducing computation and KV-cache memory during multi-view rollouts. A physical-interaction data pipeline covers diverse interactions and long-tailed events. From Pre-train onward, these data are combined with large-scale videos and heterogeneous visual modalities to learn visual, geometric, and dynamic priors. During Mid-train, training across diverse view configurations further improves the model's ability to capture how actions and object states shape subsequent scene evolution. After distillation-based Post-train, we apply the model to closed-loop simulation, closed-loop training, and World Action Model (WAM) applications. These applications generate physically consistent future states and support world-state prediction, policy learning, and action generation for Physical AI.

我们推出了小米汽车世界模型2.0，这是一个物理人工智能的统一流式因果世界模型。它建模并生成现实世界中的因果物理演化，并统一异构条件输入以实现视图一致的生成。该架构集成了视图时间注意力（VTA）来捕获跨视图空间依赖性和时间动态，以及多视图旋转位置编码（RoPE）来编码跨视图和时间的位置关系。 WorldRec 2.0 在一次前向传递中根据多视图、多时间观测重建静态和动态 3D 高斯，并使用它们的渲染作为显式场景上下文，以在长视野生成过程中保留几何结构和外观。条件输入通过共享表示空间中的 VAE 潜在串联进行融合。我们将门控线性注意 (GLA) 合并到 VTA 的时间层中，以实现高效的流式推理，减少多视图推出期间的计算和 KV 缓存。物理交互数据管道涵盖各种交互和长尾事件。从预训练开始，这些数据与大规模视频和异构视觉模式相结合，以学习视觉、几何和动态先验。在中间训练期间，跨不同视图配置的训练进一步提高了模型捕获动作和对象状态如何塑造后续场景演化的能力。经过基于蒸馏的 Post-train 之后，我们将模型应用于闭环仿真、闭环训练和世界动作模型（WAM）应用。这些应用程序生成物理上一致的未来状态，并支持物理人工智能的世界状态预测、策略学习和行动生成。

</details>

## 5. Stand-In Effects and Pooling Amplification: Design-Based Tests of Whether Biological World Models Use Actions / 替代效应和池化放大：基于设计的生物世界模型是否使用动作的测试

**Date**: 2026-11-01 | **arXiv**: [2611.01666v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01666v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01666v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, q-bio.QM, stat.ME

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Action-conditioned world models are increasingly trained on data from randomized experiments, and whether their predictions respond to actions is usually checked by replacing or removing the actions and measuring the loss in accuracy. We show that such diagnostics report action effects where there are none: under autocorrelated designs, future actions stand in for history the model does not see, and a better model learns the stand-in better. When the design law is known, a conditional randomization test written as an e-value is valid in finite samples whatever the model, provided the resampler conditions on the full state the design used; resampling from a simpler law reproduces the false effect. We identify a second failure, specific to world models: when many sites share one action sequence (cells in a microscope field, pixels in a frame), the natural pooled score turns small model errors along the action contrast into overwhelming evidence against a true effect. In a pixel world, its median log e-value fell from $-17.3$ to $-6832$ as frames grew from 64 to 4096 pixels, while the oracle's rose. Tempering the score by a design effect estimated on training data changes the bet, not the validity. Our own preregistered analysis of optogenetic ERK experiments failed in exactly this way; a tempered statistic, selected on synthetic data and registered before the data were obtained, then rejected the null on a never-opened field ($E\approx 1.1\times10^{10}$). Every analysis is preregistered, fixed in a dated protocol before it was run, or labelled exploratory, and every reported result is generated from hashed result files.

行动条件世界模型越来越多地根据随机实验的数据进行训练，并且通常通过替换或删除行动并测量准确性损失来检查它们的预测是否对行动做出反应。我们表明，这样的诊断报告了没有行动效果的地方：在自相关设计下，未来的行动代表模型看不到的历史，更好的模型可以更好地学习替代。当设计法则已知时，无论模型如何，以 e 值形式编写的条件随机化测试在有限样本中都是有效的，只要重采样器条件处于设计所使用的完整状态；从更简单的定律中重新采样会重现错误的效果。我们发现了第二个失败，特定于世界模型：当许多站点共享一个动作序列（显微镜视野中的细胞，帧中的像素）时，自然的汇总分数将沿着动作对比的小模型错误变成反对真实效果的压倒性证据。在像素世界中，随着帧从 64 像素增长到 4096 像素，其对数 e 值中值从 $-17.3$ 下降到 $-6832$，而预言机的值则上升。通过训练数据估计的设计效果来调整分数会改变赌注，而不是有效性。我们自己的光遗传学 ERK 实验预注册分析正是以这种方式失败了；一个经过调整的统计数据，在合成数据上选择并在获得数据之前注册，然后拒绝从未打开的字段上的空值（$E\approx 1.1\times10^{10}$）。每个分析都经过预先注册，在运行之前固定在过时的协议中，或者标记为探索性，并且每个报告的结果都是从散列结果文件生成的。

</details>

## 6. World Models Need Not Be Right to Rank Policies Right: Relative Bounds and Audit Limits / 世界模型不一定要正确地对政策进行排序：相对界限和审计限制

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.01139v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01139v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01139v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Selecting a reinforcement-learning (RL) policy usually requires additional environment interaction, while learned world models offer cheap but potentially biased evaluations. This raises two distinct questions: can shared model errors preserve pairwise rankings despite poor absolute values, and do they make a small number of real audits more informative? We derive an exact pairwise simulation identity and a relative bound separating common-mode Bellman residuals from policy-specific error. The analysis motivates RAVEL (Relative Auditing of Value Errors in Learned models), a selective policy tournament whose eliminations use held-out real outcomes. We test 45 SAC, TD3, and PPO policies per task in DMC cheetah-run and walker-walk. At rollout horizon 500, model rankings remain strong (Kendall tau = 0.962 and 0.800), and same-run differential-error ratios are 0.674 and 0.106. Yet mean paired audit-error correlations are only 0.007 and 0.090, and pairing changes standard deviation by -2.5 percent and +1.9 percent. Simultaneous intervals attain 99.98 percent and 99.75 percent coverage, but certify only 13.2 percent and 0 percent of decisions at eight audits. RAVEL improves low-budget walker-walk regret, whereas real-only successive halving is better at the largest budget, rejecting the precommitted twofold efficiency target. Frozen-model optimization lowers real return without increasing predicted return on average. Thus ranking fidelity, useful audit covariance, and upward model exploitation are distinct properties, separated in part by closed-loop trajectory divergence.

选择强化学习（RL）策略通常需要额外的环境交互，而学习世界模型提供廉价但可能存在偏见的评估。这就提出了两个不同的问题：尽管绝对值很差，但共享模型错误是否可以保留成对排名，以及它们是否可以使少量真实审计提供更多信息？我们推导出精确的成对模拟恒等式和相对界限，将共模贝尔曼残差与策略特定误差分开。该分析激发了 RAVEL（学习模型中价值错误的相对审核），这是一项选择性政策锦标赛，其淘汰赛使用保留的真实结果。我们在 DMC 猎豹跑和步行者行走中测试每个任务的 45 个 SAC、TD3 和 PPO 策略。在推出范围 500 时，模型排名仍然强劲（Kendall tau = 0.962 和 0.800），同一运行的差分误差率为 0.674 和 0.106。然而，平均配对审计错误相关性仅为 0.007 和 0.090，并且配对将标准差改变了 -2.5% 和 +1.9%。同步间隔达到 99.98% 和 99.75% 的覆盖率，但在 8 次审核中仅验证了 13.2% 和 0% 的决策。 RAVEL 改善了低预算步行者步行的遗憾，而仅实数连续减半在最大预算下效果更好，拒绝了预先承诺的双倍效率目标。冻结模型优化会降低实际回报，但不会增加平均预测回报。因此，排名保真度、有用的审计协方差和向上模型开发是不同的属性，部分由闭环轨迹分歧分开。

</details>

## 7. pico-WAM: Evaluating Counterfactual Prediction and Planning Error in a Compact World Action Model / pico-WAM：评估紧凑世界行动模型中的反事实预测和规划错误

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.01039v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01039v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01039v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

When a World Action Model fails at control, is the bottleneck proposal generation or trajectory scoring? We introduce pico-WAM, a compact model with supplied physical state for tracing policy-dynamics interactions. Using 206 Push-T demonstrations and 15 training seeds, we compare data regimes and search configurations. Counterfactual branch training improves short-horizon cost ranking and enlarges the coverage advantage of policy-restarted over warm-started search by 4.8 percentage points; restart wins under both data regimes. Replacing learned trajectory scores with simulator costs improves coverage by 12.2 points while retaining learned proposals. Substituting simulator states in proposal generation alone shows no detected gain. Component diagnostics and a separate door-opening study show why better ranking need not yield better control or outperform imitation. These controlled interventions identify scoring as an effective improvement target in the tested setting and provide a reproducible protocol for diagnosing compact world models.

当世界行动模型无法控制时，瓶颈是提案生成还是轨迹评分？我们引入 pico-WAM，这是一种紧凑模型，提供用于跟踪策略动态交互的物理状态。使用 206 个 Push-T 演示和 15 个训练种子，我们比较数据机制和搜索配置。反事实分支训练提高了短期成本排名，并将策略重启相对于热启动搜索的覆盖优势扩大了4.8个百分点；在两种数据制度下重新启动都会获胜。用模拟器成本替换学习的轨迹分数可将覆盖率提高 12.2 个点，同时保留学习的建议。仅在提案生成中替换模拟器状态未显示检测到的增益。组件诊断和一项单独的开门研究表明，为什么更好的排名不一定会带来更好的控制或超越模仿。这些受控干预措施将评分确定为测试环境中的有效改进目标，并为诊断紧凑世界模型提供了可重复的协议。

## 8. GURU: A Hierarchical Neuro-Symbolic Multi-Agent System for Verification-Aware Automation of Mission-Critical Digital Engineering and Operations / GURU：一种分层神经符号多代理系统，用于关键任务数字工程和运营的验证感知自动化

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.01001v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01001v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01001v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CE, cs.MA, eess.SY

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

An AI can find a safe action faster than any human, but it must never decide what counts as safe. We present GURU, a hierarchical neuro-symbolic multi-agent system for mission-critical digital engineering and operations, built on one partition of authority: learned models propose, rank, and accelerate; a symbolic rules engine checks every consequential action against hard limits before it executes; and validated physics confirms and decides. GURU runs a workflow as a closed control loop, with Navigation, Action, and Planning agents sharing one Common Internal Language and one training factory. Nuclear, aviation, and national-security guidance now requires this pattern; a harnessed language model keeps the model at the center, GURU puts a validated reference there. In computational fluid dynamics, the workflow raised the converged-simulation rate on unseen aircraft geometries from under 1 percent to 74 percent over 100 single-attempt cases, ran 54 live cases to Mach 6 on three facilities, and an ablation put the symbolic layer at 13 points of boundary-layer capture. Knowledge-graph retrieval and role adapters raised ParaView control success from 55 to 75 percent. In space domain awareness, where a language model cannot be trusted to decide, a frozen self-supervised world model under validated propagation scored highest of five methods at conjunction risk, including fully fine-tuned language models, with several-times-lower seed variance: the trustworthy design gives up nothing in accuracy, and the recipe transferred to reactor anomaly detection. A radar-to-avoidance debris pipeline was demonstrated in stages on a 2026 reentry; the hierarchy coordinates simulated teams of 200 aircraft. Training scaled near-linearly past 1,000 Aurora nodes and ran at 8,000. The approach extends to a control-grade microreactor digital twin with validated physics, not a learned model, as the safety basis.

人工智能可以比任何人类更快地找到安全的行动，但它永远不能决定什么才是安全的。我们提出了 GURU，这是一种用于关键任务数字工程和操作的分层神经符号多智能体系统，它建立在一个权限划分上：学习模型提出、排序和加速；符号规则引擎在执行之前检查每个后续操作是否符合硬限制；并经过验证的物理学确认并决定。 GURU 将工作流程作为闭环控制运行，导航、行动和规划代理共享一种通用内部语言和一个培训工厂。核、航空和国家安全指导现在需要这种模式；利用的语言模型将模型置于中心位置，GURU 在那里放置经过验证的参考。在计算流体动力学方面，该工作流程在 100 个单次尝试案例中将未见过的飞机几何形状的聚合模拟率从不到 1% 提高到 74%，在三个设施上以 6 马赫的速度运行了 54 个实际案例，并且消融将符号层置于边界层捕获的 13 个点处。知识图检索和角色适配器将 ParaView 控制成功率从 55% 提高到 75%。在空间领域意识中，语言模型不能被信任来做出决定，经过验证的传播下的冻结自我监督世界模型在联合风险的五种方法中得分最高，包括完全微调的语言模型，其种子方差要低几倍：值得信赖的设计在准确性上不会放弃任何东西，并且配方转移到反应堆异常检测。 2026 年再入时分阶段演示了雷达躲避碎片管道；该层次结构协调由 200 架飞机组成的模拟团队。训练规模近乎线性地超过 1,000 个 Aurora 节点，并以 8,000 个节点运行。该方法扩展到控制级微反应器数字孪生，以经过验证的物理原理（而不是学习模型）作为安全基础。

## 9. Why Koopman Structure Does Not Help Differentiable World Models for Humanoid Whole-Body Tracking / 为什么库夫曼结构无助于人形全身跟踪的微分世界模型

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.00951v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00951v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.00951v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We test whether Koopman structure, linear dynamics in a lifted state space, helps when a humanoid whole-body tracking policy is trained by backpropagating through a differentiable world model, replacing the MLP world model of an otherwise unchanged pipeline for simulated Unitree G1 tracking. It does not: return is at best equal (0.823 versus 0.830), one-step error on identical data is 1.3 to 1.6 times the MLP's, and training is about 15% slower. Lifted rollouts diverge or fail to train a policy; of the schemes tested, re-lifting at every imagined step trains best. Re-lifting never uses the lifted linear dynamics and leaves a model affine in the action, with state-dependent offset and slope; an action-affine MLP also predicts worse, so the cost belongs to this class. At a fixed state, the curvature of the true dynamics in the action explains only part of it. Across states, the policy's actions span a curved region, so an affine model fits policy data only as the tangent at the policy's action, the operating point: on G1, the MLP's tangent there is as accurate as the MLP; at the mean action, 31 times worse. The offset must encode, from the state alone, where the policy acts, whereas an MLP reads the action. Supplying the policy's action removes most of the remaining gap on G1, Ant and Humanoid. The only online repair approaching the MLP feeds the action into the nonlinear lift, giving up the structure. The MLP world model thus remains the better choice here.

我们测试了库普曼结构（提升状态空间中的线性动力学）在通过可微世界模型反向传播来训练人形全身跟踪策略时是否有帮助，以替换用于模拟 Unitree G1 跟踪的其他不变管道的 MLP 世界模型。但事实并非如此：回报最多是相等的（0.823 与 0.830），相同数据上的一步误差是 MLP 的 1.3 到 1.6 倍，并且训练速度大约慢 15%。提升的部署出现分歧或无法训练策略；在所测试的方案中，在每一个想象的步骤中重新举重训练效果最好。重新提升从不使用提升的线性动力学，并在动作中留下模型仿射，具有与状态相关的偏移和斜率；动作仿射 MLP 也会预测更差的结果，因此成本属于此类。在固定状态下，动作中真实动力学的曲率只能解释其中的一部分。在各州之间，政策的行动跨越了一个弯曲区域，因此仿射模型仅将政策数据拟合为政策行动处的切线（操作点）：在 G1 上，MLP 的切线与 MLP 一样准确；在 G1 上，MLP 的切线与 MLP 一样准确；在平均行动中，情况更糟 31 倍。偏移量必须仅从策略执行的状态进行编码，而 MLP 则读取操作。提供策略的操作消除了 G1、Ant 和 Humanoid 上剩余的大部分差距。唯一接近 MLP 的在线修复将动作输入非线性升力，放弃结构。因此，MLP 世界模型仍然是更好的选择。

## 10. Solvable dynamics model of in-context learning / 情境学习的可解动力学模型

**Date**: 2026-10-31 | **arXiv**: [2611.00921v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00921v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.00921v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cond-mat.dis-nn

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Pretrained Transformer architectures display a surprising in-context learning ability without additional training. How the learning process guides the network to achieve this intriguing capability remains unsolved. In this study, we focus on a single-layer, single-head linear attention model, where empirical risk minimization is defined for learning. Given a finite amount of training examples, we determine the exact solution of network parameter dynamics, which is linked to the training error and generalization error, characterizing how memorization and generalization emerge from learning and how these transitions are related to curvatures of the empirical risk landscape. Furthermore, we calculate a data-dependent free energy, from which the training and generalization errors in the long-time limit of learning dynamics can be well predicted. Our work adds insights toward understanding the mechanism of in-context learning, especially from a learning dynamics perspective.

预训练的 Transformer 架构无需额外训练即可展现令人惊讶的上下文学习能力。学习过程如何引导网络实现这种有趣的能力仍然悬而未决。在本研究中，我们关注单层、单头线性注意力模型，其中为学习定义了经验风险最小化。给定有限数量的训练样本，我们确定网络参数动态的精确解，该解与训练误差和泛化误差相关，描述了记忆和泛化如何从学习中产生，以及这些转变如何与经验风险景观的曲率相关。此外，我们计算了数据相关的自由能，从中可以很好地预测学习动态的长期限制中的训练和泛化误差。我们的工作为理解情境学习机制提供了见解，特别是从学习动态的角度来看。
