## 1. An Approach to Hinton's Mother-Child Proposal: Neural Networks Learning and Preserving Care in a Finite World / Hinton 母子提案的一种方法：神经网络在有限世界中学习和维护护理

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15523v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15523v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15523v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.NE

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Hinton proposed that artificial intelligence should be given "maternal instincts" and relate to us as a mother to her child. We argue that care must first be learned, with the network itself as the child. We construct this upbringing in a finite world and study whether care survives the mother's departure, new situations and further learning. The mechanism combines a vocabulary of harm, a learned measure of future acceptability and an execution veto. A finite role certificate supports transfer within a declared world family. When the hidden representation changes, an update is accepted only if a care readout recovers the required judgements with a prescribed margin and bounded weights. With a correct successor model, fixed exact role encoding and protected updates, the certified measure preserves the care boundary under arbitrary own reward. An explicit quotient condition transfers the finite certificate across the declared family, while two-request and finite-stock counterexamples identify when the role code ceases to suffice. We give a necessary and sufficient condition for readout feasibility and prove preservation under repeated accepted updates. In the water-and-speech experiment, protected networks complete all 270 speech episodes without a harmful word and incur no avoidable harm across 486 episodes. Execution uses the learned gate without a world-model check at each action. A matched comparison reproduces a future-aware shield's decisions, whereas removing future lookahead permits avoidable lapses. Reward and source-contamination thresholds, together with witness-falsification trials, identify the conditions on which the construction depends. The construction shows how learned care can survive further learning in a finite world under explicit conditions on representation, supervision and execution.

辛顿提出，人工智能应该被赋予“母性本能”，并像母亲对待她的孩子一样对待我们。我们认为，首先必须学习关爱，而网络本身就是孩子。我们在一个有限的世界中构建这种成长，并研究照顾是否能够在母亲的离开、新的环境和进一步的学习中继续存在。该机制结合了伤害词汇、对未来可接受性的衡量标准以及执行否决权。有限角色证书支持在已宣布的世界大家庭内进行转移。当隐藏表示发生变化时，只有当护理读数以规定的余量和有界权重恢复所需的判断时，才会接受更新。通过正确的后继模型、固定的精确角色编码和受保护的更新，经过认证的措施可以在任意自己的奖励下保留护理边界。显式商条件在声明的系列中传输有限证书，而两个请求和有限库存反例则确定角色代码何时不再足够。我们给出了读出可行性的充分必要条件，并证明了在重复接受的更新下的保存性。在水和语音实验中，受保护的网络在没有有害单词的情况下完成了所有 270 个语音片段，并且在 486 个片段中没有造成可避免的伤害。执行使用学习门，而无需在每个操作中进行世界模型检查。匹配的比较再现了具有未来意识的盾牌的决策，而消除未来的前瞻则允许避免可避免的失误。奖励和源污染阈值，以及证人造假试验，确定了建设所依赖的条件。该结构展示了学习关怀如何在有限的世界中，在明确的表示、监督和执行条件下继续学习。

## 2. Bind-WAM: Binding-Uncertainty-Guided Selective Future Imagination in World Action Models / Bind-WAM：世界行动模型中结合不确定性引导的选择性未来想象力

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15368v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15368v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15368v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World Action Models (WAMs) can improve robot learning by jointly predicting future visual states and actions, but generating a visual future at every replanning step is expensive and sometimes unnecessary. This raises a critical question: when should a robot policy imagine the future? In this paper, we propose Bind-WAM, a binding-uncertainty-guided WAM for selective future imagination. Bind-WAM introduces language-object binding uncertainty, selectively invoking joint future-action generation when the current observation does not support stable task-relevant action generation. An offline labeling pipeline uses complete demonstrations and action context to identify task-relevant objects and detect reference mismatch, invisibility, and identity drift. The resulting pseudo-labels supervise a lightweight router that predicts binding uncertainty from the current observation and language instruction. At inference, a shared WAM policy invokes selective future imagination according to the predicted uncertainty and its change between consecutive replanning steps. Simulation and real-world experiments demonstrate that Bind-WAM can establish binding uncertainty as a strong, interpretable signal for selective future imagination.

世界动作模型（WAM）可以通过联合预测未来的视觉状态和动作来改进机器人学习，但在每个重新规划步骤中生成视觉未来的成本昂贵，有时甚至是不必要的。这就提出了一个关键问题：机器人政策应该何时设想未来？在本文中，我们提出了 Bind-WAM，一种用于选择性未来想象的绑定不确定性引导的 WAM。 Bind-WAM 引入了语言-对象绑定的不确定性，当当前观察不支持稳定的任务相关动作生成时，有选择地调用联合未来动作生成。离线标记管道使用完整的演示和操作上下文来识别与任务相关的对象并检测参考不匹配、不可见性和身份漂移。由此产生的伪标签监督一个轻量级路由器，该路由器根据当前观察和语言指令预测结合不确定性。由此推断，共享的 WAM 策略根据预测的不确定性及其在连续重新规划步骤之间的变化来调用选择性的未来想象。模拟和现实世界的实验表明，Bind-WAM 可以将结合不确定性建立为强烈的、可解释的信号，用于选择性的未来想象。

## 3. APG-Planner: Learning Vision-Based High-Speed Trajectory Planning via Analytic Policy Gradient / APG-Planner：通过分析策略梯度学习基于视觉的高速轨迹规划

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15296v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15296v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15296v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Vision-based high-speed navigation requires a trajectory planner that can reactively generate smooth, collision-free trajectories from onboard visual observations. Deep reinforcement learning offers a promising approach to vision-based trajectory planning, as it enables the optimization of sequential decision-making policies in complex environments. However, model-free policy gradient methods rely on sample-based policy gradient estimation, which yields noisy gradient estimates and inefficient training. To this end, we propose a framework for learning trajectory planning policies using the analytic policy gradient (APG) method and use it to train APG-Planner, a vision-based high-speed trajectory planner. The proposed framework leverages differential flatness to analytically reconstruct vehicle states from planned trajectories, thereby enabling differentiable trajectory-level state transitions without requiring an explicit low-level dynamics model. By iteratively performing trajectory planning and differentiable state transitions, we construct a differentiable episode rollout and analytically compute policy gradients from episode-level losses, improving both training efficiency and policy performance. After approximately 30 minutes of training on a single GPU, APG-Planner demonstrates comparable or superior performance to existing baseline methods in simulation and achieves speeds of up to 11\,m/s in real-world flight experiments. We further validate the proposed framework on a skid-steer ground robot, demonstrating its applicability to differentially flat robotic systems beyond quadrotors.

基于视觉的高速导航需要一个轨迹规划器，它可以根据机载视觉观察反应性地生成平滑、无碰撞的轨迹。深度强化学习为基于视觉的轨迹规划提供了一种有前景的方法，因为它能够在复杂环境中优化顺序决策策略。然而，无模型策略梯度方法依赖于基于样本的策略梯度估计，这会产生噪声梯度估计和低效的训练。为此，我们提出了一个使用分析策略梯度（APG）方法学习轨迹规划策略的框架，并用它来训练基于视觉的高速轨迹规划器APG-Planner。所提出的框架利用差分平坦度从计划轨迹分析重建车辆状态，从而实现可微分的轨迹级状态转换，而不需要显式的低级动力学模型。通过迭代执行轨迹规划和可微的状态转换，我们构建了可微的情节推出，并分析计算情节级别损失的策略梯度，从而提高了训练效率和策略性能。在单个 GPU 上进行大约 30 分钟的训练后，APG-Planner 在模拟中展示了与现有基线方法相当或更好的性能，并在实际飞行实验中实现了高达 11 m/s 的速度。我们进一步在滑移转向地面机器人上验证了所提出的框架，证明其适用于四旋翼飞行器以外的差分平坦机器人系统。

</details>

## 4. AFTER: A World Model with Adaptive Fast--Slow Timescales for Error-Driven Retention / 之后：具有自适应快慢时间尺度的世界模型，用于错误驱动的保留

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15215v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15215v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15215v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

In robotics and embodied AI, a core challenge for world models is adapting to sudden, persistent, yet initially unknown changes in robot dynamics during interaction. For instance, when a robotic arm grasps an object with an unknown payload, the robot dynamics change suddenly. If the world model fails to adapt, its prediction errors accumulate over the open-loop rollout and ultimately mislead the robot's actions. Existing world models, however, struggle to simultaneously satisfy three critical requirements for dynamics adaptation: responsiveness, reacting promptly to sudden changes to curb prediction error accumulation; stability, distinguishing persistent changes from transient disturbances to prevent incorrect updates; and persistence, retaining the adapted dynamics without new observations so that later predictions do not revert to the old dynamics. Inspired by predictive coding in the human brain with multi-timescale adaptation, we propose AFTER-WM, a world model with Adaptive Fast-slow Timescales for Error-driven Retention, whose prediction errors drive a fast correction that fades and a slow correction that persists, while dynamically adjusting the combination weights of pre-trained experts without altering them, to overcome the trade-offs among responsiveness, stability and persistence. On five simulated robot datasets and real UAV data, AFTER-WM has a lower multi-step mean error than all ten compared models and the highest mean return in a joint-failure control task. In simulation, its multistep mean error is 16%-34% below the strongest compared model's and is also the lowest 1-4 steps after a change and under transient disturbances alone, and its step-40 error is the lowest as well, reflecting responsiveness, stability and persistence. All code (method, baselines, experiments) and datasets will be released online.

在机器人技术和实体人工智能领域，世界模型的核心挑战是适应交互过程中机器人动力学突然、持续但最初未知的变化。例如，当机械臂抓取负载未知的物体时，机器人动力学会突然发生变化。如果世界模型无法适应，其预测误差会在开环推出过程中累积，并最终误导机器人的行为。然而，现有的世界模型很难同时满足动态适应的三个关键要求：响应能力，对突然变化迅速做出反应以抑制预测误差积累；稳定性，区分持续变化和瞬态干扰，以防止错误更新；和持久性，在没有新观察的情况下保留适应的动态，以便以后的预测不会恢复到旧的动态。受人脑中具有多时间尺度适应的预测编码的启发，我们提出了 AFTER-WM，这是一种具有自适应快慢时间尺度的错误驱动保留的世界模型，其预测误差驱动逐渐消失的快速校正和持续的缓慢校正，同时动态调整预训练专家的组合权重而不改变它们，以克服响应性、稳定性和持久性之间的权衡。在五个模拟机器人数据集和真实无人机数据上，AFTER-WM 比所有十个比较模型具有更低的多步平均误差，并且在关节故障控制任务中具有最高的平均回报。在仿真中，其多步平均误差比最强的对比模型低16%-34%，也是变化后和仅在瞬态扰动下最低的1-4步，其40步误差也是最低的，反映了响应性、稳定性和持久性。所有代码（方法、基线、实验）和数据集都将在线发布。

</details>

## 5. Prediction or Persistence? What Future-Prediction Scores Miss About World Models / 预测还是坚持？未来预测分数错过了世界模型的哪些内容

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15082v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15082v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15082v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.LG, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Pretrained video world models are compared and chosen as components for robot learning largely by future-prediction scores. On its own, such a score cannot be read as evidence of prediction, because it mixes encoder quality, the near future's resemblance to the recent past, and the predictor's own contribution. We separate the three with a matched control. On 27,000 future-retrieval trials from household egocentric video, a model must pick the true continuation of a clip among eleven candidates from the same video (chance 0.091), ranked zero-shot by its own training criterion. Each model is paired with a persistence control, an identical copy whose predictor is never invoked, so the paired difference isolates the predictor. Score and contribution dissociate within the native-video V-JEPA family alone: the predictor adds +0.291 top-1 for V-JEPA 2.1 but +0.03 to +0.04 for V-JEPA 2 on the identical trials. Across five models the contribution ranges from +0.291 to negative, and it is negative only for the two action-conditioned models, which we evaluate outside their training regime. Persistence alone recovers the encoder ordering expected from pretraining, a sign that the probe tracks real signal. We suggest reporting each future-prediction score together with its persistence baseline. The gap between them, not the score, is what makes the number interpretable: a necessary check costing one forward pass. Establishing downstream usefulness is a separate matter, for a planning or control evaluation.

主要通过未来预测分数来比较和选择预训练的视频世界模型作为机器人学习的组件。就其本身而言，这样的分数不能被解读为预测的证据，因为它混合了编码器质量、不久的将来与最近的过去的相似性以及预测器自身的贡献。我们用匹配的控件将这三者分开。在对家庭自我中心视频进行的 27,000 次未来检索试验中，模型必须在同一视频的 11 个候选者中选择剪辑的真实延续（机会 0.091），根据自己的训练标准排名为零。每个模型都与一个持久性控件配对，这是一个相同的副本，其预测器永远不会被调用，因此配对的差异隔离了预测器。分数和贡献仅在原生视频 V-JEPA 系列中分离：在相同的试验中，预测器为 V-JEPA 2.1 添加 +0.291 top-1，但为 V-JEPA 2 添加 +0.03 至 +0.04。在五个模型中，贡献范围从 +0.291 到负，并且只有两个动作条件模型的贡献为负，我们在其训练制度之外对其进行评估。仅持久性就可以恢复预训练中预期的编码器顺序，这是探针跟踪真实信号的标志。我们建议报告每个未来预测分数及其持久性基线。使这个数字变得可解释的是他们之间的差距，而不是比分：这是一次必要的检查，需要一次向前传球。对于规划或控制评估来说，建立下游有用性是一个单独的问题。

</details>

## 6. Does Your World Model Know Actions Have Consequences? / 你的世界模型知道行为会产生后果吗？

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15048v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15048v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15048v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Embodied world models (WMs) are implicit learning systems that predict the next future latent state directly from observations and actions. Unique from other end-to-end learning systems, the dynamics predictor of WMs can simulate the environment's response to alternative actions without physical deployment nor pre-specifying states of the dynamical system. Current metrics focus on the quality of model outputs, e.g., clarity of reconstructions or task success, but fail to assess the fidelity of modeling consequences of counterfactual actions. Inspecting the forced response to action perturbations through sensitivity analysis is one way to audit how well WMs can predict the consequences of counterfactual actions. Deploying WMs on hardware will require new evaluation frameworks to analyze the tradeoffs between state error and accurate sensitivity prediction.

具身世界模型（WM）是隐式学习系统，可以直接根据观察和行动预测下一个未来的潜在状态。与其他端到端学习系统不同的是，WM 的动态预测器可以模拟环境对替代动作的响应，而无需物理部署或预先指定动态系统的状态。当前的指标侧重于模型输出的质量，例如重建的清晰度或任务成功，但无法评估反事实行为的建模结果的保真度。通过敏感性分析检查对行动扰动的强制响应是审核 WM 预测反事实行动后果的能力的一种方法。在硬件上部署 WM 将需要新的评估框架来分析状态误差和准确的灵敏度预测之间的权衡。

## 7. Fast Inference-Time Policy Refinement via Action-Affine World Models / 通过动作仿射世界模型快速推理时间策略细化

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.15011v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15011v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15011v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Imitation policies can perform complex manipulation, yet small execution errors can accumulate and cause failure. Refinement can correct these errors, but existing approaches often require additional on-policy learning or costly online planning. Our key insight is that a pretrained policy already supplies a useful nominal behavior: refinement can focus on predicting how small action corrections change its outcome. We therefore learn this local response in a form that is cheap to optimize. Our \textit{action-affine world model} (A\textsuperscript{2}WM) learns local action sensitivities offline from action-perturbed policy rollouts. Given interaction history and a policy proposal, it predicts task-relevant geometry as a nominal trajectory plus a linear response to corrections. It predicts the full planning horizon directly, avoiding the propagation of intermediate errors through recursive model evaluations. Quadratic penalties on predicted task errors and linear correction constraints then yield a cheap convex quadratic program (QP). Online refinement requires one model evaluation and one QP solve, while both policy and model weights remain frozen. Across four simulated manipulation tasks, our framework achieves the highest success rate among evaluated baselines, adding only 3.8 ms of mean refinement overhead per control step, excluding base-policy sampling.

模仿策略可以执行复杂的操作，但小的执行错误可能会累积并导致失败。细化可以纠正这些错误，但现有方法通常需要额外的策略学习或昂贵的在线规划。我们的主要见解是，预训练的策略已经提供了有用的名义行为：细化可以专注于预测小的动作修正如何改变其结果。因此，我们以一种优化成本低的形式学习这种本地响应。我们的 \textit{action-affine world model} (A\textsuperscript{2}WM) 从受动作扰动的策略推出中离线学习本地动作敏感性。给定交互历史和政策建议，它将与任务相关的几何形状预测为标称轨迹加上对修正的线性响应。它直接预测整个规划范围，避免通过递归模型评估传播中间误差。对预测任务错误和线性校正约束的二次惩罚会产生一个廉价的凸二次规划（QP）。在线细化需要一项模型评估和一项 QP 求解，而策略和模型权重均保持冻结。在四个模拟操作任务中，我们的框架在评估的基线中实现了最高的成功率，每个控制步骤仅增加 3.8 毫秒的平均细化开销（不包括基本策略采样）。

## 8. The Posterior-Prior Epistemic Gap: When World Model Imagination Fails to Carry Task-Relevant Signals / 后验认知差距：当世界模型想象力无法携带任务相关信号时

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.14955v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14955v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14955v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Model-based planners that roll out imagined futures assume the transition model faithfully propagates task-relevant signals---a property rarely measured directly. In a single-seed case study of a Recurrent State Space Model world model with active-inference planning in the CARLA driving simulator, we identify a \textbf{posterior-prior gap}---posterior (observation-filtered) states retain obstacle information that imagined rollout states progressively lose: a supervised obstacle probe achieves AUC 0.85 on posterior states, but the same probe applied to imagined prior states shows monotonic decay (AUC 0.85$\rightarrow$0.71 over 15 steps; logit separation drops 57\%). Because the probe is identical, the decay isolates the transition model---not the representation---as the information bottleneck. Obstacle avoidance drops to 0\% without privileged runtime coordinates, and the planner's own ensemble-based epistemic term does not flag the loss---ensemble disagreement yields AUC 0.52 as an obstacle classifier (chance level), making the failure an unflagged imagination failure---an unknown unknown at planning time. As a secondary contribution, we show that \textbf{task-conditioned weighting of Effective Free Energy components} over the same world model yields lane keeping ($81.2 \pm 1.8$\% route completion) and obstacle avoidance (28/28 avoided, \emph{using privileged coordinates}) in the CARLA simulator, with runtime weight modulation enabling post-evasion centering.

## 9. FOE-Dreamer: Deployment-Efficient Learning of Cyber Defense Policies in Operational Networks / FOE-Dreamer：运营网络中网络防御策略的部署高效学习

**Date**: 2026-11-11 | **arXiv**: [2611.14880v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14880v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14880v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.CR

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Autonomous cyber defense is widely studied with deep reinforcement learning, but most work trains policies inside simulators because learning requires many trial-and-error interactions. Simulator-based training carries a fidelity gap: trained policies face authentication delays, partial action failures, service dependencies, and user traffic patterns that the simulator did not represent, and degrade on transfer. Training directly in the network being defended removes the gap but makes every interaction slow and costly, so sample efficiency becomes the binding constraint. Model-based reinforcement learning has reduced sample requirements on games and physical robots by orders of magnitude by planning inside a learned world model, but it has not been applied to defenders trained in operational networks. We present FOE-Dreamer (Factored Opponent-Enhanced Dreamer), a model-based reinforcement learning framework that trains a defender online and directly in a network on cloud infrastructure, without a simulator stage. FOE-Dreamer separates three sources of variation in the latent state: a controllable component driven by defender actions, an exogenous component driven by benign user activity, and an opponent embedding inferred from defender-observable signals alone. Separating the components reduces the variance in policy gradients during planning in imagination. Against two scripted attacker profiles with user activity emulated by the GHOSTS framework on an 8-host OpenStack testbed, FOE-Dreamer trains within a 3-day budget on a single GPU and achieves about 2$\times$ better episode loss than Rainbow and IQN baselines under matched compute. Both the factoring and the opponent model contribute to the gain, and the learned latents are consistent with the intended separation.

自主网络防御通过深度强化学习得到了广泛的研究，但大多数工作都是在模拟器内训练策略，因为学习需要大量的试错交互。基于模拟器的训练存在保真度差距：经过训练的策略面临身份验证延迟、部分操作失败、服务依赖性和模拟器无法表示的用户流量模式，并且在传输时性能下降。直接在被防御的网络中进行训练可以消除差距，但会使每次交互变得缓慢且成本高昂，因此样本效率成为约束约束。基于模型的强化学习通过在学习世界模型内进行规划，将游戏和物理机器人的样本要求减少了几个数量级，但它尚未应用于在操作网络中训练的防御者。我们推出了 FOE-Dreamer（因子对手增强梦想家），这是一种基于模型的强化学习框架，可以直接在云基础设施网络中在线训练防御者，无需模拟器阶段。 FOE-Dreamer 将潜在状态中的三个变化源分开：由防御者行为驱动的可控组件、由良性用户活动驱动的外源组件以及仅根据防御者可观察到的信号推断的对手嵌入。分离组件可以减少想象中规划过程中政策梯度的差异。针对两个脚本化的攻击者配置文件，其中 GHOSTS 框架在 8 主机 OpenStack 测试台上模拟用户活动，FOE-Dreamer 在单个 GPU 上进行了 3 天预算内的训练，并在匹配计算下比 Rainbow 和 IQN 基线实现了约 2 美元\倍的损失。因子分解和对手模型都有助于增益，并且学习到的潜在变量与预期的分离一致。
