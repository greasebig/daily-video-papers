## 1. VGGA: Geometry-Grounded Autoencoder for Video Generation / VGGA：用于视频生成的基于几何的自动编码器

**Date**: 2026-10-12 | **arXiv**: [2610.16204v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16204v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.16204v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Autoencoders are the core component of latent video generative models, compressing high-dimensional videos into compact latent spaces for downstream generation. However, existing video autoencoders are primarily optimized for pixel-level reconstruction, providing limited supervision for learning structured latent representations. We argue that videos should not be treated merely as signals to be reconstructed, but rather as 2D projections of dynamic 3D worlds that exhibit explicit and well-structured geometric properties. Building on this insight, we propose \textbf{V}ideo \textbf{G}eometry \textbf{G}rounded \textbf{A}utoencoder (\textbf{VGGA}), a geometry-aware video autoencoder that explicitly incorporates geometric constraints into the reconstruction process. Specifically, VGGA grounds the learned representations along three complementary geometric dimensions: \emph{Structural Depth}, \emph{Motion Correspondence}, and \emph{Projective Warping}. These objectives are unified within a Video Geometry Decoder, which employs a shared spatio-temporal upsampling trunk to jointly optimize geometric learning and RGB reconstruction. Through these geometry-grounded objectives, VGGA encourages the latent representations to capture the underlying 3D structure and motion of dynamic scenes. VGGA consistently improves video generation across both geometry-centric and human-motion domains, reducing gFVD by 28.1\% on RealEstate10K and 37.7\% on Taichi-HD. Notably, these substantial generation improvements are achieved while maintaining comparable reconstruction quality, demonstrating that geometry-grounded representation learning yields a more effective latent space for video generation.

自动编码器是潜在视频生成模型的核心组件，将高维视频压缩成紧凑的潜在空间以供下游生成。然而，现有的视频自动编码器主要针对像素级重建进行优化，为学习结构化潜在表示提供有限的监督。我们认为，视频不应仅仅被视为要重建的信号，而应被视为动态 3D 世界的 2D 投影，展现出明确且结构良好的几何属性。基于这一见解，我们提出 \textbf{V}ideo \textbf{G}eometry \textbf{G}rounded \textbf{A}utoencoder (\textbf{VGGA})，这是一种几何感知视频自动编码器，它明确地将几何约束纳入重建过程。具体来说，VGGA 将学习到的表示建立在三个互补的几何维度上：\emph{结构深度}、\emph{运动对应}和 \emph{投影扭曲}。这些目标在视频几何解码器中统一，该解码器采用共享的时空上采样主干来联合优化几何学习和 RGB 重建。通过这些基于几何的目标，VGGA 鼓励潜在表示捕获动态场景的底层 3D 结构和运动。 VGGA 持续改进了以几何为中心和人体运动领域的视频生成，在 RealEstate10K 上将 gFVD 降低了 28.1%，在 Taichi-HD 上降低了 37.7%。值得注意的是，这些实质性的生成改进是在保持可比较的重建质量的同时实现的，这表明基于几何的表示学习为视频生成提供了更有效的潜在空间。

## 2. EventLens: Event-Structure Reinforcement Learning for Video Understanding / EventLens：用于视频理解的事件结构强化学习

**Date**: 2026-10-12 | **arXiv**: [2610.16172v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16172v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.16172v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.MM

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent multimodal large language models (MLLMs) achieve strong results on video question answering, yet often fail to recover the temporal structure that makes a video coherent. They may merge neighboring events, miss single-event progression, or infer implausible relations across events. We study structured temporal understanding as the ability to recover event structure from visual evidence, including event boundaries, single-event progression, and multi-event relations. We argue that this gap arises from a mismatch between current post-training objectives and temporal reasoning, where caption-style supervision allows models to rely on language shortcuts rather than visual temporal grounding. To address this, we propose EventLens, a vision-centric reinforcement learning framework that learns video understanding through event-structure recovery. Instead of reconstructing captions, EventLens trains models to recover temporal structure under controlled perturbations, such as altered temporal granularity, reversed progression, and shuffled event order. We instantiate this framework with a three-level temporal ontology and derive three verifiable task families: event segmentation, progression discrimination, and multi-event relation reasoning. These tasks admit deterministic rewards, enabling task-specialized GRPO training without human preference labels or learned reward models. We further introduce multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MT-OPD) to consolidate specialized policies into a unified model. Experiments on Qwen3-VL backbones show consistent improvements on temporal grounding and event-sensitive reasoning benchmarks, while preserving general video-QA performance. Learning curves further demonstrate that EventLens achieves comparable downstream transfer with significantly reduced training cost compared to direct mixed RL optimization.

## 3. Interactive 4D Volumetric Liquid Forecasting under Moving-Solid Interaction / 移动-固体相互作用下的交互式 4D 体积液体预测

**Date**: 2026-10-12 | **arXiv**: [2610.16167v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16167v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.16167v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CE, cs.LG

**Code**: https://github.com/jongwonryu/TIDE-TIDES.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We propose the task of Interactive 4D Volumetric Liquid Forecasting: predicting the spatiotemporal evolution of a future dense 3D liquid velocity field conditioned on how a solid object moves through it. This task provides an upstream physical forecasting primitive for systems that need to reason about motion-conditioned liquid response, including robotic liquid manipulation, digital twins, and rigid-fluid video generation. Existing works have made efforts in predicting flow around static or prescribed solid geometries and synthesizing visually plausible fluid motion. Yet these approaches provide limited coverage for interactive liquid dynamics, as they are not capable of forecasting how a moving solid induces a future full-domain liquid response. In this forecasting scenario, the hydrodynamic effect is condensed near an evolving liquid-solid interface, but the prediction target is a global spatiotemporal rollout. To address this challenge, we propose a two-level recurrent forecasting framework, TIDE/TIDES: TIDE isolates localized interface-driven forecasting, while TIDES adds transport-aware coupling and explicit stabilization to reduce accumulated rollout error. Across adapted neighboring baselines, controlled component ablations, motion/size OOD tests, and long-horizon evaluation, TIDES achieves the best forecasting performance and the ablations identify localized interaction, transport coupling, and stabilization as the key contributors. Together, our task formulation, benchmark, and TIDE/TIDES study establish a controlled foundation for moving-solid-conditioned dense liquid forecasting. The code is publicly available at https://github.com/jongwonryu/TIDE-TIDES.

我们提出了交互式 4D 体积液体预测的任务：预测未来密集 3D 液体速度场的时空演化，以固体物体如何穿过它为条件。该任务为需要推理运动条件液体响应的系统提供了上游物理预测原语，包括机器人液体操纵、数字孪生和刚性流体视频生成。现有的工作致力于预测静态或规定的固体几何形状周围的流动，并合成视觉上合理的流体运动。然而，这些方法对交互式液体动力学的覆盖范围有限，因为它们无法预测移动的固体如何引起未来的全域液体响应。在这种预测场景中，流体动力学效应集中在不断变化的液-固界面附近，但预测目标是全球时空推广。为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了一个两级循环预测框架，TIDE/TIDES：TIDE 隔离本地化接口驱动的预测，而 TIDES 增加了传输感知耦合和显式稳定以减少累积的推出误差。通过调整邻近基线、受控组件消融、运动/尺寸 OOD 测试和长视野评估，TIDES 实现了最佳预测性能，并且消融将局部相互作用、传输耦合和稳定性确定为关键贡献者。我们的任务制定、基准测试和潮汐/潮汐研究共同为移动固体条件稠密液体预报奠定了受控基础。该代码可在 https://github.com/jongwonryu/TIDE-TIDES 上公开获取。

## 4. Elephant in the Fridge: Constant-Memory Frame Packing for Long Video Understanding / 冰箱里的大象：用于长视频理解的恒定内存帧封装

**Date**: 2026-10-12 | **arXiv**: [2610.16118v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16118v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.16118v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.LG, cs.MM

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Hour-long videos, streaming feeds, and movie-length content are pushing vision-language models (VLMs) past their breaking point -- the standard recipe of encoding each frame into visual tokens yields sequences that grow linearly with video length, causing prohibitive memory consumption and inference latency. Existing approaches, such as token reduction and keyframe selection, shorten the token sequence but leave the O(n) scaling intact, failing to resolve the fundamental bottleneck. We propose FramePack-U, a strikingly simple, training-free frame packing framework that compresses arbitrarily long videos into a fixed size visual representation at constant memory cost. FramePack-U requires no fine-tuning or architectural modification -- it plugs directly into off-the-shelf VLMs at inference time. Inspired by FramePack from the generation literature, we propose a content-aware compression strategy for visual understanding under a fixed token budget. Specifically, FramePack-U (1) scores each frame by its semantic similarity to the text prompt and ranks frames in a diversity-driven manner, and (2) packs the selected frames at varying resolutions into a fixed-length context window. This concentrates the memory budget on frames that are both instruction-relevant and visually diverse. While FramePack-U can in theory pack arbitrarily many frames into the fixed budget, we identify an empirical instantiation that generalizes robustly across settings. Extensive experiments on LongVideoBench, MLVU, and Video-MME show that FramePack-U consistently outperforms full-resolution baselines and prior token reduction and keyframe selection methods across diverse model families, parameter scales, and frame budgets. Notably, on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B with a 128-frame budget, FramePack-U yields gains of +12.9\%, +10.6\%, and +9.7\% over the official model on the three benchmarks, respectively.

长达一小时的视频、流媒体和电影长度的内容正在推动视觉语言模型 (VLM) 突破极限——将每帧编码为视觉标记的标准方法会产生随视频长度线性增长的序列，从而导致过高的内存消耗和推理延迟。现有的方法，例如令牌缩减和关键帧选择，缩短了令牌序列，但保持 O(n) 缩放不变，未能解决根本瓶颈。我们提出了 FramePack-U，这是一种非常简单、无需训练的帧打包框架，可以以恒定的内存成本将任意长的视频压缩为固定大小的视觉表示。 FramePack-U 不需要微调或架构修改——它在推理时直接插入现成的 VLM。受到生成文献中 FramePack 的启发，我们提出了一种内容感知压缩策略，用于在固定令牌预算下进行视觉理解。具体来说，FramePack-U (1) 根据每个帧与文本提示的语义相似性对每个帧进行评分，并以多样性驱动的方式对帧进行排名，(2) 将不同分辨率的选定帧打包到固定长度的上下文窗口中。这将内存预算集中在与指令相关且视觉上多样化的帧上。虽然 FramePack-U 理论上可以将任意多的帧打包到固定预算中，但我们确定了一个可以在设置中稳健推广的经验实例。 LongVideoBench、MLVU 和 Video-MME 上的大量实验表明，FramePack-U 在不同的模型系列、参数规模和帧预算中始终优于全分辨率基线以及先前的标记缩减和关键帧选择方法。值得注意的是，在具有 128 帧预算的 Qwen3.5-35B-A3B 上，FramePack-U 在三个基准测试中分别比官方模型获得 +12.9\%、+10.6\% 和 +9.7\% 的增益。

## 5. PIGEONS: Incremental Long-Video Captioning with Persistent Identity Gallery and Evolving Overarching Narrative Summary / 鸽子：增量长视频字幕与持久身份库和不断发展的总体叙事摘要

**Date**: 2026-10-12 | **arXiv**: [2610.16066v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16066v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.16066v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Detailed video captioning has become an essential semantic interface for multi-modal understanding and generation. While existing methods mainly target short videos, extending them to long videos remains challenging, primarily due to limited context windows of current video understanding models. A naive solution is to adopt segment-wise captioning followed by concatenation, yet this paradigm often suffers from inconsistent subject references and fragmented long-range narratives. To mitigate these issues, we introduce PIGEONS, an incremental long-video captioning model built upon a Persistent Identity Gallery and an Evolving Overarching Narrative Summary. Before caption generation, PIGEONS scans the entire video to construct an identity gallery that captures key subjects along with their visual references, thereby providing a stable referential anchor. The model then performs clip-by-clip captioning conditioned on the persistent gallery and an evolving overarching narrative summary, which compactly maintains and updates essential long-range contextual information after each captioning step. To enable these capabilities, we develop a three-stage training strategy that integrates supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning. Extensive experiments demonstrate that PIGEONS markedly improves long-video captioning performance, achieving superior accuracy, completeness, and subject referential consistency over existing methods, while maintaining competitive performance on short-video captioning.

详细的视频字幕已成为多模态理解和生成的重要语义接口。虽然现有方法主要针对短视频，但将其扩展到长视频仍然具有挑战性，这主要是由于当前视频理解模型的上下文窗口有限。一个幼稚的解决方案是采用分段字幕，然后串联，但这种范例经常遭受不一致的主题引用和支离破碎的长距离叙述的困扰。为了缓解这些问题，我们引入了 PIGEONS，这是一种基于持久身份库和不断发展的总体叙事摘要构建的增量长视频字幕模型。在生成字幕之前，PIGEONS 会扫描整个视频以构建一个身份图库，捕获关键主题及其视觉参考，从而提供稳定的参考锚点。然后，该模型根据持久图库和不断发展的总体叙述摘要执行逐个剪辑的字幕，在每个字幕步骤后紧凑地维护和更新基本的远程上下文信息。为了实现这些功能，我们开发了一个集成了监督微调和强化学习的三阶段训练策略。大量实验表明，PIGEONS 显着提高了长视频字幕性能，与现有方法相比，实现了卓越的准确性、完整性和主题参考一致性，同时保持了短视频字幕的竞争性能。

## 6. Meaningful Moments: A Corpus of VLM-Derived Temporal Importance Labels for Video Action Recognition / 有意义的时刻：用于视频动作识别的 VLM 派生时间重要性标签语料库

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Not every moment in a video is equally useful for recognizing an action. A few decisive moments often carry the evidence: the instant an object moves, the peak of a dive, or one object placed on another. Existing video datasets rarely provide large-scale, action-conditioned labels for which short segments carry that evidence. This work asks whether vision-language models can identify meaningful moments at scale and whether those labels improve action recognition.We introduce Meaningful Moments (MM), a corpus of about 4.58 million VLM-generated per-segment importance scores for nearly 500,000 class-labeled videos spanning Something-Something v2 (SSv2), Kinetics-400 (K400), and Diving-48 across 622 action classes. We freeze the action classifier and vary only temporal sampling. An alpha-parameterized comparison of importance-led selection against an importance-inverted control separates gains from the VLM signal from gains caused by variable-density sampling alone. Across all three datasets, VLM-derived importance is informative: at the hard-cut endpoint, keeping VLM-important segments outperforms keeping VLM-designated filler. Usefulness depends on class structure. On Diving-48, importance-led fast-forwarding preserves full-video recognition while the inverted control collapses by roughly 30 percentage points. On K400, hard-cut selection beats uniform sampling by 1.45 percentage points, rising to 2.05 under threshold selection. On SSv2, the labels carry signal but do not recover full-video performance. Direct scoring matches greedy removal within its noise floor at roughly 25 times fewer oracle calls, and a four-VLM cross-oracle study shows consistent downstream trends. Existing approaches seek to prevent such failures through improved memory and temporal modeling, but once a failure occurs, the tracker has no explicit mechanism to recognize or correct it. We address this gap with Dual-Agent, a failure-aware tracking framework that complements propagation with explicit verification and recovery.
